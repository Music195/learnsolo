Sample Paper: Mathematics Course 2

A sample paper in the style of EJU Mathematics Course 2 (original problems, not an official past paper). Four longer problems, each in parts. Try each one first. Open the hint if you are stuck, then compare with the full solution.

Problem 1

$f(x) = x^2 - 4x + 3$. Let $m(a)$ be the minimum of $f(x)$ for $a \le x \le a + 1$.

  1. (1) Find $m(0)$.
  2. (2) Find all $a$ with $m(a) = 0$.
  3. (3) Find the smallest value of $m(a)$ as $a$ varies. (Type this one in the box.)

Uses: Maximum and Minimum of y = a(x − p)² + q

Show hint

$f(x) = (x - 2)^2 - 1$. Compare the axis $x = 2$ with the range $a \le x \le a + 1$: to the right of it, inside it, or to the left of it.

Show solution
  1. $f(x) = (x - 2)^2 - 1$, axis $x = 2$.
  2. (1) $a = 0$: the range $0 \le x \le 1$ is left of the axis, so $f$ decreases and $m(0) = f(1) = 0$.
  3. (2) $a + 1 \le 2$ ($a \le 1$): $m(a) = f(a + 1) = a^2 - 2a$. $a^2 - 2a = 0$ gives $a = 0$ or $2$; only $a = 0$ satisfies $a \le 1$.
  4. $1 < a < 2$: the axis is inside the range, so $m(a) = -1 \ne 0$.
  5. $a \ge 2$: $m(a) = f(a) = a^2 - 4a + 3 = 0$ gives $a = 1$ or $3$; only $a = 3$ satisfies $a \ge 2$.
  6. (3) Every $m(a)$ is a value of $f$, so $m(a) \ge -1$, and $m(a) = -1$ when $1 \le a \le 2$.

(1) $m(0) = 0$ (2) $a = 0, 3$ (3) $-1$, for $1 \le a \le 2$

Problem 2

A bag holds 4 red balls and 3 white balls. Three balls are taken out at the same time.

  1. (1) Find the probability that exactly 2 of them are red.
  2. (2) Find the expected number of red balls. (Type this one in the box.)

Uses: Combination Count, Expectation

Show hint

There are ${}_7\mathrm{C}_3 = 35$ equally likely choices. For (2), find the probability of 0, 1, 2 and 3 red balls first.

Show solution
  1. (1) ${}_4\mathrm{C}_2 \cdot {}_3\mathrm{C}_1 = 18$ choices, so the probability is $\dfrac{18}{35}$.
  2. (2) 0 red: $\dfrac{1}{35}$; 1 red: $\dfrac{4 \cdot 3}{35} = \dfrac{12}{35}$; 2 red: $\dfrac{18}{35}$; 3 red: $\dfrac{4}{35}$ (they add up to $1$ ✓).
  3. Expected value $= \dfrac{0 \cdot 1 + 1 \cdot 12 + 2 \cdot 18 + 3 \cdot 4}{35} = \dfrac{60}{35} = \dfrac{12}{7}$.

(1) $\dfrac{18}{35}$ (2) $\dfrac{12}{7}$

Shortcut: each of the 3 balls is red with probability $\dfrac{4}{7}$, so the expected number is $3 \cdot \dfrac{4}{7} = \dfrac{12}{7}$.

Problem 3

Consider the curve $C: y = \log x$.

  1. (1) Find the tangent line to $C$ that passes through the origin.
  2. (2) Find the area of the region bounded by $C$, this tangent line and the $x$-axis. (Type this one in the box.)

Uses: Equation of the Tangent Line, Area Bounded by y = f(x)

Show hint

Write the tangent at $(t, \log t)$ and make it pass through $(0, 0)$. For (2), draw it: a triangle minus the area under $C$.

Show solution
  1. (1) The tangent at $(t, \log t)$ is $y = \dfrac{1}{t}(x - t) + \log t$.
  2. Through $(0, 0)$: $0 = -1 + \log t$, so $t = e$ and the tangent is $y = \dfrac{x}{e}$, touching at $(e, 1)$.
  3. (2) Under the tangent from $x = 0$ to $e$ is a triangle of area $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot e \cdot 1 = \dfrac{e}{2}$.
  4. Under $C$ from $x = 1$ to $e$: $\displaystyle\int_1^e \log x\,dx = \Big[x\log x - x\Big]_1^e = 0 - (-1) = 1$.
  5. The region is the triangle minus that part: $\dfrac{e}{2} - 1$.

(1) $y = \dfrac{x}{e}$ (2) $\dfrac{e}{2} - 1$

Problem 4

The sequence $\{a_n\}$ has $a_1 = 1$ and $a_{n+1} = 2a_n + 1$.

  1. (1) Find the general term $a_n$.
  2. (2) Find $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{10} a_k$. (Type this one in the box.)

Uses: Geometric Sequence General Term, Geometric Sequence Sum

Show hint

Add $1$ to both sides: $a_{n+1} + 1 = 2(a_n + 1)$. So $\{a_n + 1\}$ is a geometric sequence.

Show solution
  1. (1) $a_{n+1} + 1 = 2(a_n + 1)$ and $a_1 + 1 = 2$, so $a_n + 1 = 2 \cdot 2^{n-1} = 2^n$ and $a_n = 2^n - 1$.
  2. (2) $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{10} (2^k - 1) = \dfrac{2(2^{10} - 1)}{2 - 1} - 10 = 2046 - 10 = 2036$.

(1) $a_n = 2^n - 1$ (2) $2036$
