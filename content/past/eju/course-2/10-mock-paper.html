Problem 1 I Q1 · Parabola tangent to a line

Let $a \ne 0$. The parabola $y = a(x + 1)(x - 3)$ touches the line $y = 2x - 7$.

  1. (1) Write the condition for touching as an equation in $a$.
  2. (2) Find the two values of $a$. (Type the smaller one in the box.)
  3. (3) For each value, find the point of contact.

Uses: Quadratic Equation: Discriminant and Real Solutions, General Forms of Linear and Quadratic Functions

Show hint

$a(x + 1)(x - 3) = 2x - 7$ must have a double root: $ax^2 - (2a + 2)x - 3a + 7 = 0$ with discriminant $0$.

Show solution
  1. (1) $D = (2a + 2)^2 - 4a(7 - 3a) = 16a^2 - 20a + 4 = 4(4a - 1)(a - 1) = 0$.
  2. (2) $a = \dfrac{1}{4}$ or $a = 1$.
  3. (3) The double root is $x = \dfrac{2a + 2}{2a}$. $a = 1$: $x = 2$, point $(2, -3)$. $a = \dfrac{1}{4}$: $x = 5$, point $(5, 3)$.

(1) $(4a - 1)(a - 1) = 0$ (2) $a = \dfrac{1}{4}, 1$ (3) $(5, 3)$ and $(2, -3)$

Problem 2 I Q2 · Repeated trials

Two dice are rolled; if the sum is $10$ or more, the player wins that round. Five rounds are played.

  1. (1) Find the probability of winning one round.
  2. (2) Find the probability of winning no rounds.
  3. (3) Find the probability of winning at least two rounds. (Type this one in the box.)

Uses: Probability in Repeated Trials (Binomial), Probability of the Complement Event

Show hint

Sums $10, 11, 12$ come from $3 + 2 + 1 = 6$ outcomes. Use the complement of “0 or 1 wins”.

Show solution
  1. (1) $\dfrac{6}{36} = \dfrac{1}{6}$.
  2. (2) $\left(\dfrac{5}{6}\right)^5 = \dfrac{3125}{7776}$.
  3. (3) Exactly one win: $5 \cdot \dfrac{1}{6}\left(\dfrac{5}{6}\right)^4 = \dfrac{3125}{7776}$.
  4. $1 - \dfrac{6250}{7776} = \dfrac{1526}{7776} = \dfrac{763}{3888}$.

(1) $\dfrac{1}{6}$ (2) $\dfrac{3125}{7776}$ (3) $\dfrac{763}{3888}$

Problem 3 II Q1 · Sums with powers of 2

Let $T_n = \displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n} k \cdot 2^{k-1} = 1 + 2 \cdot 2 + 3 \cdot 2^2 + \dots + n \cdot 2^{n-1}$.

  1. (1) Compute $T_n - 2T_n$ and simplify it using the sum of a geometric sequence.
  2. (2) Find $T_n$ in terms of $n$.
  3. (3) Find $T_{10}$. (Type this one in the box.)

Uses: Sum of Geometric Sequence, Properties of Sigma (Σ)

Show hint

Write $2T_n$ under $T_n$, shifted by one place, and subtract.

Show solution
  1. (1) $T_n - 2T_n = 1 + 2 + 2^2 + \dots + 2^{n-1} - n \cdot 2^n = 2^n - 1 - n \cdot 2^n$.
  2. (2) $-T_n = (1 - n)2^n - 1$, so $T_n = (n - 1)2^n + 1$.
  3. (3) $T_{10} = 9 \cdot 1024 + 1 = 9217$.

(2) $T_n = (n - 1)2^n + 1$ (3) $9217$

Problem 4 II Q2 · Vectors and lines

In the coordinate plane, let $O$ be the origin, $A(1, 2)$ and $B(4, -2)$.

  1. (1) Find $|\overrightarrow{AB}|$ and the area of triangle $OAB$.
  2. (2) Let $H$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $O$ to line $AB$. Writing $\overrightarrow{OH} = \overrightarrow{OA} + t\overrightarrow{AB}$, find $t$ and $H$.
  3. (3) Find the length $OH$. (Type this one in the box.)

Uses: Vector AB and Its Magnitude, Triangle Area with Vectors, Vector Equation of a Straight Line, Vector Perpendicularity

Show hint

$\overrightarrow{AB} = (3, -4)$. Use $\overrightarrow{OH} \cdot \overrightarrow{AB} = 0$. You can check $OH$ with the area: area $= \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot AB \cdot OH$.

Show solution
  1. (1) $\overrightarrow{AB} = (3, -4)$, $|\overrightarrow{AB}| = 5$. Area $= \dfrac{1}{2}|1 \cdot (-2) - 2 \cdot 4| = 5$.
  2. (2) $\overrightarrow{OH} = (1 + 3t, 2 - 4t)$, and $(1 + 3t) \cdot 3 + (2 - 4t) \cdot (-4) = 25t - 5 = 0$: $t = \dfrac{1}{5}$, $H\left(\dfrac{8}{5}, \dfrac{6}{5}\right)$.
  3. (3) $OH = \sqrt{\dfrac{64 + 36}{25}} = 2$. (Check: $\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 5 \cdot 2 = 5$.)

(1) $5$, area $5$ (2) $t = \dfrac{1}{5}$, $H\left(\dfrac{8}{5}, \dfrac{6}{5}\right)$ (3) $2$

Problem 5 III · Rational functions

Consider $f(x) = \dfrac{x^2 + 1}{x - 1}$ for $x \ne 1$.

  1. (1) Write $f(x) = x + p + \dfrac{q}{x - 1}$, and find the asymptotes of $y = f(x)$.
  2. (2) Find $f'(x)$ and the values of $x$ where $f'(x) = 0$.
  3. (3) Find the local minimum value of $f(x)$. (Type this one in the box.)

Uses: Quotient Rule for Derivatives, Necessary Condition for Local Extrema, Graph of a Rational Function

Show hint

$x^2 + 1 = (x - 1)(x + 1) + 2$. The local minimum is on the branch $x > 1$.

Show solution
  1. (1) $f(x) = x + 1 + \dfrac{2}{x - 1}$: asymptotes $x = 1$ and $y = x + 1$.
  2. (2) $f'(x) = 1 - \dfrac{2}{(x - 1)^2} = 0 \iff (x - 1)^2 = 2 \iff x = 1 \pm \sqrt{2}$.
  3. (3) $f'$ changes from $-$ to $+$ at $x = 1 + \sqrt{2}$: local minimum $f(1 + \sqrt{2}) = 2 + \sqrt{2} + \dfrac{2}{\sqrt{2}} = 2 + 2\sqrt{2}$.
  4. (The local maximum is $f(1 - \sqrt{2}) = 2 - 2\sqrt{2}$.)

(1) $x = 1$, $y = x + 1$ (2) $x = 1 \pm \sqrt{2}$ (3) $2 + 2\sqrt{2}$

Problem 6 IV · Riemann sums

Find the following limits by writing each sum as a definite integral.

  1. (1) $\displaystyle\lim_{n \to \infty} \dfrac{1}{n}\sum_{k=1}^{n} \left(\dfrac{k}{n}\right)^2$.
  2. (2) $\displaystyle\lim_{n \to \infty} \dfrac{1}{n}\sum_{k=1}^{n} e^{k/n}$.
  3. (3) $\displaystyle\lim_{n \to \infty} \sum_{k=1}^{n} \dfrac{n}{n^2 + k^2}$. (Type this one in the box.)

Uses: Riemann Sum and Definite Integral, Definite Integral: Substitution Method

Show hint

$\displaystyle\lim_{n \to \infty} \dfrac{1}{n}\sum_{k=1}^{n} f\left(\dfrac{k}{n}\right) = \int_0^1 f(x)\,dx$. In (3), divide top and bottom by $n^2$; for $\displaystyle\int_0^1 \dfrac{dx}{1 + x^2}$ substitute $x = \tan\theta$.

Show solution
  1. (1) $\displaystyle\int_0^1 x^2\,dx = \dfrac{1}{3}$.
  2. (2) $\displaystyle\int_0^1 e^x\,dx = e - 1$.
  3. (3) $\dfrac{n}{n^2 + k^2} = \dfrac{1}{n} \cdot \dfrac{1}{1 + (k/n)^2}$, so the limit is $\displaystyle\int_0^1 \dfrac{dx}{1 + x^2}$.
  4. With $x = \tan\theta$, $dx = \dfrac{d\theta}{\cos^2\theta}$: $\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/4} d\theta = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$.

(1) $\dfrac{1}{3}$ (2) $e - 1$ (3) $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$
