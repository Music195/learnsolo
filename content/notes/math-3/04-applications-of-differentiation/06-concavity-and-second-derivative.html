Sign of the Second Derivative and Concavity

Is the slope getting steeper or flatter?

If $f''(x) > 0$, the slope $f'$ is increasing. The curve bends upward like a bowl: concave up (下に凸). Every tangent lies below the curve.

If $f''(x) < 0$, the slope is decreasing. The curve bends downward like a cap: concave down (上に凸). Every tangent lies above the curve.

On an interval

$$f''(x) > 0 \Rightarrow \text{concave up (下に凸)}, \qquad f''(x) < 0 \Rightarrow \text{concave down (上に凸)}$$

Concave up: the chord between two points lies above the curve; the tangent lies below. Concave down: the other way round.

  1. Let $f'' > 0$ and compare $f$ with its tangent at $a$: $g(x) = f(x) - \{f(a) + f'(a)(x - a)\}$.
  2. $g'(x) = f'(x) - f'(a)$. Since $f'' > 0$, $f'$ is increasing, so $g' < 0$ for $x < a$ and $g' > 0$ for $x > a$.
  3. So $g$ has its minimum $g(a) = 0$ at $a$: $g(x) \ge 0$, meaning the curve is above the tangent.

Concave up parts in blue, concave down parts in orange

Example 1

Find where $f(x) = x^3 - 3x^2 + 2$ is concave up and concave down.

Show solution
  1. $f'(x) = 3x^2 - 6x$, $\;f''(x) = 6x - 6 = 6(x - 1)$.
  2. $f'' < 0$ for $x < 1$ and $f'' > 0$ for $x > 1$.

Concave down for $x < 1$, concave up for $x > 1$

Example 2

Find the concavity of $f(x) = \log x$ and of $f(x) = e^{-x}$.

Show solution
  1. $(\log x)'' = -\dfrac{1}{x^2} < 0$ for all $x > 0$.
  2. $(e^{-x})'' = e^{-x} > 0$ for all $x$.

$\log x$ is concave down everywhere; $e^{-x}$ is concave up everywhere

Example 3

Show that $\sin x \ge \dfrac{2}{\pi}x$ for $0 \le x \le \dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

Show solution
  1. $(\sin x)'' = -\sin x \le 0$ on this interval, so $y = \sin x$ is concave down there.
  2. For a concave-down curve, the chord lies below the curve. The chord from $(0, 0)$ to $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}, 1\right)$ is $y = \dfrac{2}{\pi}x$.

$\sin x \ge \dfrac{2}{\pi}x$ on $\left[0, \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right]$ ✓

Concavity says nothing about up or down movement. $e^{-x}$ is decreasing but concave up. The sign of $f'$ gives increasing/decreasing; the sign of $f''$ gives the bending.
Japanese textbooks say 下に凸 ("bulging downward") for $f'' > 0$, the bowl shape. Picture where the bulge points: down for a bowl.
  • $f'' > 0$: concave up (下に凸), tangents below the curve.
  • $f'' < 0$: concave down (上に凸), tangents above the curve.
  • Concavity gives chord/tangent inequalities.
