Approximation Formula

Near the touching point, the tangent is almost the curve

Close to $x = a$, the curve and its tangent line are hard to tell apart. So for a small step $h$, use the tangent's height instead of the true value: $f(a + h) \approx f(a) + f'(a)h$.

The error is roughly proportional to $h^2$. Halve the step and the error drops to about a quarter.

When $|h|$ (or $|x|$) is small

$$f(a + h) \approx f(a) + f'(a)h, \qquad f(x) \approx f(0) + f'(0)x, \qquad \Delta y \approx y'\,\Delta x$$

Near $x = 0$: $(1 + x)^p \approx 1 + px$, $\;\sin x \approx x$, $\;\tan x \approx x$, $\;e^x \approx 1 + x$, $\;\log(1 + x) \approx x$ ($x$ in radians).

  1. $f'(a) = \displaystyle\lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(a + h) - f(a)}{h}$, so for small $h$: $\dfrac{f(a + h) - f(a)}{h} \approx f'(a)$.
  2. Multiply by $h$: $f(a + h) - f(a) \approx f'(a)h$.
  3. With $a = 0$, $h = x$: e.g. $f(x) = (1 + x)^p$ gives $f(0) = 1$, $f'(0) = p$, so $(1 + x)^p \approx 1 + px$.

Example 1

Estimate $\sqrt{9.1}$.

Show solution
  1. $f(x) = \sqrt{x}$, $a = 9$, $h = 0.1$: $f(9) = 3$, $f'(9) = \dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{9}} = \dfrac{1}{6}$.
  2. $\sqrt{9.1} \approx 3 + \dfrac{0.1}{6}$

$\approx 3.017$ (true value $3.0166\ldots$)

Example 2

Estimate $\sin 31°$ (use $\sqrt{3} \approx 1.732$, $\pi \approx 3.14$).

Show solution
  1. Work in radians: $31° = \dfrac{\pi}{6} + \dfrac{\pi}{180}$, with $h = \dfrac{\pi}{180}$.
  2. $\sin 31° \approx \sin\dfrac{\pi}{6} + \cos\dfrac{\pi}{6} \cdot \dfrac{\pi}{180} = 0.5 + 0.866 \times 0.01744$.

$\approx 0.515$ (true value $0.5150\ldots$)

Example 3

A sphere's radius grows from $10$ cm to $10.2$ cm. Estimate the increase in volume.

Show solution
  1. $V = \dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3$, $V' = 4\pi r^2$.
  2. $\Delta V \approx V'(10)\,\Delta r = 400\pi \times 0.2$

$\approx 80\pi \approx 251$ cm³

Example 4

A cube's side is measured with a relative error of $1\%$. About what relative error does that give in its volume?

Show solution
  1. $V = x^3$, $\Delta V \approx 3x^2\,\Delta x$, so $\dfrac{\Delta V}{V} \approx 3\dfrac{\Delta x}{x}$.

About $3\%$

The approximation is only good for small $h$. Choose $a$ so that $f(a)$ and $f'(a)$ are known exactly and $a + h$ is close by. For trig functions, $h$ must be in radians: $1° = \dfrac{\pi}{180}$.
$(1 + x)^p \approx 1 + px$ covers roots and reciprocals: $\sqrt{1 + x} \approx 1 + \dfrac{x}{2}$, $\;\dfrac{1}{1 + x} \approx 1 - x$. Rewrite the number in the form $(1 + \text{small})^p$ first, e.g. $\sqrt{4.08} = 2\sqrt{1.02} \approx 2.02$.
  • $f(a + h) \approx f(a) + f'(a)h$: the tangent line used as an estimate.
  • Near $0$: $(1 + x)^p \approx 1 + px$, $\sin x \approx x$, $e^x \approx 1 + x$, $\log(1 + x) \approx x$.
  • Small changes: $\Delta y \approx y'\Delta x$.
