Volume of Solid of Revolution About x-axis

Every slice is a disk

Spin the region under $y = f(x)$ around the $x$-axis. Each perpendicular slice of the solid is a disk of radius $|f(x)|$, so its area is $S(x) = \pi\{f(x)\}^2$.

If the region lies between two curves, each slice is a washer (a disk with a hole). Its area is $\pi$(outer radius)² − $\pi$(inner radius)².

Rotating about the $x$-axis, $a \le x \le b$

$$V = \pi\int_a^b \{f(x)\}^2\,dx = \pi\int_a^b y^2\,dx, \qquad V = \pi\int_a^b \left[\{f(x)\}^2 - \{g(x)\}^2\right]dx \;\; (f \ge g \ge 0)$$
  1. The point $(x, f(x))$ moves round a circle of radius $|f(x)|$ about the axis. So the slice at $x$ is a disk with $S(x) = \pi f(x)^2$.
  2. By note 18, $V = \displaystyle\int_a^b S(x)\,dx = \pi\int_a^b f(x)^2\,dx$.
  3. Between two curves, the slice is the outer disk minus the inner one: $S(x) = \pi(f^2 - g^2)$.

The solid approximated by $n$ thin cylinders

Example 1

The region under $y = \sqrt{x}$, $0 \le x \le 4$, is rotated about the $x$-axis. Find the volume.

Show solution
  1. $V = \pi\displaystyle\int_0^4 x\,dx = \pi\Big[\frac{x^2}{2}\Big]_0^4$

$8\pi$

Example 2

The region under $y = \sin x$, $0 \le x \le \pi$, is rotated about the $x$-axis. Find the volume.

Show solution
  1. $V = \pi\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi} \sin^2 x\,dx = \pi\int_0^{\pi} \frac{1 - \cos 2x}{2}\,dx = \pi \cdot \frac{\pi}{2}$

$\dfrac{\pi^2}{2}$

Example 3

The region between $y = x$ and $y = x^2$ is rotated about the $x$-axis. Find the volume.

Show solution
  1. They meet at $x = 0, 1$; on $[0, 1]$, $x \ge x^2 \ge 0$. Washers with outer radius $x$, inner radius $x^2$.
  2. $V = \pi\displaystyle\int_0^1 (x^2 - x^4)\,dx = \pi\left(\frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{5}\right)$

$\dfrac{2\pi}{15}$

Example 4

The ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2} + \dfrac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$ is rotated about the $x$-axis. Find the volume.

Show solution
  1. $y^2 = b^2\left(1 - \dfrac{x^2}{a^2}\right)$
  2. $V = \pi\displaystyle\int_{-a}^{a} b^2\left(1 - \frac{x^2}{a^2}\right)dx = 2\pi b^2\left(a - \frac{a}{3}\right)$

$\dfrac{4}{3}\pi ab^2$ (a sphere when $a = b$)

For a washer the area is $\pi(f^2 - g^2)$, not $\pi(f - g)^2$. Subtract the two disk areas. Also, if the region crosses the axis, the parts above and below overlap when spun. Use the larger radius on each slice.
Only $y^2$ is needed. For curves like a circle or an ellipse, solve the equation for $y^2$ instead of $y$; it avoids square roots.
  • $V = \pi\int_a^b y^2\,dx$.
  • Washers: $V = \pi\int_a^b (f^2 - g^2)\,dx$.
  • $\sin^2$ and $\cos^2$: lower the power before integrating.
