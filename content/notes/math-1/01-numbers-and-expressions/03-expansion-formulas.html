Expansion Formulas

Shortcuts for brackets that come up all the time

Every formula here is just the distributive law (note 02) done once and remembered. Spotting the pattern saves writing out every product and avoids sign slips.

Squares and difference of squares

$$(a + b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2, \qquad (a - b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2, \qquad (a + b)(a - b) = a^2 - b^2$$

Two linear brackets

$$(x + a)(x + b) = x^2 + (a + b)x + ab, \qquad (ax + b)(cx + d) = acx^2 + (ad + bc)x + bd$$

Cubes

$$(a \pm b)^3 = a^3 \pm 3a^2b + 3ab^2 \pm b^3, \qquad (a \pm b)(a^2 \mp ab + b^2) = a^3 \pm b^3$$

Take the top signs together or the bottom signs together.

  1. $(a + b)^2 = (a + b)(a + b) = a^2 + ab + ba + b^2$.
  2. $ab = ba$, so the middle terms combine: $a^2 + 2ab + b^2$.
  3. $(a + b)^3 = (a + b)(a^2 + 2ab + b^2)$; expanding and collecting gives $a^3 + 3a^2b + 3ab^2 + b^3$.

Example 1

Expand $(3x - 2y)^2$.

Show solution
  1. $(3x)^2 - 2 \cdot 3x \cdot 2y + (2y)^2$

$9x^2 - 12xy + 4y^2$

Example 2

Expand $(2x + 3)(3x - 1)$.

Show solution
  1. $ac = 6$, $\;ad + bc = 2(-1) + 3 \cdot 3 = 7$, $\;bd = -3$.

$6x^2 + 7x - 3$

Example 3

Expand $(a + b + c)^2$.

Show solution
  1. Treat $a + b$ as one block $M$: $(M + c)^2 = M^2 + 2Mc + c^2$.
  2. $= a^2 + 2ab + b^2 + 2ac + 2bc + c^2$

$a^2 + b^2 + c^2 + 2ab + 2bc + 2ca$

Example 4

Expand $(x - 2)^3$ and $(x + 1)(x^2 - x + 1)$.

Show solution
  1. $(x - 2)^3 = x^3 - 3 \cdot x^2 \cdot 2 + 3 \cdot x \cdot 4 - 8$
  2. $(x + 1)(x^2 - x + 1)$ is the sum-of-cubes pattern with $b = 1$.

$x^3 - 6x^2 + 12x - 8$; $\;x^3 + 1$

$(a + b)^2 \ne a^2 + b^2$. Don't forget the middle term $2ab$; the picture above shows exactly where it comes from.
For long expressions, replace a repeated chunk with one letter, e.g. $(x^2 + x + 1)(x^2 + x - 3)$: let $M = x^2 + x$ and expand $(M + 1)(M - 3)$.
  • $(a \pm b)^2 = a^2 \pm 2ab + b^2$ and $(a + b)(a - b) = a^2 - b^2$.
  • $(x + a)(x + b)$: the middle coefficient is the sum and the constant is the product.
  • Cubes: coefficients $1, 3, 3, 1$.
