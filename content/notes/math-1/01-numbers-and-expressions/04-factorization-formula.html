Factorization Formula

Expansion run backwards

Factorising turns a sum into a product. Read each expansion formula from right to left. Always take out any common factor first, then look for a pattern.

Patterns

$$a^2 \pm 2ab + b^2 = (a \pm b)^2, \qquad a^2 - b^2 = (a + b)(a - b)$$
$$x^2 + (a + b)x + ab = (x + a)(x + b), \qquad a^3 \pm b^3 = (a \pm b)(a^2 \mp ab + b^2)$$

Cross method for $px^2 + qx + r$ (たすき掛け)

$$acx^2 + (ad + bc)x + bd = (ax + b)(cx + d)$$

Choose $a, c$ with $ac = p$ and $b, d$ with $bd = r$ so that the cross sum $ad + bc = q$.

Factorise $px^2 + qx + r$ (whole numbers)

Example 1

Factorise $x^2 - 2x - 15$.

Show solution
  1. Two numbers with product $-15$ and sum $-2$: $3$ and $-5$.

$(x + 3)(x - 5)$

Example 2

Factorise $3x^2 + 5x - 2$.

Show solution
  1. $3 = 3 \times 1$, $\;-2 = (-1) \times 2$. Cross: $3 \cdot 2 + 1 \cdot (-1) = 5$ ✓

$(3x - 1)(x + 2)$

Example 3

Factorise $2x^3 - 18x$.

Show solution
  1. Common factor first: $2x(x^2 - 9)$.
  2. Difference of squares: $x^2 - 9 = (x + 3)(x - 3)$.

$2x(x + 3)(x - 3)$

Example 4

Factorise $x^2 + xy - 2y^2 + 2x + 7y - 3$.

Show solution
  1. Arrange in $x$: $x^2 + (y + 2)x - (2y^2 - 7y + 3)$.
  2. $2y^2 - 7y + 3 = (2y - 1)(y - 3)$, so the constant term is $-(2y - 1)(y - 3)$.
  3. Find two terms with product $-(2y - 1)(y - 3)$ and sum $y + 2$: $(2y - 1)$ and $-(y - 3)$.

$(x + 2y - 1)(x - y + 3)$

Example 5

Factorise $8x^3 - 27$.

Show solution
  1. $(2x)^3 - 3^3 = (2x - 3)\big((2x)^2 + 2x \cdot 3 + 3^2\big)$

$(2x - 3)(4x^2 + 6x + 9)$

With several letters, arrange by the letter of lowest degree (or by one letter, as in Example 4) and treat the others as numbers.
Factorise completely: $x^4 - 1 = (x^2 + 1)(x^2 - 1)$ is not finished, because $x^2 - 1 = (x + 1)(x - 1)$.
  • Common factor first, then a pattern.
  • $px^2 + qx + r$: cross method, checking that the cross sum equals $q$.
  • Check by expanding your answer.
