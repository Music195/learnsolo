Triangle Incircle Area Formula

Three slices, all with height $r$

The incircle touches all three sides, so its centre $I$ is the same distance $r$ from each side. Joining $I$ to the corners cuts the triangle into three triangles with bases $a$, $b$, $c$ and the same height $r$. Add them up and you get the area.

Area and inradius

$$S = \frac{1}{2}r(a + b + c)$$

$r$: radius of the incircle. Rearranged: $r = \dfrac{2S}{a + b + c}$.

  1. Let $I$ be the incentre. Its distance to each of $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ is $r$.
  2. $\triangle IBC = \dfrac{1}{2}ar$, $\;\triangle ICA = \dfrac{1}{2}br$, $\;\triangle IAB = \dfrac{1}{2}cr$.
  3. These three fill $\triangle ABC$ exactly, so $S = \dfrac{1}{2}r(a + b + c)$.

Example 1

A right triangle has sides 6, 8, 10. Find the inradius $r$.

Show solution
  1. $S = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 6 \cdot 8 = 24$
  2. $24 = \dfrac{1}{2}r(6 + 8 + 10) = 12r$

$r = 2$

Example 2

$\triangle ABC$ has $a = 7$, $b = 8$, $c = 5$. Find the inradius $r$.

Show solution
  1. $\cos A = \dfrac{64 + 25 - 49}{2 \cdot 8 \cdot 5} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, so $A = 60°$.
  2. $S = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 8 \cdot 5 \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = 10\sqrt{3}$ (note 08)
  3. $10\sqrt{3} = \dfrac{1}{2}r(7 + 8 + 5) = 10r$

$r = \sqrt{3}$

Example 3

A triangle with sides 13, 14, 15 has area 84. Find the area of its incircle.

Show solution
  1. $84 = \dfrac{1}{2}r(13 + 14 + 15) = 21r$, so $r = 4$.
  2. Incircle area $= \pi r^2$.

$16\pi$

The two tangent lengths from one corner are equal. With $s = \dfrac{a + b + c}{2}$, the corner $A$ is $s - a$ from both touch points next to it (and $B$ is $s - b$, $C$ is $s - c$).
$r$ is the inradius (circle inside, touching the sides). The law of sines uses the circumradius $R$ (circle through the corners).
  • $S = \dfrac{1}{2}r(a + b + c)$.
  • To find $r$: get $S$ first (note 08 or 10), then $r = \dfrac{2S}{a + b + c}$.
  • Proof: split at the incentre into three triangles of height $r$.
