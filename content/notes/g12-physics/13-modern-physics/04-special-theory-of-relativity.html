Special Theory of Relativity

Concept — Space-Time Invariance and Relativistic Transformations

In 1905, Albert Einstein published the Special Theory of Relativity, revolutionizing classical Newtonian concepts of absolute space and universal time:

  • The Michelson-Morley Experiment (1887): Searched for the hypothesized "luminiferous ether" through which light was thought to propagate. The null result demonstrated that the speed of light is completely independent of the Earth's orbital velocity, disproving the ether hypothesis.
  • Einstein's Two Postulates of Special Relativity:
    1. Principle of Relativity: The laws of physics are identical in all inertial (non-accelerating) reference frames. There is no preferred or absolute inertial frame.
    2. Constancy of the Speed of Light: The speed of light in vacuum is an absolute universal constant ($c = 3.0 \times 10^8\mathrm{\,m/s}$), identical for all observers regardless of the relative motion of the emitter or detector.
  • The Lorentz Relativistic Factor ($\gamma$): $$\gamma = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - \frac{v^2}{c^2}}} \ge 1$$
    • For ordinary non-relativistic velocities ($v \ll c$), $\frac{v^2}{c^2} \to 0$, so $\gamma \approx 1$, recovering classical Newtonian mechanics.
    • As relative velocity approaches the speed of light ($v \to c$), $\gamma \to \infty$.
  • Kinematic Relativistic Effects:
    • Time Dilation: Clocks in relative motion run slower when observed by a stationary observer: $$\Delta t = \gamma \Delta t_0 = \frac{\Delta t_0}{\sqrt{1 - \frac{v^2}{c^2}}}$$ where $\Delta t_0$ is the proper time (elapsed time measured in the reference frame where the clock remains stationary at the same spatial coordinates). Direct experimental evidence is provided by high-speed atmospheric cosmic muons ($v \approx 0.995c$) surviving to reach ground level.
    • Length Contraction: An object in motion appears physically contracted along its axis of motion: $$L = \frac{L_0}{\gamma} = L_0 \sqrt{1 - \frac{v^2}{c^2}}$$ where $L_0$ is the proper length (measured in the object's own rest frame). Transverse dimensions perpendicular to motion remain unchanged.
  • Relativistic Dynamics and Mass-Energy Equivalence:
    • Relativistic Momentum: $p = \gamma m_0 v$. As $v \to c$, momentum and inertial resistance to acceleration approach infinity, preventing any massive particle from ever reaching or exceeding $c$.
    • Mass-Energy Equivalence ($E = m c^2$): $$\text{Rest Energy: } E_0 = m_0 c^2 \qquad\qquad \text{Total Energy: } E = \gamma m_0 c^2 = E_0 + K$$ $$\text{Relativistic Kinetic Energy: } K = (\gamma - 1) m_0 c^2$$ In matter-antimatter annihilation (e.g., $e^- + e^+ \to 2\gamma$), $100\%$ of the rest mass is converted into electromagnetic photon energy ($1.022\mathrm{\,MeV}$).
Core Formulas — Lorentz Transformations and Relativistic Relations
$$\gamma = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - \frac{v^2}{c^2}}} \qquad\qquad \Delta t = \gamma \Delta t_0 \qquad\qquad L = \frac{L_0}{\gamma} \qquad\qquad E = \gamma m_0 c^2 = m_0 c^2 + K$$
  • $\gamma$ = Lorentz relativistic dilation factor ($\gamma \ge 1$)
  • $v$ = relative speed between reference frames ($\mathrm{m/s}$)
  • $c = 3.0 \times 10^8\mathrm{\,m/s}$ = universal speed of light in vacuum
  • $\Delta t_0, L_0$ = proper time interval and proper length measured in rest frames
  • $\Delta t, L$ = dilated time interval and contracted length observed in moving frame
  • $m_0$ = invariant rest mass ($\mathrm{kg}$)
  • $E_0 = m_0 c^2$ = rest energy ($\mathrm{J}$ or $\mathrm{MeV}$)
  • $K = (\gamma - 1) m_0 c^2$ = relativistic kinetic energy ($\mathrm{J}$)

Worked Example 13.7 — Relativistic Spaceship Length Contraction
Textbook Example 13.7 (Page 245):
An earth-bound astronomer observing through a telescope spots an extraterrestrial spacecraft flashing across the sky. The spacecraft has a proper length of $50\mathrm{\,m}$ in its own rest frame and is traveling at $95\%$ of the speed of light ($v = 0.95c$). What is the length of the ship as measured from the Earth-bound frame of reference?
Given Data:
  • Proper length $L_0 = 50\mathrm{\,m}$
  • Speed $v = 0.95c \implies \frac{v}{c} = 0.95$
Step 1: Compute the contraction factor $\sqrt{1 - \frac{v^2}{c^2}}$: $$\sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{v}{c}\right)^2} = \sqrt{1 - (0.95)^2} = \sqrt{1 - 0.9025} = \sqrt{0.0975} \approx 0.31225$$ Step 2: Calculate contracted observed length $L$: $$L = L_0 \sqrt{1 - \frac{v^2}{c^2}} = (50\mathrm{\,m}) \times (0.31225) \approx 15.61\mathrm{\,m}$$ Conclusion:
To the Earth observer, the spacecraft appears contracted along its direction of travel to only $15.61\mathrm{\,m}$ (less than a third of its rest length).
Worked Example 13.6 — Pair Annihilation Rest Energy Release
Textbook Example 13.6 (Page 245):
In positron-emission tomography ($\mathrm{PET}$), a stationary positron undergoes mutual annihilation with a stationary electron ($e^- + e^+ \to 2\gamma$). Calculate the rest energy of one electron in $\mathrm{MeV}$, and find the total energy emitted in the resulting gamma ray photons ($m_e = 9.11 \times 10^{-31}\mathrm{\,kg}$).
Step 1: Rest energy of a single electron/positron $E_0 = m_e c^2$: $$E_0 = (9.11 \times 10^{-31}\mathrm{\,kg}) \times (3.0 \times 10^8\mathrm{\,m/s})^2 = (9.11 \times 10^{-31}) \times (9.0 \times 10^{16}) = 8.199 \times 10^{-14}\mathrm{\,J}$$ Step 2: Convert to $\mathrm{MeV}$ ($1\mathrm{\,MeV} = 1.602 \times 10^{-13}\mathrm{\,J}$): $$E_0 = \frac{8.199 \times 10^{-14}\mathrm{\,J}}{1.602 \times 10^{-13}\mathrm{\,J/MeV}} \approx 0.511\mathrm{\,MeV}$$ Step 3: Total energy of the two gamma photons: Because both the electron and positron are annihilated: $$E_{\text{total}} = 2 \times E_0 = 2 \times 0.511\mathrm{\,MeV} = 1.022\mathrm{\,MeV}$$ Two identical gamma photons fly off back-to-back in opposite directions, each carrying exactly $0.511\mathrm{\,MeV}$ ($511\mathrm{\,keV}$).
Key Takeaways
  • Einstein's Postulates: Laws of physics are identical in all inertial frames; speed of light $c$ is constant for all observers.
  • Lorentz factor $\gamma = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - v^2/c^2}} \ge 1$ governs relativistic space-time transformations.
  • Time dilation: Moving clocks tick slower ($\Delta t = \gamma \Delta t_0$).
  • Length contraction: Moving objects shorten along direction of motion ($L = L_0 / \gamma$).
  • Mass-energy equivalence: $E = \gamma m_0 c^2$; rest energy is $E_0 = m_0 c^2$.
