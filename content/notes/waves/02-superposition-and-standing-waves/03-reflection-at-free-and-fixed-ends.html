Reflection at Free and Fixed Ends

A free end reflects the wave as it is; a fixed end turns it upside down

When a wave reaches the end of a rope it comes back: a reflected wave. What it looks like depends on the end.

  • Free end (the rope ends on a ring that slides freely up and down a pole): the end can move, so it swings up and back down with the wave. The reflected wave is the same way up. The incident and reflected waves add at the end, so the end moves with twice the amplitude: the free end is an antinode.
  • Fixed end (tied to a wall): the end can't move, $y = 0$ there all the time. The wall pulls the rope back the other way, so the reflected wave is upside down: its phase is shifted by $\pi$ (half a turn). The fixed end is a node.

How to draw the reflected wave. Pretend the rope carries on through the wall and let the incident wave keep going.

  • Free end: fold the part that has gone past the wall back over the wall (a mirror image).
  • Fixed end: first flip it upside down, then fold it back.

The real rope is the sum of the incident and reflected waves (superposition).

Reflection rules (for a continuous wave of wavelength $\lambda$)

$$\text{fixed end: node at the end, nodes at } 0,\ \tfrac{\lambda}{2},\ \lambda, \dots \text{ from it}$$
$$\text{free end: antinode at the end, nodes at } \tfrac{\lambda}{4},\ \tfrac{3\lambda}{4}, \dots \text{ from it}$$

Fixed end: reflected wave inverted (phase changes by $\pi$). Free end: no phase change. Distances are measured back from the end.

  1. At a fixed end the displacement is always 0, so the end is a node. At a free end the two waves arrive in step, so it is an antinode.
  2. The incident and reflected waves form a standing wave (next note), in which neighbouring nodes are $\lambda/2$ apart and an antinode sits halfway between two nodes.
  3. Fixed end: nodes at $0, \lambda/2, \lambda, \dots$ from the end.
  4. Free end: antinode at the end, so the nearest node is $\lambda/4$ away, then every $\lambda/2$: $\lambda/4, 3\lambda/4, \dots$

Example 1

An upward pulse with a steep front travels towards a wall to which the rope is tied. Describe the reflected pulse.

Show solution
  1. Fixed end: flip it upside down, then fold it back.
  2. Folding swaps the front and the back, but the steep side still leads as it travels back.

A downward pulse travelling back, with the steep side still in front

Example 2

The same pulse reaches a free end instead. Describe the reflected pulse, and how high the end rises if the pulse is 2.0 cm high.

Show solution
  1. Free end: fold it back without flipping, so the reflected pulse is upward.
  2. At the end, the incident and reflected peaks overlap: $2.0 + 2.0$.

An upward pulse travelling back; the end rises to 4.0 cm

Example 3

A wave of wavelength 0.80 m reflects from a fixed end. Find the distances from the end of the first three nodes.

Show solution
  1. Fixed end: nodes at $0, \lambda/2, \lambda$.
  2. $\lambda/2 = 0.40$ m.

0, 0.40 m and 0.80 m

Example 4

The same wave reflects from a free end. Find the distances from the end of the first two nodes and the first two antinodes.

Show solution
  1. Antinodes at $0, \lambda/2$: 0 and 0.40 m.
  2. Nodes at $\lambda/4, 3\lambda/4$: 0.20 m and 0.60 m.

Nodes: 0.20 m, 0.60 m; antinodes: 0 (the end), 0.40 m

Example 5

Near a reflecting end, nodes are found 0.15 m, 0.45 m and 0.75 m from the end. Find the wavelength and say which kind of end it is.

Show solution
  1. Neighbouring nodes are $\lambda/2$ apart: $\lambda/2 = 0.30$ m, so $\lambda = 0.60$ m.
  2. The first node is at $0.15 = \lambda/4$, not at 0, so the end is an antinode.

$\lambda = 0.60$ m; a free end

For a fixed end, both steps are needed: flip and fold. Flipping without folding puts the steep side on the wrong end.
You'll use this again for sound in pipes: a closed end is a fixed end (node), an open end is a free end (antinode).
  • Free end: reflected wave same way up; the end is an antinode.
  • Fixed end: reflected wave inverted (phase shift $\pi$); the end is a node.
  • Draw by extending past the end, then fold (free) or flip and fold (fixed).
