Ceva's Theorem (チェバの定理)

Three lines through one point

Pick a point $P$ inside $\triangle ABC$. The lines $AP$, $BP$, $CP$ hit the opposite sides at $D$ (on $BC$), $E$ (on $CA$) and $F$ (on $AB$).

Walk round the triangle $A \to F \to B \to D \to C \to E \to A$ and multiply the ratios of the pieces. You always get exactly 1.

Ceva's theorem

$$\frac{AF}{FB} \cdot \frac{BD}{DC} \cdot \frac{CE}{EA} = 1$$

Converse: if $D$, $E$, $F$ are on the sides and the product is 1, then $AD$, $BE$, $CF$ meet at one point.

  1. $\triangle APC$ and $\triangle BPC$ share the side $PC$. Their heights to line $PC$ are in the ratio $AF : FB$, so $\dfrac{AF}{FB} = \dfrac{\triangle APC}{\triangle BPC}$.
  2. In the same way, $\dfrac{BD}{DC} = \dfrac{\triangle APB}{\triangle APC}$ and $\dfrac{CE}{EA} = \dfrac{\triangle BPC}{\triangle APB}$.
  3. Multiply: every area cancels, leaving 1.

Drag $P$ (or the corners)

Example 1

$AD$, $BE$, $CF$ meet at one point inside $\triangle ABC$. $AF : FB = 2 : 3$ and $BD : DC = 4 : 5$. Find $CE : EA$.

Show solution
  1. $\dfrac{2}{3} \cdot \dfrac{4}{5} \cdot \dfrac{CE}{EA} = 1$
  2. $\dfrac{CE}{EA} = \dfrac{15}{8}$

$CE : EA = 15 : 8$

Example 2

In $\triangle ABC$, $D$ is on $BC$ with $BD : DC = 2 : 1$, and $E$ is the midpoint of $CA$. $AD$ and $BE$ meet at $P$, and $CP$ meets $AB$ at $F$. Find $AF : FB$.

Show solution
  1. $\dfrac{AF}{FB} \cdot \dfrac{2}{1} \cdot \dfrac{1}{1} = 1$

$AF : FB = 1 : 2$

Example 3

Use Ceva's theorem to show that the three angle bisectors of a triangle meet at one point.

Show solution
  1. With $a = BC$, $b = CA$, $c = AB$, the angle bisector theorem gives $\dfrac{AF}{FB} = \dfrac{b}{a}$, $\;\dfrac{BD}{DC} = \dfrac{c}{b}$, $\;\dfrac{CE}{EA} = \dfrac{a}{c}$.
  2. Product: $\dfrac{b}{a} \cdot \dfrac{c}{b} \cdot \dfrac{a}{c} = 1$

By the converse of Ceva, the bisectors meet at one point (the incenter).

To get the order right, start at a corner and walk round the triangle: corner → point on side → corner → … Each fraction is "the piece you just walked" over "the next piece".
  • Lines from the corners through one point: $\dfrac{AF}{FB} \cdot \dfrac{BD}{DC} \cdot \dfrac{CE}{EA} = 1$.
  • Product 1 $\Rightarrow$ the three lines meet at one point (converse).
  • Proof idea: each ratio is a ratio of two triangle areas.
