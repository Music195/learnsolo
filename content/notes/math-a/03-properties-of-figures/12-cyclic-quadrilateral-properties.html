Properties of a Cyclic Quadrilateral

Opposite angles share the whole circle

A cyclic quadrilateral has all four corners on one circle. $\angle A$ looks at arc $BCD$ and $\angle C$ looks at arc $BAD$. Together those two arcs make the whole circle, so the two angles add up to half of $360°$.

Cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$

$$\angle A + \angle C = 180°, \qquad \angle B + \angle D = 180°$$

Equivalently: the exterior angle at a corner equals the interior angle at the opposite corner, e.g. $\angle DCE = \angle A$ when $BC$ is extended to $E$.

  1. Let $O$ be the centre. The central angles of arc $BCD$ and arc $BAD$ add up to $360°$.
  2. By the inscribed angle theorem, $\angle A$ is half the central angle of arc $BCD$, and $\angle C$ is half the central angle of arc $BAD$.
  3. So $\angle A + \angle C = \dfrac{1}{2} \times 360° = 180°$.
  4. Exterior angle: $\angle DCE = 180° - \angle BCD = \angle A$.

Drag the corners round the circle

Keep them in the order $A, B, C, D$ round the circle.

Example 1

$ABCD$ is cyclic with $\angle A = 65°$ and $\angle B = 75°$. Find $\angle C$ and $\angle D$.

Show solution
  1. $\angle C = 180° - 65°$, $\;\angle D = 180° - 75°$

$\angle C = 115°$, $\angle D = 105°$

Example 2

$ABCD$ is cyclic and $BC$ is extended past $C$ to $E$. $\angle BAD = 80°$. Find $\angle DCE$.

Show solution
  1. The exterior angle at $C$ equals the opposite interior angle $\angle A$.

$\angle DCE = 80°$

Example 3

$ABCD$ is cyclic with $\angle ABC = 100°$ and $\angle ACD = 30°$. Find $\angle CAD$.

Show solution
  1. $\angle ADC = 180° - 100° = 80°$ (opposite angles)
  2. In $\triangle ACD$: $\angle CAD = 180° - 80° - 30°$

$\angle CAD = 70°$

Only opposite angles ($A$ and $C$, or $B$ and $D$) add to $180°$. Neighbouring angles like $A$ and $B$ don't.
  • Cyclic quadrilateral: opposite angles add to $180°$.
  • Exterior angle = opposite interior angle.
  • Proof: the two opposite angles look at arcs that make the whole circle.
