Incenter & Angle Bisectors (内心・内接円)

One circle touching all three sides

Every point on the bisector of an angle is the same distance from the two sides of that angle.

The three angle bisectors of a triangle meet at one point $I$, the incenter. $I$ is the same distance $r$ from all three sides, so the circle with centre $I$ and radius $r$ (the incircle) touches every side.

Inradius from the area

$$S = \frac{1}{2}\, r\,(a + b + c)$$

$S$: area of the triangle. Split it into $\triangle IBC$, $\triangle ICA$, $\triangle IAB$, each with height $r$.

Angle at the incenter

$$\angle BIC = 90° + \frac{\angle A}{2}$$

Because $\angle IBC + \angle ICB = \dfrac{\angle B + \angle C}{2} = \dfrac{180° - \angle A}{2}$.

  1. Let the bisectors of $\angle A$ and $\angle B$ meet at $I$.
  2. $I$ on the bisector of $\angle A$: same distance from $AB$ and $AC$. On the bisector of $\angle B$: same distance from $BA$ and $BC$.
  3. So $I$ is the same distance from $CA$ and $CB$, which puts it on the bisector of $\angle C$. All three meet at $I$.

Drag the corners

Example 1

$I$ is the incenter of $\triangle ABC$ and $\angle A = 70°$. Find $\angle BIC$.

Show solution
  1. $\angle B + \angle C = 110°$, so $\angle IBC + \angle ICB = 55°$
  2. $\angle BIC = 180° - 55°$

$125°$

Example 2

Find the inradius of the right triangle with sides 3, 4, 5.

Show solution
  1. Area $S = \dfrac{1}{2} \times 3 \times 4 = 6$
  2. $6 = \dfrac{1}{2}\, r\, (3 + 4 + 5) = 6r$

$r = 1$

Example 3

In $\triangle ABC$, $AB = 5$, $BC = 6$, $CA = 7$. The incircle touches $BC$ at $D$. Find $BD$.

Show solution
  1. The two tangents from one corner are equal. Call them $x$ (from $A$), $y$ (from $B$), $z$ (from $C$).
  2. $x + y = 5$, $\;y + z = 6$, $\;z + x = 7$. Adding: $x + y + z = 9$.
  3. $BD = y = 9 - (z + x) = 9 - 7$

$BD = 2$

Don't mix them up. Incenter: angle bisectors, equal distance to the sides, always inside. Circumcenter: perpendicular bisectors, equal distance to the corners.
  • Incenter = where the angle bisectors meet; equal distance $r$ to all sides.
  • $S = \dfrac{1}{2} r (a + b + c)$.
  • $\angle BIC = 90° + \dfrac{\angle A}{2}$.
  • Tangents from the same corner are equal.
