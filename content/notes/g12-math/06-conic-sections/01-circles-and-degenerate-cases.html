Circles and Degenerate Cases

Concept — Conic Sections & The Circle

The curves obtained by intersecting a plane with a double right-circular cone (နှစ်ဖက်ချွန် ကတော့ချွန်) are called conic sections (ကတော့ချွန်ဖြတ်ပိုင်းများ): circles (စက်ဝိုင်း), ellipses (ဘဲဥပုံ), parabolas (ပါရာဘိုလာ), and hyperbolas (ဟိုက်ပါဘိုလာ). Every conic section satisfies a second-degree equation in $x$ and $y$:

$$Ax^2 + Bxy + Cy^2 + Dx + Ey + F = 0$$

A circle is the set of all points $(x, y)$ in a plane that are equidistant from a fixed point called the center $(h, k)$ (ဗဟိုချက်) by a fixed distance called the radius $r$ (အချင်းဝက်).

Core Formulas — Standard Form, General Form & Degenerate Cases
  • Standard Form of the Equation of a Circle: With center $(h, k)$ and radius $r > 0$: $$(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2 \qquad (\text{Unit circle at origin: } x^2 + y^2 = 1)$$
  • General Form of the Equation of a Circle ($A = C \neq 0, B = 0$): $$x^2 + y^2 + 2gx + 2fy + c = 0 \iff (x + g)^2 + (y + f)^2 = g^2 + f^2 - c$$ Here the center is $(h, k) = (-g, -f)$ and the radius is $r = \sqrt{g^2 + f^2 - c}$.
  • Degenerate Cases of $x^2 + y^2 + 2gx + 2fy + c = 0$: Let $\Delta = g^2 + f^2 - c$:
    • If $g^2 + f^2 - c > 0$: the graph is a real circle with center $(-g, -f)$ and radius $\sqrt{g^2 + f^2 - c}$.
    • If $g^2 + f^2 - c = 0$: the graph degenerates to a single point $(-g, -f)$.
    • If $g^2 + f^2 - c < 0$: the equation has no real solution and therefore no graph.
Derivation — Completing the Square on the General Circle Equation

Starting from the standard form $(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2$, expanding gives $x^2 + y^2 - 2hx - 2ky + (h^2 + k^2 - r^2) = 0$. Setting $2g = -2h$, $2f = -2k$, and $c = h^2 + k^2 - r^2$ produces the general form $x^2 + y^2 + 2gx + 2fy + c = 0$.

Conversely, given $x^2 + y^2 + 2gx + 2fy + c = 0$, group the $x$- and $y$-terms and complete the square by adding $g^2 + f^2$ to both sides:

$$(x^2 + 2gx + g^2) + (y^2 + 2fy + f^2) = g^2 + f^2 - c \implies (x + g)^2 + (y + f)^2 = g^2 + f^2 - c$$

Comparing with $(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2$ immediately yields center $(-g, -f)$ and $r^2 = g^2 + f^2 - c$.

Worked Examples — Textbook Examples 1, 2, 4 & 5

Example 1 & 2 (Finding the Equation of a Circle):
(a) Find an equation of the circle of radius $4$ centered at $(-1, 2)$ in standard form.
(b) Find an equation for the circle with center $(1, -2)$ that passes through $(4, 2)$.

Show step-by-step solution

(a) With $(h, k) = (-1, 2)$ and $r = 4$:

$$(x - (-1))^2 + (y - 2)^2 = 4^2 \implies (x + 1)^2 + (y - 2)^2 = 16$$

(b) The radius $r$ is the distance from the center $(1, -2)$ to the point $(4, 2)$ on the circle:

$$r = \sqrt{(4 - 1)^2 + (2 - (-2))^2} = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$$

Thus the standard form is $(x - 1)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = 25$, or in general form $x^2 + y^2 - 2x + 4y - 20 = 0$.

Answer: (a) $(x + 1)^2 + (y - 2)^2 = 16$; (b) $(x - 1)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = 25$ (or $x^2 + y^2 - 2x + 4y - 20 = 0$).

Example 4 (Finding Center & Radius by Two Methods): Find the center and radius of:
(a) $x^2 + y^2 - 2x + 10y + 23 = 0$   (b) $2x^2 + 2y^2 - 24x + 47 = 0$.

Show step-by-step solution

(a) Method 1 (Completing the Square):

$$(x^2 - 2x + 1^2) + (y^2 + 10y + 5^2) = -23 + 1 + 25 \implies (x - 1)^2 + (y + 5)^2 = 3$$

Method 2 (Comparing with $x^2 + y^2 + 2gx + 2fy + c = 0$): Here $2g = -2 \implies g = -1$, $2f = 10 \implies f = 5$, and $c = 23$. Thus center $= (-g, -f) = (1, -5)$ and $r = \sqrt{(-1)^2 + 5^2 - 23} = \sqrt{3}$.

(b) Divide by $2$ first! Dividing $2x^2 + 2y^2 - 24x + 47 = 0$ by $2$ gives:

$$x^2 + y^2 - 12x + \frac{47}{2} = 0 \implies (x^2 - 12x + 36) + y^2 = 36 - \frac{47}{2} = \frac{25}{2}$$ $$(x - 6)^2 + y^2 = \left(\frac{5}{\sqrt{2}}\right)^2 \implies \text{Center } = (6, 0), \quad r = \frac{5}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{5\sqrt{2}}{2}$$

Answer: (a) Center $(1, -5)$, radius $\sqrt{3}$; (b) Center $(6, 0)$, radius $\dfrac{5\sqrt{2}}{2}$.

Example 5 (Classifying Degenerate Circle Equations): Describe the graphs of:
(a) $(x - 1)^2 + (y + 4)^2 = -9$   (b) $(x - 1)^2 + (y + 4)^2 = 0$   (c) $x^2 + y^2 - 4x + 2y - 44 = 0$.

Show step-by-step solution
  • (a) Since the right-hand side is $-9 < 0$ (and a sum of squares of real numbers is never negative), the equation has no graph.
  • (b) A sum of two real squares is zero if and only if both terms are zero ($x = 1, y = -4$), so the graph is the single point $(1, -4)$.
  • (c) Here $g = -2, f = 1, c = -44$, so $g^2 + f^2 - c = 4 + 1 + 44 = 49 > 0$. The graph is a circle with center $(2, -1)$ and radius $r = \sqrt{49} = 7$.

Answer: (a) No graph; (b) Single point $(1, -4)$; (c) Circle with center $(2, -1)$ and radius $7$.

Common Mistakes to Avoid
  • Forgetting to Divide by $A$ When $A = C \neq 1$: In $2x^2 + 2y^2 - 24x + 47 = 0$ (Example 4b), you must divide every term by $2$ before reading off $2g, 2f,$ and $c$ or completing the square!
  • Sign Reversal of Center Coordinates: In $(x + 7)^2 + (y - 2)^2 = 9$, the center is $(-7, 2)$, not $(7, -2)$. Similarly, in $x^2 + y^2 + 2gx + 2fy + c = 0$, the center is $(-g, -f)$.
Exam Tip — Circles Tangent to the Coordinate Axes (Exercise 6.1 #2d, 2e)

If a circle with center $(h, k)$ is tangent to the $x$-axis, its radius is the vertical distance to the $x$-axis: $r = |k|$. If it is tangent to the $y$-axis, its radius is the horizontal distance to the $y$-axis: $r = |h|$.

Key Takeaways (မှတ်သားရန် အချက်များ)
  • Standard Circle Equation (စက်ဝိုင်း၏ စံညီမျှခြင်း): $(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2$, center $(h, k)$, radius $r$.
  • General Circle Equation (ယေဘုယျညီမျှခြင်း): $x^2 + y^2 + 2gx + 2fy + c = 0$, center $(-g, -f)$, radius $\sqrt{g^2 + f^2 - c}$.
  • Degenerate Cases: $g^2 + f^2 - c > 0$ (circle), $= 0$ (single point), $< 0$ (no graph).
