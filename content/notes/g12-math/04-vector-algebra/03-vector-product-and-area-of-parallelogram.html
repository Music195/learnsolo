Vector Product and Area of a Parallelogram

Concept — The Vector (Cross) Product & Area of a Parallelogram

Unlike the scalar product, the vector product (or cross product, ဗက်တာမြှောက်လဒ်) is defined only in three-dimensional space and produces a new vector $\vec{a} \times \vec{b}$ that is simultaneously perpendicular to both $\vec{a}$ and $\vec{b}$ ($\vec{a} \times \vec{b} \perp \vec{a}$ and $\vec{a} \times \vec{b} \perp \vec{b}$).

For $\vec{a} = \begin{pmatrix} x_1 \\ y_1 \\ z_1 \end{pmatrix}$ and $\vec{b} = \begin{pmatrix} x_2 \\ y_2 \\ z_2 \end{pmatrix}$, the vector product is defined by:

$$\vec{a} \times \vec{b} = \begin{pmatrix} x_1 \\ y_1 \\ z_1 \end{pmatrix} \times \begin{pmatrix} x_2 \\ y_2 \\ z_2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} y_1z_2 - z_1y_2 \\ z_1x_2 - x_1z_2 \\ x_1y_2 - y_1x_2 \end{pmatrix}$$
Core Formulas — Magnitude, Area of Parallelogram/Triangle & Properties
  • Magnitude of the Vector Product: If $\theta$ ($0 \le \theta \le \pi$) is the angle between $\vec{a}$ and $\vec{b}$, then: $$|\vec{a} \times \vec{b}| = |\vec{a}|\,|\vec{b}|\sin\theta$$
  • Area of a Parallelogram & Triangle: If adjacent sides of parallelogram $OACB$ (or $\triangle OAB$) are determined by $\overrightarrow{OA} = \vec{a}$ and $\overrightarrow{OB} = \vec{b}$, then: $$\text{Area of Parallelogram } OACB = |\vec{a} \times \vec{b}|, \qquad \text{Area of } \triangle ABC = \frac{1}{2}\left|\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC}\right|$$
  • Unit Vector Perpendicular to Both $\vec{a}$ and $\vec{b}$: $$\hat{n} = \frac{\vec{a} \times \vec{b}}{|\vec{a} \times \vec{b}|}$$
  • Algebraic & Geometric Properties: $$\vec{a} \times \vec{b} = -(\vec{b} \times \vec{a}) \text{ (anti-commutative)}, \qquad \vec{a} \parallel \vec{b} \implies \vec{a} \times \vec{b} = \vec{0} \quad (\text{in particular, } \vec{a} \times \vec{a} = \vec{0})$$ $$\vec{a} \perp \vec{b} \implies |\vec{a} \times \vec{b}| = |\vec{a}|\,|\vec{b}|, \qquad |\vec{a} \times \vec{b}|^2 + (\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b})^2 = |\vec{a}|^2|\vec{b}|^2 \text{ (Lagrange's Identity)}$$
Proof — Orthogonality $(\vec{a} \times \vec{b}) \cdot \vec{a} = 0$ & Area Formula

1. Proof that $\vec{a} \times \vec{b} \perp \vec{a}$: Expanding the scalar product of $\vec{a} \times \vec{b}$ with $\vec{a}$:

$$(\vec{a} \times \vec{b}) \cdot \vec{a} = \begin{pmatrix} y_1z_2 - z_1y_2 \\ z_1x_2 - x_1z_2 \\ x_1y_2 - y_1x_2 \end{pmatrix} \cdot \begin{pmatrix} x_1 \\ y_1 \\ z_1 \end{pmatrix} = (y_1z_2 - z_1y_2)x_1 + (z_1x_2 - x_1z_2)y_1 + (x_1y_2 - y_1x_2)z_1$$ $$= x_1y_1z_2 - x_1y_2z_1 + x_2y_1z_1 - x_1y_1z_2 + x_1y_2z_1 - x_2y_1z_1 = 0$$

Since $(\vec{a} \times \vec{b})\cdot\vec{a} = 0$, $\vec{a} \times \vec{b}$ is perpendicular to $\vec{a}$ (and similarly $(\vec{a} \times \vec{b})\cdot\vec{b} = 0$).

2. Area of Parallelogram $OACB$: With base $OA = |\vec{a}|$ and perpendicular height $h = |\vec{b}|\sin\theta$, the area is $\text{base} \times \text{height} = |\vec{a}|(|\vec{b}|\sin\theta) = |\vec{a} \times \vec{b}|$. Since diagonal $AB$ bisects the parallelogram into two congruent triangles, $\alpha(\triangle OAB) = \frac{1}{2}|\vec{a} \times \vec{b}|$.

Worked Examples — Textbook Examples 12, 13, 14 & 15

Example 12 (Area of a Parallelogram): Find the area of the parallelogram determined by the vectors $\vec{a} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix}$ and $\vec{b} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 4 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix}$.

Show step-by-step solution

Compute the vector product $\vec{a} \times \vec{b}$:

$$\vec{a} \times \vec{b} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix} \times \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 4 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 2(-1) - 3(4) \\ 3(1) - 1(-1) \\ 1(4) - 2(1) \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -14 \\ 4 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix}$$

Now calculate the magnitude $|\vec{a} \times \vec{b}|$:

$$|\vec{a} \times \vec{b}| = \sqrt{(-14)^2 + 4^2 + 2^2} = \sqrt{196 + 16 + 4} = \sqrt{216} = 6\sqrt{6}$$

Answer: The area of the parallelogram is $\sqrt{216} = 6\sqrt{6}\text{ unit}^2$.

Example 13 (Area of a Triangle in 3D Space): Find the area of $\triangle ABC$ with vertices $A(1, -1, 3)$, $B(0, 4, 1)$, and $C(2, 1, -1)$.

Show step-by-step solution

Form two adjacent edge vectors $\overrightarrow{AB}$ and $\overrightarrow{AC}$ from vertex $A$:

$$\overrightarrow{AB} = \overrightarrow{OB} - \overrightarrow{OA} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 - 1 \\ 4 - (-1) \\ 1 - 3 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 5 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad \overrightarrow{AC} = \overrightarrow{OC} - \overrightarrow{OA} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 - 1 \\ 1 - (-1) \\ -1 - 3 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \\ -4 \end{pmatrix}$$

Compute their cross product $\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC}$:

$$\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC} = \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 5 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix} \times \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \\ -4 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 5(-4) - (-2)(2) \\ (-2)(1) - (-1)(-4) \\ (-1)(2) - 5(1) \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -16 \\ -6 \\ -7 \end{pmatrix}$$ $$\left|\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC}\right| = \sqrt{(-16)^2 + (-6)^2 + (-7)^2} = \sqrt{256 + 36 + 49} = \sqrt{341}$$

Answer: $\text{Area of }\triangle ABC = \dfrac{1}{2}\left|\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC}\right| = \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{341}\text{ unit}^2$.

Example 14 (Unit Vector Perpendicular to Two Vectors): Given $\vec{a} = 3\hat{i} + 2\hat{j} + 5\hat{k}$ and $\vec{b} = \hat{i} - 4\hat{j} + 2\hat{k}$:
(a) Calculate $\vec{a} \times \vec{b}$.   (b) Find a unit vector $\hat{n}$ perpendicular to both $\vec{a}$ and $\vec{b}$.

Show step-by-step solution

(a)

$$\vec{a} \times \vec{b} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 2 \\ 5 \end{pmatrix} \times \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -4 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 2(2) - 5(-4) \\ 5(1) - 3(2) \\ 3(-4) - 2(1) \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 24 \\ -1 \\ -14 \end{pmatrix}$$

(b) Since $\vec{a} \times \vec{b}$ is perpendicular to both $\vec{a}$ and $\vec{b}$, we divide by its magnitude:

$$|\vec{a} \times \vec{b}| = \sqrt{24^2 + (-1)^2 + (-14)^2} = \sqrt{576 + 1 + 196} = \sqrt{773}$$

Answer: $\hat{n} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{773}}\begin{pmatrix} 24 \\ -1 \\ -14 \end{pmatrix}$.

Example 15 (Simplifying Cross Products Algebraically): Given $|\vec{a}| = 4$, $|\vec{b}| = 5$, and that $\vec{a}$ and $\vec{b}$ are perpendicular, evaluate $|(2\vec{a} - \vec{b}) \times (\vec{a} + 3\vec{b})|$.

Show step-by-step solution

Expand using the distributive law, remembering $\vec{a} \times \vec{a} = \vec{0}$, $\vec{b} \times \vec{b} = \vec{0}$, and $\vec{b} \times \vec{a} = -(\vec{a} \times \vec{b})$:

$$(2\vec{a} - \vec{b}) \times (\vec{a} + 3\vec{b}) = 2(\vec{a} \times \vec{a}) + 6(\vec{a} \times \vec{b}) - (\vec{b} \times \vec{a}) - 3(\vec{b} \times \vec{b})$$ $$= \vec{0} + 6(\vec{a} \times \vec{b}) + (\vec{a} \times \vec{b}) - \vec{0} = 7(\vec{a} \times \vec{b})$$

Since $\vec{a} \perp \vec{b}$, $|\vec{a} \times \vec{b}| = |\vec{a}|\,|\vec{b}| = 4 \times 5 = 20$:

$$|(2\vec{a} - \vec{b}) \times (\vec{a} + 3\vec{b})| = 7|\vec{a} \times \vec{b}| = 7(4)(5) = 140$$

Answer: $140$.

Common Mistakes to Avoid
  • Middle Component Sign Error: In $\vec{a} \times \vec{b}$, the $y$-component is $z_1x_2 - x_1z_2$ (or $-(x_1z_2 - z_1x_2)$). Always check your answer by verifying that $(\vec{a} \times \vec{b}) \cdot \vec{a} = 0$ and $(\vec{a} \times \vec{b}) \cdot \vec{b} = 0$!
  • Forgetting the Factor of $\frac{1}{2}$ for Triangle Area: $|\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC}|$ is the area of the parallelogram; the area of $\triangle ABC$ is $\frac{1}{2}|\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC}|$.
Exam Tip — Textbook Shortcut: "Down Product minus Up Product"

Write the two column vectors side by side and repeat the top two rows at the bottom (or cover the row you are calculating): for the $x$-row, cross-multiply rows $2$ and $3$ ($y_1z_2 - z_1y_2$); for the $y$-row, cross-multiply row $3$ with row $1$ ($z_1x_2 - x_1z_2$); for the $z$-row, cross-multiply rows $1$ and $2$ ($x_1y_2 - y_1x_2$).

Key Takeaways (မှတ်သားရန် အချက်များ)
  • Vector Product (ဗက်တာမြှောက်လဒ်): $\vec{a} \times \vec{b} = \begin{pmatrix} y_1z_2 - z_1y_2 \\ z_1x_2 - x_1z_2 \\ x_1y_2 - y_1x_2 \end{pmatrix}$ is perpendicular to both $\vec{a}$ and $\vec{b}$.
  • Parallelogram & Triangle Areas (အနားပြိုင်စတုဂံနှင့် တြိဂံဧရိယာ): $\text{Area}(\text{Parallelogram}) = |\vec{a} \times \vec{b}|$ and $\text{Area}(\triangle ABC) = \frac{1}{2}|\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC}|$.
  • Anti-Commutativity & Self-Cross: $\vec{b} \times \vec{a} = -(\vec{a} \times \vec{b})$ and $\vec{a} \times \vec{a} = \vec{0}$.
