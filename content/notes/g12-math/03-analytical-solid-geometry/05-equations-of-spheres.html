Equations of Spheres

All Points in Space at Distance $r$ from Center $C(x_1, y_1, z_1)$

A sphere (စက်လုံး) is the set of all points $(x, y, z)$ in three-dimensional space whose distance from a fixed point $C(x_1, y_1, z_1)$ (the center) is equal to a constant positive number $r$ (the radius): $$\sqrt{(x - x_1)^2 + (y - y_1)^2 + (z - z_1)^2} = r$$ Squaring both sides gives the standard equation of a sphere.

Just as a tangent line to a circle is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact, the tangent plane (ထိမျက်နှာပြင်) to a sphere at a surface point $P(x_0, y_0, z_0)$ is perpendicular to the radius line $CP$!

Standard Equation of a Sphere & Tangent Plane

$$(x - x_1)^2 + (y - y_1)^2 + (z - z_1)^2 = r^2$$

• Center at origin $O(0, 0, 0)$: $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = r^2$.
• Inside / On / Outside Test for $P(x_0, y_0, z_0)$: Compare $CP^2 = (x_0 - x_1)^2 + (y_0 - y_1)^2 + (z_0 - z_1)^2$ with $r^2$: if $CP^2 < r^2$ (inside), $CP^2 = r^2$ (on the sphere), $CP^2 > r^2$ (outside).
• Tangent Plane at $P(x_0, y_0, z_0)$: Normal directed values are $(CP) = (x_0 - x_1, \; y_0 - y_1, \; z_0 - z_1)$, so: $$(x_0 - x_1)(x - x_0) + (y_0 - y_1)(y - y_0) + (z_0 - z_1)(z - z_0) = 0$$

  1. The point of contact $P(x_0, y_0, z_0)$ is the point on the tangent plane closest to the center $C(x_1, y_1, z_1)$.
  2. Therefore, the line $CP$ is perpendicular to the tangent plane at $P$.
  3. Since $(CP) = (x_0 - x_1, y_0 - y_1, z_0 - z_1)$, these directed values give the coefficients $(a, b, c)$ of the tangent plane $ax + by + cz = d$.

3D Sphere & Tangent Plane at $P$: Radius $CP$ (orange) is always perpendicular to the Tangent Plane

Example 1: Tangent Plane to a Sphere (Textbook Page 38, Example 11)

Find the equation of the plane tangent to the sphere $$(x - 2)^2 + (y - 1)^2 + (z + 1)^2 = 14$$ at the point $P(3, 4, 1)$.

Show solution
  1. The center of the sphere is $C(2, 1, -1)$.
    Directed values of the line joining center $C(2, 1, -1)$ and $P(3, 4, 1)$ are: $$(CP) = (3 - 2, \; 4 - 1, \; 1 - (-1)) = (1, 3, 2)$$
  2. Since line $CP$ is perpendicular to the tangent plane, the plane equation has the form: $$x + 3y + 2z = d$$
  3. Since $P(3, 4, 1)$ lies on this plane, substitute $(3, 4, 1)$: $$d = 3 + 3(4) + 2(1) = 3 + 12 + 2 = 17$$

$x + 3y + 2z = 17$

Example 2: Sphere Touching a Given Plane (Textbook Page 38, Example 12)

Find the equation of the sphere with center $C(0, 1, 0)$ and touching the plane $x - 2y + 2z + 5 = 0$.

Show solution
  1. The line through $C(0, 1, 0)$ perpendicular to the plane $x - 2y + 2z + 5 = 0$ has directed values $(1, -2, 2)$: $$\frac{x - 0}{1} = \frac{y - 1}{-2} = \frac{z - 0}{2} = s \implies (x, y, z) = (s, \; 1 - 2s, \; 2s)$$
  2. At the point of contact $P$ on the plane: $$s - 2(1 - 2s) + 2(2s) + 5 = 0 \implies 9s + 3 = 0 \implies s = -\frac{1}{3}$$ Thus $P = \left(-\dfrac{1}{3}, \dfrac{5}{3}, -\dfrac{2}{3}\right)$.
  3. Radius $r = CP = \sqrt{\left(-\frac{1}{3} - 0\right)^2 + \left(\frac{5}{3} - 1\right)^2 + \left(-\frac{2}{3} - 0\right)^2} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{9} + \frac{4}{9} + \frac{4}{9}} = 1$.

$x^2 + (y - 1)^2 + z^2 = 1$

Example 3: Sphere Through Three Points with Center on $yz$-Plane (Textbook Page 39, Example 13)

Find the equation of the sphere passing through $(9, 0, 0)$, $(3, 13, 6)$, and $(11, 0, 10)$, given that its center lies on the $yz$-plane.

Show solution
  1. Since the center lies on the $yz$-plane ($x_1 = 0$), let the center be $C(0, y_1, z_1)$ and radius be $r$: $$(x - 0)^2 + (y - y_1)^2 + (z - z_1)^2 = r^2$$
  2. Equate squared distances from $(0, y_1, z_1)$ to each point:
    From $(9, 0, 0)$: $81 + y_1^2 + z_1^2 = r^2 \quad (1)$
    From $(3, 13, 6)$: $9 + (13 - y_1)^2 + (6 - z_1)^2 = r^2 \quad (2)$
    From $(11, 0, 10)$: $121 + y_1^2 + (10 - z_1)^2 = r^2 \quad (3)$
  3. From $(1)$ and $(3)$: $81 + z_1^2 = 121 + 100 - 20z_1 + z_1^2 \implies 20z_1 = 140 \implies z_1 = 7$.
  4. Substitute $z_1 = 7$ into $(1)$ and $(2)$: $$81 + y_1^2 + 49 = 9 + (169 - 26y_1 + y_1^2) + 1 \implies 130 = 179 - 26y_1$$ Solving gives $y_1 = 2$, $z_1 = 7$, and $r^2 = 81 + 2^2 + 7^2 = 134$.

$x^2 + (y - 2)^2 + (z - 7)^2 = 134$

Don't Forget to Square $r$ on the Right Side! A sphere with center $(1, -2, 4)$ and radius $r = 3$ has equation $(x - 1)^2 + (y + 2)^2 + (z - 4)^2 = 9$ (not $3$), and notice the signs inside the brackets are opposite to the center coordinates!
Sphere on a Diameter $AB$: If $A$ and $B$ are endpoints of a diameter, the center $C$ is the midpoint of $AB$ and the radius is $r = CA = \frac{1}{2}AB$.
  • Sphere with center $(x_1, y_1, z_1)$ and radius $r$: $(x - x_1)^2 + (y - y_1)^2 + (z - z_1)^2 = r^2$ (စက်လုံးညီမျှခြင်း).
  • The radius line $CP$ is perpendicular to the tangent plane at the point of contact $P$ (ထိမျက်နှာပြင်).
  • A point $P$ is inside if $CP < r$, on the sphere if $CP = r$, and outside if $CP > r$.
