Parallel, Skew, and Perpendicular Lines

Spatial Relationships of Lines in Three Dimensions

In a 2D plane, two distinct lines either intersect at a single point or are parallel. But in 3D space, a third possibility arises: two lines can be neither parallel nor intersect!

Such lines are called skew lines (စောင်းမျဉ်းများ သို့မဟုတ် ဖြတ်မျဉ်းမဟုတ်-အပြိုင်မဟုတ်သော မျဉ်းများ). They lie in different parallel planes and never meet.

To analyze the spatial relationship between two lines $L_1$ and $L_2$ with directed values $(l_1, m_1, n_1)$ and $(l_2, m_2, n_2)$:

  • Parallel Lines: Their directed values are proportional (scalar multiples of each other).
  • Perpendicular Lines: The sum of the products of their directed values equals zero ($l_1 l_2 + m_1 m_2 + n_1 n_2 = 0$).
  • Intersecting Lines: They are not parallel and have a common point satisfying both parametric systems simultaneously.
  • Skew Lines: They are not parallel and have no common point (the system of parametric equations is inconsistent).

Mathematical Conditions

1. Condition for Parallel Lines ($L_1 \parallel L_2$)

$$(l_1, m_1, n_1) = c(l_2, m_2, n_2) \iff \frac{l_1}{l_2} = \frac{m_1}{m_2} = \frac{n_1}{n_2} = c \quad (c \ne 0)$$

2. Condition for Perpendicular Lines ($L_1 \perp L_2$)

$$l_1 l_2 + m_1 m_2 + n_1 n_2 = 0$$

3. Complete Classification Algorithm (Intersection vs. Skew)

  1. Check if directed values are proportional. If yes, lines are Parallel.
  2. If not parallel, express both lines parametrically with distinct parameters $s$ and $t$:
    $L_1: (x_1 + s l_1, \; y_1 + s m_1, \; z_1 + s n_1)$
    $L_2: (x_2 + t l_2, \; y_2 + t m_2, \; z_2 + t n_2)$
  3. Set up three equations by equating coordinates: $$\begin{cases} x_1 + s l_1 = x_2 + t l_2 & (1) \\ y_1 + s m_1 = y_2 + t m_2 & (2) \\ z_1 + s n_1 = z_2 + t n_2 & (3) \end{cases}$$
  4. Solve equations (1) and (2) for $s$ and $t$.
  5. Substitute $(s, t)$ into equation (3):
    • If LHS = RHS: The lines Intersect at the point found by plugging $s$ into $L_1$.
    • If LHS $\ne$ RHS: The lines are Skew.

Example 1: Classifying Lines as Parallel, Intersecting, or Skew (Textbook Page 32, Example 5)

Given $P(2, 1, 3)$, $Q(6, -5, 4)$, $R(2, 3, 4)$, and $S(-1, 5, 4)$, determine whether the lines $PQ$ and $RS$ are parallel, skew, or intersect.

Show solution
  1. Find directed values for $PQ$ and $RS$: $$(PQ) = (l_1, m_1, n_1) = (6 - 2, \; -5 - 1, \; 4 - 3) = (4, -6, 1)$$ $$(RS) = (l_2, m_2, n_2) = (-1 - 2, \; 5 - 3, \; 4 - 4) = (-3, 2, 0)$$
  2. Compare ratios of directed values: $$\frac{4}{-3} \ne \frac{-6}{2} = -3 \ne \frac{1}{0}$$ Since directed values are not scalar multiples of each other, the lines are not parallel.
  3. Write parametric equations using parameters $s$ for $PQ$ and $t$ for $RS$:
    Line $PQ$: $(x, y, z) = (2 + 4s, \; 1 - 6s, \; 3 + s)$
    Line $RS$: $(x, y, z) = (2 - 3t, \; 3 + 2t, \; 4)$
  4. Equate coordinates to find common intersection $(s, t)$: $$\begin{align} 2 + 4s &= 2 - 3t &\implies 4s + 3t = 0 \quad (1) \\ 1 - 6s &= 3 + 2t &\implies -6s - 2t = 2 \quad (2) \\ 3 + s &= 4 &\implies s = 1 \quad (3) \end{align}$$
  5. From equation (3), we must have $s = 1$.
    Substitute $s = 1$ into equation (1): $$4(1) + 3t = 0 \implies 3t = -4 \implies t = -\frac{4}{3}$$ Now test $(s, t) = \left(1, -\frac{4}{3}\right)$ in equation (2): $$-6(1) - 2\left(-\frac{4}{3}\right) = -6 + \frac{8}{3} = -\frac{10}{3} \ne 2$$ Equation (2) is not satisfied!
  6. Because the system has no solution, lines $PQ$ and $RS$ do not intersect. Since they are also not parallel, they are skew lines.

Example 2: Proving Lines are Perpendicular (Textbook Page 33, Example 6)

Given $P(0, 0, 1)$, $Q(3, 6, 4)$, $R(0, 3, 1)$, and $S(3, 0, 4)$, show that the lines $PQ$ and $RS$ are perpendicular.

Show solution
  1. Compute the directed values of $PQ$: $$(PQ) = (l_1, m_1, n_1) = (3 - 0, \; 6 - 0, \; 4 - 1) = (3, 6, 3)$$
  2. Compute the directed values of $RS$: $$(RS) = (l_2, m_2, n_2) = (3 - 0, \; 0 - 3, \; 4 - 1) = (3, -3, 3)$$
  3. Apply the perpendicularity condition $l_1 l_2 + m_1 m_2 + n_1 n_2$: $$(3)(3) + (6)(-3) + (3)(3) = 9 - 18 + 9 = 0$$
  4. Since the sum of the products of their directed values is zero, the lines $PQ$ and $RS$ are perpendicular ($PQ \perp RS$).

Example 3: Perpendicular Line and Point of Intersection (Textbook Page 34, Example 7)

Find the equation of the line passing through the point $A(4, 7, -3)$ and perpendicular to the line $L_1$: $$(x, y, z) = (8 + 2k, \; -1 + 3k, \; 1 - k)$$ Find also the point of intersection of the two lines.

Show solution
  1. The directed values of the given line $L_1$ are $(l_1, m_1, n_1) = (2, 3, -1)$.
    Let $B$ be the point of intersection on $L_1$. For some parameter $k$, $B$ has coordinates: $$B(8 + 2k, \; -1 + 3k, \; 1 - k)$$
  2. The directed values of line segment $AB$ connecting $A(4, 7, -3)$ to $B$ are: $$(AB) = ((8 + 2k) - 4, \; (-1 + 3k) - 7, \; (1 - k) - (-3)) = (4 + 2k, \; -8 + 3k, \; 4 - k)$$
  3. Since line $AB$ is perpendicular to $L_1$, their directed values must satisfy $l_1 l_2 + m_1 m_2 + n_1 n_2 = 0$: $$2(4 + 2k) + 3(-8 + 3k) + (-1)(4 - k) = 0$$ $$8 + 4k - 24 + 9k - 4 + k = 0$$ $$14k - 20 = 0 \implies 14k = 20 \implies k = \frac{10}{7}$$ Wait, let's double check arithmetic: $8 - 24 - 4 = -20$; $4k + 9k + k = 14k \implies k = 10/7$.
  4. Substitute $k = \frac{10}{7}$ into $B$: $$x = 8 + 2\left(\tfrac{10}{7}\right) = \frac{76}{7}, \quad y = -1 + 3\left(\tfrac{10}{7}\right) = \frac{23}{7}, \quad z = 1 - \frac{10}{7} = -\frac{3}{7}$$ Intersection point: $B\left(\frac{76}{7}, \frac{23}{7}, -\frac{3}{7}\right)$.
  5. Directed values for the perpendicular line $AB$: $$4 + 2\left(\tfrac{10}{7}\right) = \frac{48}{7}, \quad -8 + 3\left(\tfrac{10}{7}\right) = -\frac{26}{7}, \quad 4 - \frac{10}{7} = \frac{18}{7}$$ Multiplying by 7 gives directed values $(48, -26, 18)$ or simplified $(24, -13, 9)$.
    Equation of the required line through $(4, 7, -3)$: $$\frac{x - 4}{24} = \frac{y - 7}{-13} = \frac{z + 3}{9}$$

Key Takeaways & Terminology (အဓိကမှတ်သားဖွယ်ရာများ)

  • Parallel Lines (အပြိုင်မျဉ်းများ): $\frac{l_1}{l_2} = \frac{m_1}{m_2} = \frac{n_1}{n_2}$. Directed values are proportional.
  • Perpendicular Lines (ထောင့်မတ်မျဉ်းများ): $l_1 l_2 + m_1 m_2 + n_1 n_2 = 0$.
  • Skew Lines (စောင်းမျဉ်းများ / ဖြတ်မျဉ်းမဟုတ်-အပြိုင်မဟုတ်သော မျဉ်းများ): Non-parallel lines that do not intersect because they lie in different planes in 3D space.
  • Intersection Test: Solve the 2-parameter system for two coordinates, then test consistency in the third coordinate.
