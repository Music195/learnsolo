Arithmetic Operations and Conjugates

Subtraction, Conjugates, and Division of Complex Numbers

Addition and multiplication of complex numbers follow standard algebraic rules with $i^2 = -1$. Subtraction is straightforward: we subtract corresponding real and imaginary components.

However, division requires eliminating the imaginary unit $i$ from the denominator. To do this, we use the complex conjugate (တွဲဖက်ကွန်ပလက်စ်ကိန်း), denoted by $\bar{z}$.

For $z = x + yi$, its conjugate is $\bar{z} = x - yi$. Multiplying $z$ by its conjugate produces a purely real number: $$z \bar{z} = (x + yi)(x - yi) = x^2 - (yi)^2 = x^2 + y^2$$ This allows us to divide any two complex numbers by multiplying the numerator and denominator by the conjugate of the denominator.

Conjugates, Inverses & Division

1. Complex Conjugate

$$z = x + yi \implies \bar{z} = x - yi \qquad (z\bar{z} = x^2 + y^2)$$

2. Multiplicative Inverse ($z^{-1}$)

For $z \ne 0$:   $z^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{z} = \dfrac{\bar{z}}{z\bar{z}} = \dfrac{x - yi}{x^2 + y^2} = \left(\dfrac{x}{x^2 + y^2}, \; \dfrac{-y}{x^2 + y^2}\right)$

3. Division Formula

$$\frac{z_1}{z_2} = \frac{x_1 + y_1 i}{x_2 + y_2 i} = \frac{(x_1 + y_1 i)(x_2 - y_2 i)}{(x_2 + y_2 i)(x_2 - y_2 i)} = \frac{(x_1 x_2 + y_1 y_2) + (y_1 x_2 - x_1 y_2)i}{x_2^2 + y_2^2}$$

Example 1 (Textbook Page 5, Example 4)

Calculate $\dfrac{2 + 3i}{3 + i}$.

Show solution
  1. Identify the conjugate of the denominator: for $3 + i$, the conjugate is $3 - i$.
  2. Multiply numerator and denominator by $(3 - i)$: $$\frac{2 + 3i}{3 + i} = \frac{(2 + 3i)(3 - i)}{(3 + i)(3 - i)}$$
  3. Expand the numerator: $(2)(3) - 2i + 9i - 3i^2 = 6 + 7i + 3 = 9 + 7i$.
  4. Expand the denominator: $3^2 - i^2 = 9 - (-1) = 10$.
  5. Separate real and imaginary components: $$\frac{9 + 7i}{10} = \frac{9}{10} + \frac{7}{10}i$$

$\dfrac{9}{10} + \dfrac{7}{10}i$ or $(0.9, 0.7)$

Example 2 (Textbook Page 6)

Find the multiplicative inverse $z^{-1}$ for $z = 3 - 2i$, and verify that $z z^{-1} = 1$.

Show solution
  1. Compute $z\bar{z} = x^2 + y^2 = 3^2 + (-2)^2 = 9 + 4 = 13$.
  2. Write the conjugate $\bar{z} = 3 + 2i$.
  3. Form the inverse: $$z^{-1} = \frac{\bar{z}}{z\bar{z}} = \frac{3 + 2i}{13} = \frac{3}{13} + \frac{2}{13}i$$
  4. Verification: $$(3 - 2i)\left(\frac{3 + 2i}{13}\right) = \frac{3^2 - (2i)^2}{13} = \frac{9 - 4(-1)}{13} = \frac{13}{13} = 1$$

$z^{-1} = \dfrac{3}{13} + \dfrac{2}{13}i$

Example 3 (Conjugate Properties)

Let $z_1 = -2 + 3i$ and $z_2 = 5 + 2i$. Show that $\overline{z_1 + z_2} = \bar{z}_1 + \bar{z}_2$.

Show solution
  1. Compute $z_1 + z_2 = (-2 + 5) + (3 + 2)i = 3 + 5i$.
  2. Take the conjugate: $\overline{z_1 + z_2} = 3 - 5i$.
  3. Compute individual conjugates: $\bar{z}_1 = -2 - 3i$ and $\bar{z}_2 = 5 - 2i$.
  4. Add them: $\bar{z}_1 + \bar{z}_2 = (-2 + 5) + (-3 - 2)i = 3 - 5i$.

Both sides equal $3 - 5i$, confirming $\overline{z_1 + z_2} = \bar{z}_1 + \bar{z}_2$

Caution on Inverses: The multiplicative inverse $z^{-1}$ exists if and only if $z \ne 0 + 0i$. Division by zero is undefined in complex numbers just like in real numbers.
Conjugate Rules to Remember:
  • $\overline{\bar{z}} = z$ (conjugate of conjugate is original)
  • $\overline{z_1 + z_2} = \bar{z}_1 + \bar{z}_2$ and $\overline{z_1 z_2} = \bar{z}_1 \bar{z}_2$
  • $\overline{\left(\frac{z_1}{z_2}\right)} = \frac{\bar{z}_1}{\bar{z}_2}$
  • The complex conjugate of $z = x + yi$ is $\bar{z} = x - yi$ (တွဲဖက်ကွန်ပလက်စ်ကိန်း).
  • $z\bar{z} = x^2 + y^2$ is always a real, non-negative quantity.
  • To divide two complex numbers, multiply both top and bottom by the conjugate of the denominator (ကိန်းခြေမှ $i$ ကိုဖျောက်ရန် တွဲဖက်ကိန်းဖြင့်မြှောက်ပါ).
