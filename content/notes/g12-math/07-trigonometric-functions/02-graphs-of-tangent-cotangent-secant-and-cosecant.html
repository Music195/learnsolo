Graphs of Tangent, Cotangent, Secant, and Cosecant

Concept — Quotient & Reciprocal Trigonometric Graphs

The four remaining trigonometric functions are defined as quotients and reciprocals of $\sin x$ and $\cos x$:

$$\tan x = \frac{\sin x}{\cos x}, \qquad \cot x = \frac{\cos x}{\sin x}, \qquad \sec x = \frac{1}{\cos x}, \qquad \csc x = \frac{1}{\sin x}$$

Whenever the denominator ($\cos x$ or $\sin x$) equals zero, the function is undefined and its graph has a vertical asymptote (ထောင့်မတ် အနန္တကပ်မျဉ်း). Under horizontal scaling by $\frac{1}{b}$ and vertical scaling by $a$ ($a, b > 0$), every point $(x, y)$ maps to $\left(\frac{x}{b}, ay\right)$.

Core Formulas — Domain, Range, Period & Asymptotes ($a > 0, b > 0$)
  • 1. Tangent ($y = a\tan bx$):
    • Period: $\dfrac{\pi}{b}$   Range: $\mathbb{R}$   Symmetry: Odd function
    • $x$-intercepts: $x = 0,\; \pm\dfrac{\pi}{b},\; \pm\dfrac{2\pi}{b},\dots$   Vertical Asymptotes: $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{2b},\; \pm\dfrac{3\pi}{2b},\dots$
  • 2. Cotangent ($y = a\cot bx$):
    • Period: $\dfrac{\pi}{b}$   Range: $\mathbb{R}$   Symmetry: Odd function
    • $x$-intercepts: $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{2b},\; \pm\dfrac{3\pi}{2b},\dots$   Vertical Asymptotes: $x = 0,\; \pm\dfrac{\pi}{b},\; \pm\dfrac{2\pi}{b},\dots$
  • 3. Secant ($y = a\sec bx = \frac{a}{\cos bx}$):
    • Period: $\dfrac{2\pi}{b}$   Range: $\{y \mid y \le -a \text{ or } y \ge a\}$   Symmetry: Even function
    • Vertical Asymptotes: $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{2b},\; \pm\dfrac{3\pi}{2b},\dots$ (where $\cos bx = 0$). Local turning points touch $y = a\cos bx$ at $(0, a)$ and $\left(\frac{\pi}{b}, -a\right)$.
  • 4. Cosecant ($y = a\csc bx = \frac{a}{\sin bx}$):
    • Period: $\dfrac{2\pi}{b}$   Range: $\{y \mid y \le -a \text{ or } y \ge a\}$   Symmetry: Odd function
    • Vertical Asymptotes: $x = 0,\; \pm\dfrac{\pi}{b},\; \pm\dfrac{2\pi}{b},\dots$ (where $\sin bx = 0$). Local turning points touch $y = a\sin bx$ at $\left(\frac{\pi}{2b}, a\right)$ and $\left(\frac{3\pi}{2b}, -a\right)$.
Justification — Why $\tan x$ Has Period $\pi$ While $\sec x$ Has Period $2\pi$

1. Period of $\tan x$ and $\cot x$: Since $\sin(x + \pi) = -\sin x$ and $\cos(x + \pi) = -\cos x$, taking their ratio cancels the minus signs:

$$\tan(x + \pi) = \frac{\sin(x + \pi)}{\cos(x + \pi)} = \frac{-\sin x}{-\cos x} = \tan x$$

Thus $y = \tan x$ and $y = \cot x$ repeat every $\pi$ radians, and scaling $x \mapsto bx$ gives period $\dfrac{\pi}{b}$.

2. Range of $y = a\sec bx$ and $y = a\csc bx$: Since $-1 \le \cos bx \le 1$ and $-1 \le \sin bx \le 1$, taking the reciprocal of a number in $[-1, 1] \setminus \{0\}$ always produces a magnitude $\ge 1$. Multiplying by $a > 0$ shows that $|y| \ge a$, so the range is $(-\infty, -a] \cup [a, \infty)$.

Worked Examples — Textbook Example 7 & Exercise 7.3

Example 7 (Graphs of $y = a\tan bx$ and $y = a\cot bx$): Describe the domain, range, period, $x$-intercepts, vertical asymptotes, and key quarter-period points of $y = 3\tan\frac{1}{2}x$ and $y = 3\cot 2x$.

Show step-by-step solution

(a) $y = 3\tan\frac{1}{2}x$ ($a = 3, b = \frac{1}{2}$): Under $(x, y) \mapsto (2x, 3y)$:

  • Period: $\dfrac{\pi}{b} = \dfrac{\pi}{1/2} = 2\pi$,   Range: $\mathbb{R}$.
  • $x$-intercepts: $x = 0, \pm 2\pi, \pm 4\pi, \dots$
  • Vertical Asymptotes: $x = \pm\pi, \pm 3\pi, \dots$ (so Domain is $x \neq \pm\pi, \pm 3\pi, \dots$).
  • Key Points in $(-\pi, \pi)$: $\left(-\frac{\pi}{2}, -3\right),\; (0, 0),\; \left(\frac{\pi}{2}, 3\right)$.

(b) $y = 3\cot 2x$ ($a = 3, b = 2$): Under $(x, y) \mapsto \left(\frac{x}{2}, 3y\right)$:

  • Period: $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$,   Range: $\mathbb{R}$.
  • $x$-intercepts: $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{4}, \pm\dfrac{3\pi}{4}, \dots$
  • Vertical Asymptotes: $x = 0, \pm\dfrac{\pi}{2}, \pm\pi, \dots$ (Domain: $x \neq 0, \pm\frac{\pi}{2}, \dots$).
  • Key Points in $\left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$: $\left(\frac{\pi}{8}, 3\right),\; \left(\frac{\pi}{4}, 0\right),\; \left(\frac{3\pi}{8}, -3\right)$.

Answer: For $y = 3\tan\frac{1}{2}x$: Period $2\pi$, asymptotes $x = \pm\pi, \pm 3\pi,\dots$; for $y = 3\cot 2x$: Period $\frac{\pi}{2}$, asymptotes $x = 0, \pm\frac{\pi}{2},\dots$.

Exercise 7.3 #3, #4, #5 (Secant, Cosecant & Odd/Even Symmetry Proofs):
(a) Find the domain, range, period, and vertical asymptotes of $y = 2\sec 3x$ and $y = 3\csc\frac{1}{2}x$.
(b) Prove that $y = a\tan bx, y = a\cot bx,$ and $y = a\csc bx$ are odd functions, while $y = a\sec bx$ is an even function.

Show step-by-step solution

(a) For $y = 2\sec 3x$ ($a = 2, b = 3$): Period $= \dfrac{2\pi}{3}$, Range $= \{y \mid y \le -2 \text{ or } y \ge 2\}$, Vertical Asymptotes at $x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{6}, \pm\dfrac{\pi}{2}, \dots$
For $y = 3\csc\frac{1}{2}x$ ($a = 3, b = \frac{1}{2}$): Period $= 4\pi$, Range $= \{y \mid y \le -3 \text{ or } y \ge 3\}$, Vertical Asymptotes at $x = 0, \pm 2\pi, \pm 4\pi, \dots$

(b) Symmetry Proofs: Since $\sin(-bx) = -\sin(bx)$ and $\cos(-bx) = \cos(bx)$:

$$f(-x) = a\tan(-bx) = a\frac{\sin(-bx)}{\cos(-bx)} = a\frac{-\sin bx}{\cos bx} = -a\tan bx = -f(x) \quad (\text{odd})$$ $$g(-x) = a\sec(-bx) = \frac{a}{\cos(-bx)} = \frac{a}{\cos bx} = a\sec bx = g(x) \quad (\text{even})$$

(Similarly, $a\cot(-bx) = -a\cot bx$ and $a\csc(-bx) = -a\csc bx$, so both are odd functions.)

Answer: $\tan, \cot, \csc$ are odd functions; $\sec$ is an even function.

Common Mistakes to Avoid
  • Period of $\tan bx$ and $\cot bx$ Is $\frac{\pi}{b}$, NOT $\frac{2\pi}{b}$: Remember that $\sin, \cos, \sec, \csc$ have base period $2\pi$ (scaled to $\frac{2\pi}{b}$), whereas $\tan$ and $\cot$ have base period $\pi$ (scaled to $\frac{\pi}{b}$).
  • $\sec x$ and $\csc x$ Have No $x$-Intercepts: Since $|\sec bx| \ge 1$ and $|\csc bx| \ge 1$, the graphs of $y = a\sec bx$ and $y = a\csc bx$ ($a \neq 0$) never cross the $x$-axis!
Exam Tip — Sketching $y = a\sec bx$ and $y = a\csc bx$ from Cosine/Sine

To sketch $y = a\sec bx$ (or $y = a\csc bx$), first lightly dash its reciprocal wave $y = a\cos bx$ (or $y = a\sin bx$). Every $x$-intercept of the dashed wave becomes a vertical asymptote, and every peak/trough of the dashed wave is a vertex where the U-shaped branches of $\sec/\csc$ touch and turn away!

Key Takeaways (မှတ်သားရန် အချက်များ)
  • Tangent & Cotangent: Period $= \dfrac{\pi}{b}$, Range $= \mathbb{R}$, both are odd functions.
  • Secant & Cosecant: Period $= \dfrac{2\pi}{b}$, Range $= (-\infty, -a] \cup [a, \infty)$; $\sec$ is even, $\csc$ is odd.
  • Asymptotes (အနန္တကပ်မျဉ်းများ): $\tan bx$ and $\sec bx$ have asymptotes at $x = \pm\frac{\pi}{2b}, \pm\frac{3\pi}{2b},\dots$; $\cot bx$ and $\csc bx$ have asymptotes at $x = 0, \pm\frac{\pi}{b}, \pm\frac{2\pi}{b},\dots$
