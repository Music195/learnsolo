Concept — Periodic Functions, Amplitude, Period & Midline

A function $f$ is called a periodic function (အချိန်မှန်ပြန်ကျော့ဖန်ရှင်) if $f(x) = f(x + p)$ for all $x$ in its domain, where $p > 0$ is a positive real number. The smallest such positive number $p$ is called the period (ကာလ) of $f$. Half the difference between the maximum and minimum values is called the amplitude (လွှဲကျယ်): $\text{Amplitude} = \frac{1}{2}(\max - \min)$.

Starting from the parent curves $y = \sin x$ and $y = \cos x$ (both with domain $\mathbb{R}$, range $[-1, 1]$, period $2\pi$, and amplitude $1$), any transformed curve $y = a\sin b(x - h) + k$ or $y = a\cos b(x - h) + k$ ($b > 0$) is obtained by mapping each point $(x, y)$ of the parent curve via:

$$(x, y) \;\longmapsto\; \left(\frac{x}{b} + h,\; ay + k\right)$$
Core Formulas — Key Properties & Five Key Points ($a > 0, b > 0$)
  • Parameters of $y = \pm a\sin b(x - h) + k$ and $y = \pm a\cos b(x - h) + k$: $$\text{Midline: } y = k, \qquad \text{Amplitude: } a, \qquad \text{Period: } T = \frac{2\pi}{b}, \qquad \text{Range: } [k - a,\; k + a]$$ (If the leading sign is $-a$, the wave is reflected across the midline $y = k$.)
  • Five Key Points over One Period $[h,\; h + \frac{2\pi}{b}]$ (Quarter-Period Step $\Delta x = \frac{\pi}{2b}$):
    • For $y = a\sin b(x - h) + k$: Starts on the midline at $(h, k)$, rises to maximum $\left(h + \frac{\pi}{2b},\, k + a\right)$, crosses midline at $\left(h + \frac{\pi}{b},\, k\right)$, drops to minimum $\left(h + \frac{3\pi}{2b},\, k - a\right)$, and returns to midline at $\left(h + \frac{2\pi}{b},\, k\right)$.
    • For $y = a\cos b(x - h) + k$: Starts at maximum $(h, k + a)$, crosses midline at $\left(h + \frac{\pi}{2b},\, k\right)$, drops to minimum $\left(h + \frac{\pi}{b},\, k - a\right)$, crosses midline at $\left(h + \frac{3\pi}{2b},\, k\right)$, and returns to maximum at $\left(h + \frac{2\pi}{b},\, k + a\right)$.
  • Phase Shift Identity Between Sine and Cosine: $$\sin\!\left(x + \frac{\pi}{2}\right) = \cos x, \qquad \cos(-bx) = \cos(bx) \text{ (even)}, \qquad \sin(-bx) = -\sin(bx) \text{ (odd)}$$
Derivation — Step-by-Step Coordinate Transformation

Consider transforming $y = \sin x$ into $y = a\sin b(x - h) + k$ ($a > 0, b > 0$):

  1. Horizontal scaling by factor $\frac{1}{b}$: $(x, y) \to \left(\frac{x}{b}, y\right)$ compresses/stretches the period from $2\pi$ to $\frac{2\pi}{b}$, giving $y = \sin bx$.
  2. Vertical scaling by factor $a$: $\left(\frac{x}{b}, y\right) \to \left(\frac{x}{b}, ay\right)$ scales the amplitude from $1$ to $a$, giving $y = a\sin bx$.
  3. Horizontal translation by $h$ units and vertical translation by $k$ units: $\left(\frac{x}{b}, ay\right) \to \left(\frac{x}{b} + h, ay + k\right)$ shifts the starting point $(0, 0)$ to $(h, k)$ and the midline $y = 0$ to $y = k$.

Applying $(x, y) \mapsto \left(\frac{x}{b} + h, ay + k\right)$ to the five parent key points $(0,0), (\frac{\pi}{2},1), (\pi,0), (\frac{3\pi}{2},-1), (2\pi,0)$ immediately generates the five key points of any transformed sine curve.

Worked Examples — Textbook Examples 1, 3, 4 & 6

Example 1 (Step-by-Step Scaling of $y = \sin x$): Describe the two transformation pathways from $y = \sin x$ to $y = 2\sin\frac{1}{2}x$, and state its period, amplitude, and five key points.

Show step-by-step solution

Here $a = 2$ and $b = \frac{1}{2}$. Under $(x, y) \mapsto \left(\frac{x}{1/2}, 2y\right) = (2x, 2y)$:

  • Method 1: Vertical scaling by scale factor $a = 2$ gives $y = 2\sin x$, followed by horizontal scaling by scale factor $\frac{1}{b} = 2$ to give $y = 2\sin\frac{1}{2}x$.
  • Method 2: Horizontal scaling by factor $\frac{1}{b} = 2$ gives $y = \sin\frac{1}{2}x$ (period $4\pi$), followed by vertical scaling by factor $a = 2$ to give $y = 2\sin\frac{1}{2}x$.

Properties: Domain $\mathbb{R}$, Range $[-2, 2]$, Amplitude $= 2$, Period $= \dfrac{2\pi}{1/2} = 4\pi$.
Five Key Points: $(0, 0),\; (\pi, 2),\; (2\pi, 0),\; (3\pi, -2),\; (4\pi, 0)$.

Answer: Amplitude $= 2$, Period $= 4\pi$, Five key points: $(0,0), (\pi,2), (2\pi,0), (3\pi,-2), (4\pi,0)$.

Example 3 & 4 (Translated & Reflected Sine Graphs): Find the midline, amplitude, period, and five key points of:
(a) $y = 3\sin 2(x - 1) + 4$   (b) $y = -3\sin\pi(x + 1) - 2$.

Show step-by-step solution

(a) $y = 3\sin 2(x - 1) + 4$: Here $a = 3, b = 2, h = 1, k = 4$:

  • Midline: $y = 4$,   Amplitude: $3$,   Period: $\dfrac{2\pi}{2} = \pi$,   Quarter-step $\Delta x = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$.
  • Five Key Points: $(1, 4),\; \left(\frac{\pi}{4} + 1,\, 7\right),\; \left(\frac{\pi}{2} + 1,\, 4\right),\; \left(\frac{3\pi}{4} + 1,\, 1\right),\; (\pi + 1,\, 4)$.

(b) $y = -3\sin\pi(x + 1) - 2$: Here $-a = -3$ (reflected), $b = \pi, h = -1, k = -2$:

  • Midline: $y = -2$,   Amplitude: $3$,   Period: $\dfrac{2\pi}{\pi} = 2$,   Quarter-step $\Delta x = \dfrac{2}{4} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.
  • Because of the minus sign in front of $3\sin$, the curve goes down to its minimum first ($-2 - 3 = -5$) before rising to its maximum ($-2 + 3 = 1$):
  • Five Key Points: $(-1, -2),\; \left(-\frac{1}{2},\, -5\right),\; (0, -2),\; \left(\frac{1}{2},\, 1\right),\; (1, -2)$.

Answer: (a) Midline $y=4$, Amp $3$, Period $\pi$; (b) Midline $y=-2$, Amp $3$, Period $2$, Key points $(-1,-2), (-\frac{1}{2},-5), (0,-2), (\frac{1}{2},1), (1,-2)$.

Example 6 (Translated & Reflected Cosine Graphs): Find the midline, amplitude, period, and five key points of $y = 2\cos\frac{\pi}{3}(x + 1) + 3$ and $y = -2\cos\frac{\pi}{3}(x + 1) + 3$.

Show step-by-step solution

Here $a = 2, b = \frac{\pi}{3}, h = -1, k = 3$:

  • Midline: $y = 3$,   Amplitude: $2$,   Period: $\dfrac{2\pi}{\pi/3} = 6$,   Quarter-step $\Delta x = \dfrac{6}{4} = 1.5$.
  • The $x$-coordinates of the five key points start at $h = -1$ and increase by $1.5$: $-1,\; 0.5,\; 2,\; 3.5,\; 5$.
  • For $y = 2\cos\frac{\pi}{3}(x + 1) + 3$ (starts at maximum $k + a = 5$): $$(-1, 5),\quad (0.5, 3),\quad (2, 1),\quad (3.5, 3),\quad (5, 5)$$
  • For $y = -2\cos\frac{\pi}{3}(x + 1) + 3$ (starts at minimum $k - a = 1$): $$(-1, 1),\quad (0.5, 3),\quad (2, 5),\quad (3.5, 3),\quad (5, 1)$$

Answer: Midline $y = 3$, Amplitude $2$, Period $6$; Key points $(-1, 5), (0.5, 3), (2, 1), (3.5, 3), (5, 5)$ and $(-1, 1), (0.5, 3), (2, 5), (3.5, 3), (5, 1)$.

Common Mistakes to Avoid
  • Factoring Out $b$ Before Reading $h$: If an equation is given as $y = 2\sin(3x - 6) + 1$, you MUST factor out $b = 3$ first: $y = 2\sin 3(x - 2) + 1$, so the horizontal shift is $h = 2$, NOT $h = 6$!
  • Amplitude Is Always Positive: For $y = -3\sin x$, the amplitude is $|{-3}| = 3$ (never $-3$). The negative sign indicates a reflection across the midline.
Exam Tip — Quarter-Period Arithmetic for the Five Key Points

Once you have $h$ and the period $T = \frac{2\pi}{b}$, divide the period by $4$ to get the quarter-step $\Delta x = \frac{T}{4} = \frac{\pi}{2b}$. Then the five $x$-coordinates are simply $h,\; h + \Delta x,\; h + 2\Delta x,\; h + 3\Delta x,\; h + 4\Delta x$!

Key Takeaways (မှတ်သားရန် အချက်များ)
  • Period & Amplitude (ကာလနှင့် လွှဲကျယ်): For $y = \pm a\sin b(x - h) + k$ and $y = \pm a\cos b(x - h) + k$ ($a, b > 0$), Amplitude $= a$, Period $= \dfrac{2\pi}{b}$, Midline $y = k$.
  • Coordinate Mapping: $(x, y) \mapsto \left(\dfrac{x}{b} + h,\; ay + k\right)$.
  • Symmetry: $y = a\sin bx$ is an odd function ($f(-x) = -f(x)$); $y = a\cos bx$ is an even function ($f(-x) = f(x)$).
