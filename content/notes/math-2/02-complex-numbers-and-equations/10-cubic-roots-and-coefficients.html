Cubic Roots and Coefficients

The same idea as for quadratics, with three roots

A cubic with roots $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ is $a(x - \alpha)(x - \beta)(x - \gamma)$. Expanding and comparing coefficients gives three relations: the sum, the sum of products of pairs, and the product.

Roots $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ of $ax^3 + bx^2 + cx + d = 0$

$$\alpha + \beta + \gamma = -\frac{b}{a}, \qquad \alpha\beta + \beta\gamma + \gamma\alpha = \frac{c}{a}, \qquad \alpha\beta\gamma = -\frac{d}{a}$$

Signs alternate: $-, +, -$. Also $\alpha^2 + \beta^2 + \gamma^2 = (\alpha + \beta + \gamma)^2 - 2(\alpha\beta + \beta\gamma + \gamma\alpha)$.

  1. $(x - \alpha)(x - \beta)(x - \gamma) = x^3 - (\alpha + \beta + \gamma)x^2 + (\alpha\beta + \beta\gamma + \gamma\alpha)x - \alpha\beta\gamma$.
  2. Multiply by $a$ and compare with $ax^3 + bx^2 + cx + d$.

Choose three roots

Example 1

Find a cubic equation with roots $2, -1, 3$.

Show solution
  1. Sum $4$; pairs $-2 - 3 + 6 = 1$; product $-6$.

$x^3 - 4x^2 + x + 6 = 0$

Example 2

$\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ are the roots of $x^3 - 2x^2 + 3x - 4 = 0$. Find $\alpha^2 + \beta^2 + \gamma^2$ and $\dfrac{1}{\alpha} + \dfrac{1}{\beta} + \dfrac{1}{\gamma}$.

Show solution
  1. Sum $2$, pairs $3$, product $4$.
  2. $\alpha^2 + \beta^2 + \gamma^2 = 4 - 6 = -2$.
  3. $\dfrac{1}{\alpha} + \dfrac{1}{\beta} + \dfrac{1}{\gamma} = \dfrac{\beta\gamma + \gamma\alpha + \alpha\beta}{\alpha\beta\gamma} = \dfrac{3}{4}$.

$-2$, $\;\dfrac{3}{4}$

Example 3

$\omega$ is an imaginary root of $x^3 = 1$. Show $\omega^2 + \omega + 1 = 0$ and find $\omega^{100} + \omega^{50} + 1$.

Show solution
  1. $x^3 - 1 = (x - 1)(x^2 + x + 1)$ and $\omega \ne 1$, so $\omega^2 + \omega + 1 = 0$. Also $\omega^3 = 1$.
  2. $100 = 3 \cdot 33 + 1$, $50 = 3 \cdot 16 + 2$: $\omega^{100} = \omega$, $\omega^{50} = \omega^2$.

$\omega + \omega^2 + 1 = 0$

The product is $-\dfrac{d}{a}$ (minus) for a cubic, but $+\dfrac{c}{a}$ for a quadratic. The sign alternates with each step.
For $\omega$ (an imaginary cube root of 1), reduce exponents mod 3 and use $\omega^2 + \omega + 1 = 0$.
  • $\alpha + \beta + \gamma = -\dfrac{b}{a}$, $\alpha\beta + \beta\gamma + \gamma\alpha = \dfrac{c}{a}$, $\alpha\beta\gamma = -\dfrac{d}{a}$.
  • Roots → equation: $x^3 - (\text{sum})x^2 + (\text{pairs})x - (\text{product}) = 0$.
  • Symmetric expressions come from these three values.
