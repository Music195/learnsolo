Tangent Line Formula

Point + slope = line

The tangent at $x = a$ touches the curve at $(a, f(a))$ and has slope $f'(a)$. A point and a slope fix a straight line.

Tangent to $y = f(x)$ at $(a, f(a))$

$$y - f(a) = f'(a)(x - a)$$

The normal (法線) is perpendicular to the tangent: slope $-\dfrac{1}{f'(a)}$ (when $f'(a) \ne 0$).

$y = x^3 - 3x$: drag the point of contact $P$

Example 1

Find the tangent to $y = x^3$ at $x = 2$.

Show solution
  1. Point $(2, 8)$; $y' = 3x^2$, slope $12$.
  2. $y - 8 = 12(x - 2)$

$y = 12x - 16$

Example 2

Find the tangents to $y = x^2 - 2x$ with slope $4$.

Show solution
  1. $y' = 2x - 2 = 4 \Rightarrow x = 3$; point $(3, 3)$.
  2. $y - 3 = 4(x - 3)$

$y = 4x - 9$

Example 3

Find the tangents to $y = x^2$ that pass through $(1, -3)$ (a point not on the curve).

Show solution
  1. Let the point of contact be $(t, t^2)$: tangent $y - t^2 = 2t(x - t)$, i.e. $y = 2tx - t^2$.
  2. It passes through $(1, -3)$: $-3 = 2t - t^2 \Rightarrow t^2 - 2t - 3 = 0 \Rightarrow t = 3, -1$.

$y = 6x - 9$ and $y = -2x - 1$

Example 4

Find the normal to $y = x^2$ at $(1, 1)$.

Show solution
  1. Tangent slope $2$, so normal slope $-\dfrac{1}{2}$.
  2. $y - 1 = -\dfrac{1}{2}(x - 1)$

$y = -\dfrac{1}{2}x + \dfrac{3}{2}$

"Tangent at $P$" means $P$ is the point of contact. "Tangent through $P$" may have $P$ off the curve: then let the contact point be $(t, f(t))$ as in Example 3.
Check your tangent: put $x = a$ into it; you must get $f(a)$.
  • Tangent at $x = a$: $y - f(a) = f'(a)(x - a)$.
  • Given slope $m$: solve $f'(x) = m$ for the contact point.
  • Through an outside point: contact point $(t, f(t))$, then solve for $t$.
