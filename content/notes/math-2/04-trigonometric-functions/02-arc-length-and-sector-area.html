Arc Length and Sector Area

Radians make the formulas simple

A sector with angle $\theta$ is the fraction $\dfrac{\theta}{2\pi}$ of the whole circle. Its arc is that fraction of $2\pi r$, and its area that fraction of $\pi r^2$. With $\theta$ in radians the $\pi$'s cancel.

Radius $r$, central angle $\theta$ (radians)

$$l = r\theta, \qquad S = \frac{1}{2}r^2\theta = \frac{1}{2}lr$$
  1. $l = 2\pi r \times \dfrac{\theta}{2\pi} = r\theta$.
  2. $S = \pi r^2 \times \dfrac{\theta}{2\pi} = \dfrac{1}{2}r^2\theta$, and $\dfrac{1}{2}r^2\theta = \dfrac{1}{2}r(r\theta) = \dfrac{1}{2}lr$.

Change $r$ and $\theta$

Example 1

A sector has radius $12$ and central angle $45°$. Find its arc length and area.

Show solution
  1. $45° = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$. $\;l = 12 \cdot \dfrac{\pi}{4} = 3\pi$.
  2. $S = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 144 \cdot \dfrac{\pi}{4} = 18\pi$.

$l = 3\pi$, $S = 18\pi$

Example 2

A sector has arc length $20$ and radius $8$. Find its area and central angle.

Show solution
  1. $S = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 20 \cdot 8 = 80$. $\;\theta = \dfrac{l}{r} = \dfrac{20}{8}$.

$S = 80$, $\theta = \dfrac{5}{2}$ rad

Example 3

A sector has perimeter $20$. Find the radius that gives the largest area.

Show solution
  1. Perimeter $2r + l = 20$, so $l = 20 - 2r$ ($0 < r < 10$).
  2. $S = \dfrac{1}{2}lr = r(10 - r) = -(r - 5)^2 + 25$.

$r = 5$ (then $l = 10$, $\theta = 2$), largest area $25$

$l = r\theta$ and $S = \dfrac{1}{2}r^2\theta$ need $\theta$ in radians. Convert degrees first.
$S = \dfrac{1}{2}lr$ looks like a triangle's area with base $l$ and height $r$. Use it when the angle isn't given.
