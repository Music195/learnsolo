AM-GM Inequality

Sum fixed → product is largest when equal

For positive $a$ and $b$, the arithmetic mean $\dfrac{a + b}{2}$ is never less than the geometric mean $\sqrt{ab}$.

Its main use: when a product such as $x \cdot \dfrac{9}{x}$ is constant, the sum $x + \dfrac{9}{x}$ has a minimum, reached when the two parts are equal.

For $a > 0$, $b > 0$

$$\frac{a + b}{2} \ge \sqrt{ab} \quad\iff\quad a + b \ge 2\sqrt{ab}$$

Equality only when $a = b$.

  1. $a + b - 2\sqrt{ab} = (\sqrt{a})^2 - 2\sqrt{a}\sqrt{b} + (\sqrt{b})^2 = (\sqrt{a} - \sqrt{b})^2 \ge 0$.
  2. Equality when $\sqrt{a} = \sqrt{b}$, i.e. $a = b$.

Example 1

$x > 0$. Find the minimum of $x + \dfrac{9}{x}$.

Show solution
  1. $x + \dfrac{9}{x} \ge 2\sqrt{x \cdot \dfrac{9}{x}} = 6$.
  2. Equality when $x = \dfrac{9}{x}$, i.e. $x = 3$ (positive), so $6$ is actually reached.

Minimum $6$ (at $x = 3$)

Example 2

$x > 1$. Find the minimum of $x + \dfrac{1}{x - 1}$.

Show solution
  1. Make the product constant: $(x - 1) + \dfrac{1}{x - 1} + 1$, with $x - 1 > 0$.
  2. $\ge 2\sqrt{1} + 1 = 3$; equality when $x - 1 = 1$.

Minimum $3$ (at $x = 2$)

Example 3

$a > 0$, $b > 0$. Find the minimum of $\left(a + \dfrac{1}{b}\right)\left(b + \dfrac{4}{a}\right)$.

Show solution
  1. Expand first: $ab + 4 + 1 + \dfrac{4}{ab} = ab + \dfrac{4}{ab} + 5$.
  2. $ab + \dfrac{4}{ab} \ge 2\sqrt{4} = 4$, equality when $ab = 2$.

Minimum $9$ (when $ab = 2$)

Example 4

A rectangle has perimeter $20$. Find its largest possible area.

Show solution
  1. Sides $x, y > 0$ with $x + y = 10$. AM-GM: $\sqrt{xy} \le 5$, so $xy \le 25$.
  2. Equality when $x = y = 5$.

$25$ (a $5 \times 5$ square)

Check that equality can really happen. In Example 3, using AM-GM on each bracket separately gives $\ge 2\sqrt{\dfrac{a}{b}} \cdot 2\sqrt{\dfrac{4b}{a}} = 8$, but equality would need $ab = 1$ and $ab = 4$ at once. So $8$ is never reached; the true minimum is $9$.
Look for two positive parts whose product is constant, such as $x$ and $\dfrac{k}{x}$. Then their sum is at least $2\sqrt{k}$.
  • $a, b > 0$: $a + b \ge 2\sqrt{ab}$, equality when $a = b$.
  • Product constant → sum has a minimum.
  • Always check the equality case is possible.
