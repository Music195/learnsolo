Logarithm Function Graph

The exponential graph, reflected in $y = x$

$y = \log_a x$ means $x = a^y$: the same relationship as $y = a^x$ with $x$ and $y$ swapped. Swapping $x$ and $y$ reflects a graph in the line $y = x$.

So every feature of $y = a^x$ has a matching one: $(0, 1)$ becomes $(1, 0)$, and the asymptote $y = 0$ becomes $x = 0$.

$y = \log_a x$ ($a > 0$, $a \ne 1$)

$$\text{domain } x > 0, \qquad \text{range: all } y, \qquad \text{passes } (1, 0) \text{ and } (a, 1), \qquad \text{asymptote } x = 0$$

$a > 1$: increasing. $0 < a < 1$: decreasing. $y = \log_a x$ and $y = a^x$ are mirror images in $y = x$ (inverse functions).

Drag $P$ on $y = a^x$; its reflection $Q$ lies on $y = \log_a x$

Example 1

Give the domain, the point on the $x$-axis and the asymptote of $y = \log_3 x$.

Show solution
  1. Antilogarithm positive: $x > 0$. $\log_3 1 = 0$. Asymptote: the $y$-axis.

$x > 0$; $(1, 0)$; $x = 0$

Example 2

Which graph is the mirror image of $y = \log_2 x$ in the line $y = x$, and which is its mirror image in the $x$-axis?

Show solution
  1. In $y = x$: swap $x, y$: $x = \log_2 y \Rightarrow y = 2^x$.
  2. In the $x$-axis: $y \to -y$: $y = -\log_2 x = \log_{\frac{1}{2}} x$.

$y = 2^x$; $\;y = \log_{\frac{1}{2}} x$

Example 3

Describe $y = \log_2 (x - 1) + 2$ and its asymptote.

Show solution
  1. $y = \log_2 x$ moved 1 right and 2 up.
  2. The asymptote $x = 0$ moves to $x = 1$; the point $(1, 0)$ moves to $(2, 2)$.

Asymptote $x = 1$; domain $x > 1$; passes $(2, 2)$

$y = \log_2 x$ is the reflection of $y = 2^x$, not of $y = \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^x$. The bases must match.
To sketch, plot $(1, 0)$ and $(a, 1)$, draw the $y$-axis as the asymptote, then rise ($a > 1$) or fall ($0 < a < 1$).

  • Through $(1, 0)$; domain $x > 0$; asymptote $x = 0$.
