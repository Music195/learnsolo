Parabola: Basic Properties

Same distance from a point and a line

Fix a point F (the focus, 焦点) and a line $\ell$ (the directrix, 準線) that doesn't pass through F. The points P that are exactly as far from F as from $\ell$ form a parabola (放物線).

The vertex lies halfway between the focus and the directrix, and the parabola bends around the focus.

Focus $F(p, 0)$, directrix $x = -p$ ($p \ne 0$)

$$y^2 = 4px$$

Vertex: the origin. Axis: the $x$-axis. Opens right if $p > 0$, left if $p < 0$. The other way round: $x^2 = 4py$ has focus $(0, p)$ and directrix $y = -p$.

  1. Let $P(x, y)$, and let H be the foot of the perpendicular from P to $x = -p$. Then $PF = \sqrt{(x - p)^2 + y^2}$ and $PH = |x + p|$.
  2. $PF = PH$: square both sides, $(x - p)^2 + y^2 = (x + p)^2$.
  3. Expand: $x^2 - 2px + p^2 + y^2 = x^2 + 2px + p^2$, so $y^2 = 4px$.

Drag $P$: the two distances stay equal

Example 1

Find the focus and the directrix of $y^2 = 8x$ and of $x^2 = -2y$.

Show solution
  1. $4p = 8$, so $p = 2$: focus $(2, 0)$, directrix $x = -2$.
  2. $x^2 = 4py$ with $4p = -2$, so $p = -\dfrac{1}{2}$: focus $\left(0, -\dfrac{1}{2}\right)$, directrix $y = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

$(2, 0)$, $x = -2$; $\;\left(0, -\dfrac{1}{2}\right)$, $y = \dfrac{1}{2}$

Example 2

Find the parabola with focus $(0, 3)$ and directrix $y = -3$.

Show solution
  1. The vertex is halfway: the origin. The axis is the $y$-axis with $p = 3$.

$x^2 = 12y$

Example 3

Find the parabola with focus $(3, 2)$ and directrix $x = -1$.

Show solution
  1. Vertex halfway: $(1, 2)$. Distance from vertex to focus: $p = 2$.
  2. Shift $y^2 = 8x$ so its vertex moves to $(1, 2)$.

$(y - 2)^2 = 8(x - 1)$

Example 4

The point P on $y^2 = 8x$ has $x$-coordinate $4$. Find its distance from the focus.

Show solution
  1. No square roots needed: PF equals the distance to the directrix $x = -2$.
  2. $PF = 4 - (-2)$

$6$ (check: $P(4, \pm 4\sqrt{2})$, $\sqrt{2^2 + 32} = 6$)

In $y^2 = 8x$, the focus is at $x = 2$, not $8$: compare with $4p$, so $p$ is a quarter of the coefficient. Also check which variable is squared. $y^2 = \ldots$ opens sideways, and $x^2 = \ldots$ opens up or down.
The distance from a point on the parabola to the focus is hard to compute directly. Replace it with the distance to the directrix, which is just $|x + p|$.
  • Parabola: $PF = PH$ (focus and directrix).
  • $y^2 = 4px$: focus $(p, 0)$, directrix $x = -p$.
  • $x^2 = 4py$: focus $(0, p)$, directrix $y = -p$.
