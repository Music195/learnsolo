Vector Dot Product Properties

Expand like ordinary brackets

The dot product obeys the familiar rules: order doesn't matter, it distributes over $+$, and numbers factor out. So $(\vec{a} + \vec{b}) \cdot (\vec{a} + \vec{b})$ expands just like $(x + y)^2$.

The big use: lengths. Because $|\vec{v}|^2 = \vec{v} \cdot \vec{v}$, you can find $|\vec{a} + \vec{b}|$ or $|2\vec{a} - \vec{b}|$ from $|\vec{a}|$, $|\vec{b}|$ and $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}$ alone, without any components.

Rules ($k$ real)

$$\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = \vec{b} \cdot \vec{a}, \quad \vec{a} \cdot (\vec{b} + \vec{c}) = \vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} + \vec{a} \cdot \vec{c}, \quad (k\vec{a}) \cdot \vec{b} = k(\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b})$$
$$|\vec{a} \pm \vec{b}|^2 = |\vec{a}|^2 \pm 2\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} + |\vec{b}|^2$$

Also $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{a} = |\vec{a}|^2$ and $(\vec{a} + \vec{b}) \cdot (\vec{a} - \vec{b}) = |\vec{a}|^2 - |\vec{b}|^2$.

  1. $\vec{a} \cdot (\vec{b} + \vec{c}) = a_1(b_1 + c_1) + a_2(b_2 + c_2)$.
  2. $= (a_1b_1 + a_2b_2) + (a_1c_1 + a_2c_2) = \vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} + \vec{a} \cdot \vec{c}$.
  3. Then $|\vec{a} + \vec{b}|^2 = (\vec{a} + \vec{b}) \cdot (\vec{a} + \vec{b}) = \vec{a} \cdot \vec{a} + 2\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} + \vec{b} \cdot \vec{b}$.

Drag $\vec{a}$ and $\vec{b}$: check $|\vec{a} + \vec{b}|^2 = |\vec{a}|^2 + 2\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} + |\vec{b}|^2$

Example 1

$|\vec{a}| = 3$, $|\vec{b}| = 2$, $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = -1$. Find $|\vec{a} + \vec{b}|$ and $|2\vec{a} - \vec{b}|$.

Show solution
  1. $|\vec{a} + \vec{b}|^2 = 9 + 2(-1) + 4 = 11$.
  2. $|2\vec{a} - \vec{b}|^2 = 4|\vec{a}|^2 - 4\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} + |\vec{b}|^2 = 36 + 4 + 4 = 44$.

$\sqrt{11}$ and $2\sqrt{11}$

Example 2

$|\vec{a}| = |\vec{b}| = 1$ and $|\vec{a} + \vec{b}| = \sqrt{3}$. Find the angle between $\vec{a}$ and $\vec{b}$.

Show solution
  1. $3 = 1 + 2\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} + 1$, so $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, $\cos\theta = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

$60°$

Example 3

$\vec{a} = (1, 2)$, $\vec{b} = (1, -1)$. Find the real $t$ that makes $|\vec{a} + t\vec{b}|$ smallest, and the smallest value.

Show solution
  1. $|\vec{a} + t\vec{b}|^2 = |\vec{a}|^2 + 2t\,\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} + t^2|\vec{b}|^2 = 5 - 2t + 2t^2$.
  2. $= 2\left(t - \dfrac{1}{2}\right)^2 + \dfrac{9}{2}$.

$t = \dfrac{1}{2}$, minimum $\dfrac{3}{\sqrt{2}} = \dfrac{3\sqrt{2}}{2}$

Example 4

$|\vec{a}| = |\vec{b}|$. Show that $\vec{a} + \vec{b}$ and $\vec{a} - \vec{b}$ are perpendicular (when both are nonzero).

Show solution
  1. $(\vec{a} + \vec{b}) \cdot (\vec{a} - \vec{b}) = |\vec{a}|^2 - \vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} + \vec{b} \cdot \vec{a} - |\vec{b}|^2 = |\vec{a}|^2 - |\vec{b}|^2 = 0$.

The diagonals of a rhombus are perpendicular

You cannot "divide by a vector": $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = \vec{a} \cdot \vec{c}$ does not give $\vec{b} = \vec{c}$. It only says $\vec{a} \perp (\vec{b} - \vec{c})$ (or $\vec{b} = \vec{c}$). Also $|\vec{a} + \vec{b}|^2 \ne |\vec{a}|^2 + |\vec{b}|^2$ unless $\vec{a} \perp \vec{b}$.
Length questions without components: square it, expand, substitute $|\vec{a}|$, $|\vec{b}|$, $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}$, then take the root.
  • Dot products expand like brackets; $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{a} = |\vec{a}|^2$.
  • $|\vec{a} \pm \vec{b}|^2 = |\vec{a}|^2 \pm 2\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} + |\vec{b}|^2$.
  • $|\vec{a} + t\vec{b}|^2$ is a quadratic in $t$: complete the square.
