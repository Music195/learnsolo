Triangle Area with Vectors

$\frac{1}{2}ab\sin C$, written with vectors

The area of triangle OAB is $\dfrac{1}{2}|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\sin\theta$. Since $\sin^2\theta = 1 - \cos^2\theta$ and $\cos\theta$ comes from the dot product, the area can be written with $|\vec{a}|$, $|\vec{b}|$ and $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}$ only.

In components the square root disappears and the area is $\dfrac{1}{2}|a_1b_2 - a_2b_1|$: cross-multiply, subtract, take the absolute value, halve.

Triangle with sides $\overrightarrow{OA} = \vec{a} = (a_1, a_2)$, $\overrightarrow{OB} = \vec{b} = (b_1, b_2)$

$$S = \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{|\vec{a}|^2|\vec{b}|^2 - (\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b})^2} = \frac{1}{2}|a_1b_2 - a_2b_1|$$

For any triangle ABC, use $\vec{a} = \overrightarrow{AB}$ and $\vec{b} = \overrightarrow{AC}$.

  1. $S = \dfrac{1}{2}|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\sin\theta$, and $\sin\theta \ge 0$, so $S = \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{|\vec{a}|^2|\vec{b}|^2(1 - \cos^2\theta)} = \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{|\vec{a}|^2|\vec{b}|^2 - (\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b})^2}$.
  2. In components: $(a_1^2 + a_2^2)(b_1^2 + b_2^2) - (a_1b_1 + a_2b_2)^2 = a_1^2b_2^2 - 2a_1a_2b_1b_2 + a_2^2b_1^2 = (a_1b_2 - a_2b_1)^2$.
  3. So $S = \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{(a_1b_2 - a_2b_1)^2} = \dfrac{1}{2}|a_1b_2 - a_2b_1|$.

Example 1

Find the area of triangle OAB with A$(3, 4)$, B$(6, 1)$.

Show solution
  1. $\dfrac{1}{2}|3 \cdot 1 - 4 \cdot 6| = \dfrac{1}{2}|-21|$.

$\dfrac{21}{2}$

Example 2

Find the area of triangle ABC with A$(1, 1)$, B$(4, 2)$, C$(2, 5)$.

Show solution
  1. $\overrightarrow{AB} = (3, 1)$, $\overrightarrow{AC} = (1, 4)$.
  2. $\dfrac{1}{2}|3 \cdot 4 - 1 \cdot 1|$

$\dfrac{11}{2}$

Example 3

$|\vec{a}| = 2$, $|\vec{b}| = 3$, $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = 3$. Find the area of the triangle formed by $\vec{a}$ and $\vec{b}$.

Show solution
  1. $\dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{4 \cdot 9 - 9} = \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{27}$.

$\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}$

Example 4

Find the area of the parallelogram with sides $\vec{a} = (4, 2)$ and $\vec{b} = (1, 5)$.

Show solution
  1. The parallelogram is two copies of the triangle: area $= |a_1b_2 - a_2b_1| = |20 - 2|$.

$18$

Don't forget the absolute value: $a_1b_2 - a_2b_1$ is negative when $\vec{b}$ is clockwise from $\vec{a}$. Take both vectors from the same vertex, such as $\overrightarrow{AB}$ and $\overrightarrow{AC}$. The angle $\theta$ in $\frac{1}{2}|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\sin\theta$ is the angle at that vertex.
Components known → use $\dfrac{1}{2}|a_1b_2 - a_2b_1|$. Only lengths and a dot product known → use the square-root form.
  • $S = \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{|\vec{a}|^2|\vec{b}|^2 - (\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b})^2}$.
  • $S = \dfrac{1}{2}|a_1b_2 - a_2b_1|$ in components.
  • $S = 0$ exactly when A, B, C are on one line.
