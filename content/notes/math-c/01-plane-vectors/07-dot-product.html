Vector Dot Product

Length × length × cos(angle)

Place $\vec{a}$ and $\vec{b}$ tail to tail and let $\theta$ ($0° \le \theta \le 180°$) be the angle between them. The dot product (内積) is the number $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = |\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\cos\theta$.

A picture: drop a perpendicular from the tip of $\vec{b}$ onto the line of $\vec{a}$. The "shadow" of $\vec{b}$ has signed length $|\vec{b}|\cos\theta$, and $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}$ is $|\vec{a}|$ times that shadow. In components it is simply $a_1b_1 + a_2b_2$.

$\vec{a} = (a_1, a_2)$, $\vec{b} = (b_1, b_2)$, angle $\theta$

$$\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = |\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\cos\theta = a_1b_1 + a_2b_2$$

If $\vec{a} = \vec{0}$ or $\vec{b} = \vec{0}$, we define $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = 0$. Also $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{a} = |\vec{a}|^2$.

  1. Let $\overrightarrow{OA} = \vec{a}$, $\overrightarrow{OB} = \vec{b}$. The cosine rule in triangle OAB: $\text{AB}^2 = |\vec{a}|^2 + |\vec{b}|^2 - 2|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\cos\theta$.
  2. In components: $(b_1 - a_1)^2 + (b_2 - a_2)^2 = (a_1^2 + a_2^2) + (b_1^2 + b_2^2) - 2|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\cos\theta$.
  3. Expanding the left side and cancelling: $-2(a_1b_1 + a_2b_2) = -2|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\cos\theta$.

Drag the tips of $\vec{a}$ (blue) and $\vec{b}$ (orange): the teal bar is the shadow of $\vec{b}$ on $\vec{a}$

Example 1

Find $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}$ for $\vec{a} = (2, 3)$, $\vec{b} = (4, 1)$, and for $\vec{a} = (5, -2)$, $\vec{b} = (3, 7)$.

Show solution
  1. $2 \cdot 4 + 3 \cdot 1 = 11$.
  2. $5 \cdot 3 + (-2) \cdot 7 = 1$.

$11$ and $1$

Example 2

$|\vec{a}| = 2$, $|\vec{b}| = 3$, and the angle between them is $120°$. Find $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}$.

Show solution
  1. $2 \cdot 3 \cdot \cos 120° = 6 \cdot \left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)$.

$-3$

Example 3

ABC is an equilateral triangle with side $2$. Find $\overrightarrow{AB} \cdot \overrightarrow{AC}$ and $\overrightarrow{AB} \cdot \overrightarrow{BC}$.

Show solution
  1. $\overrightarrow{AB}$ and $\overrightarrow{AC}$ start at A, angle $60°$: $2 \cdot 2 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} = 2$.
  2. For $\overrightarrow{AB}$ and $\overrightarrow{BC}$, move them tail to tail: the angle is $180° - 60° = 120°$, so $2 \cdot 2 \cdot \left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)$.

$2$ and $-2$

Example 4

$\vec{u} = (1, 4)$, $\vec{v} = (2, -1)$. Find $(2\vec{u}) \cdot (3\vec{v})$.

Show solution
  1. $\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = 2 - 4 = -2$.
  2. $(2\vec{u}) \cdot (3\vec{v}) = 6(\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v})$.

$-12$

The angle $\theta$ is measured with both vectors starting at the same point. In a triangle, $\overrightarrow{AB}$ and $\overrightarrow{BC}$ are head to tail, so their angle is $180°$ minus the angle B (Example 3).
The sign of $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}$ tells the type of angle: positive → acute, zero → right angle, negative → obtuse. Watch the shadow in the demo flip behind O.

  • $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{a} = |\vec{a}|^2$.
