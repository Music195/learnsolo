Equation of a Sphere

All points at distance $r$ from a centre

A sphere (球面) with centre C$(a, b, c)$ and radius $r$ is the set of points P with CP $= r$. Square the distance formula and you have its equation, just like a circle in the plane with one more term.

A plane such as $z = k$ cuts the sphere in a circle, as long as the plane is close enough to the centre. Pythagoras gives that circle's radius.

Centre $(a, b, c)$, radius $r > 0$

$$(x - a)^2 + (y - b)^2 + (z - c)^2 = r^2$$
$$\text{cut by } z = k \;(|k - c| < r): \text{ circle of radius } \sqrt{r^2 - (k - c)^2}$$

Expanded form: $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 + lx + my + nz + d = 0$. Complete the square in each letter to find the centre and radius.

  1. P$(x, y, z)$ is on the sphere $\iff$ CP $= r$ $\iff$ CP$^2 = r^2$, which is the equation above.
  2. Put $z = k$: $(x - a)^2 + (y - b)^2 = r^2 - (k - c)^2$.
  3. If $r^2 - (k - c)^2 > 0$, this is a circle in the plane $z = k$ with centre $(a, b, k)$ and radius $\sqrt{r^2 - (k - c)^2}$.

A sphere with centre $(0, 0, c)$ cut by the plane $z = k$

Example 1

Find the equation of the sphere with centre $(1, 2, 3)$ passing through the origin.

Show solution
  1. $r^2 = 1^2 + 2^2 + 3^2 = 14$.

$(x - 1)^2 + (y - 2)^2 + (z - 3)^2 = 14$

Example 2

Find the centre and radius of $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 - 6x + 4y - 2z = 11$.

Show solution
  1. $(x - 3)^2 - 9 + (y + 2)^2 - 4 + (z - 1)^2 - 1 = 11$.
  2. $(x - 3)^2 + (y + 2)^2 + (z - 1)^2 = 25$.

Centre $(3, -2, 1)$, radius $5$

Example 3

Find the sphere that has the segment joining A$(1, -1, 2)$ and B$(3, 3, 0)$ as a diameter.

Show solution
  1. Centre = midpoint $(2, 1, 1)$.
  2. $r^2 = (2 - 1)^2 + (1 + 1)^2 + (1 - 2)^2 = 6$.

$(x - 2)^2 + (y - 1)^2 + (z - 1)^2 = 6$

Example 4

The sphere $(x - 1)^2 + (y + 2)^2 + (z - 3)^2 = 25$ meets the $xy$-plane in a circle. Find its centre and radius.

Show solution
  1. Put $z = 0$: $(x - 1)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = 25 - 9 = 16$.

Centre $(1, -2, 0)$, radius $4$

Example 5

A sphere touches all three coordinate planes, has its centre in the region $x, y, z > 0$, and passes through $(2, 1, 1)$. Find its radius.

Show solution
  1. Touching the three planes: centre $(r, r, r)$, radius $r$.
  2. $(2 - r)^2 + (1 - r)^2 + (1 - r)^2 = r^2$, so $2r^2 - 8r + 6 = 0$, $r^2 - 4r + 3 = 0$.

$r = 1$ or $r = 3$

After completing the square, check the right side. If it is $0$, the "sphere" is a single point; if it is negative, there are no points at all. Also, $r^2$ is on the right, not $r$: $(x - 1)^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 9$ has radius $3$.
"The sphere touches the $xy$-plane" means the radius equals the distance from the centre to that plane, which is $|c|$. Example 5 uses this for all three planes.
  • $(x - a)^2 + (y - b)^2 + (z - c)^2 = r^2$.
  • Expanded form → complete the square to get centre and radius.
  • Cut by $z = k$: circle of radius $\sqrt{r^2 - (k - c)^2}$ when $|k - c| < r$.
