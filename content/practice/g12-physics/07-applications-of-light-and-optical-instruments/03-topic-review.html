Applications of Light and Optical Instruments

Topic review: a mix of the whole chapter, best attempted after completing the concept sets. Six exam-style problems across cameras, the eye, microscopes, fibres, spectrometers and photometry. Try each one first. Open the hint if you are stuck, then compare with the full step-by-step solution.

Problem 1

A small lamp of luminous intensity $100\ \mathrm{cd}$ shines equally in all directions. Find its total luminous flux. (You may type an answer such as 400π; type $\Phi$ in lm.)

Uses: Photometry and Illumination

Show hint

$\Phi = I\omega$, and a whole sphere is $4\pi$ steradians.

Show solution
  1. $\Phi = I \times 4\pi = 100 \times 4\pi = 400\pi \approx 1257\ \mathrm{lm}$.

$\Phi = 400\pi \approx 1257\ \mathrm{lm}$

Problem 2

A $24\ \mathrm{m}$ tall tree, $30\ \mathrm{m}$ away, is photographed with a $50\ \mathrm{mm}$ lens. How tall is its image on the sensor? (Type the height in mm, to 3 significant figures.)

Uses: The Camera

Show hint

Find $v$, then $h_i = h_o\dfrac{v}{u}$ (size only).

Show solution
  1. $\dfrac1v = \dfrac{1}{0.050} - \dfrac{1}{30} = 20 - 0.0333 = 19.967\ \mathrm{m^{-1}} \implies v \approx 0.05008\ \mathrm{m}$.
  2. $h_i = 24 \times \dfrac{0.05008}{30} \approx 0.0401\ \mathrm{m} = 40.1\ \mathrm{mm}$ (inverted).

$h_i \approx 40.1\ \mathrm{mm}$

Problem 3

A spectrometer with a grating of $600$ lines per mm is used with sodium light ($\lambda = 589\ \mathrm{nm}$). At what angle is the first-order image seen? (Type $\theta$ in degrees, to 3 significant figures.)

Uses: The Optical Spectrometer

Show hint

$d = \dfrac{1}{600}\ \mathrm{mm}$ and $d\sin\theta = m\lambda$.

Show solution
  1. $d = \dfrac{10^{-3}}{600} = 1.667 \times 10^{-6}\ \mathrm{m}$.
  2. $\sin\theta = \dfrac{589 \times 10^{-9}}{1.667 \times 10^{-6}} \approx 0.3534 \implies \theta \approx 20.7^\circ$.

$\theta \approx 20.7^\circ$

Problem 4

The lens-to-retina distance in an eye is $2.0\ \mathrm{cm}$. What is the power of the eye lens when it focuses on a page $50\ \mathrm{cm}$ away? (Type $P$ in dioptres.)

Uses: The Human Eye and Vision Defects

Show hint

$P = \dfrac1u + \dfrac1v$ in metres; the image on the retina is real.

Show solution
  1. $P = \dfrac{1}{0.50} + \dfrac{1}{0.020} = 2.0 + 50 = 52\ \mathrm{D}$.

$P = +52\ \mathrm{D}$

Problem 5

A compound microscope has $f_o = 0.50\ \mathrm{cm}$, $f_e = 2.0\ \mathrm{cm}$ and a tube length of $16\ \mathrm{cm}$. Estimate its magnifying power with the final image at $D = 25\ \mathrm{cm}$. (Type $M$ with its sign.)

Uses: The Compound Microscope

Show hint

Use the approximation $M \approx -\dfrac{L}{f_o}\cdot\dfrac{D}{f_e}$.

Show solution
  1. $M \approx -\dfrac{16}{0.50} \times \dfrac{25}{2.0} = -32 \times 12.5 = -400$.

$M \approx -400$

Problem 6

An endoscope fibre has core index $1.62$ and cladding index $1.52$. Find (i) the critical angle inside the fibre and (ii) the largest angle to the axis at which light from air can enter and still be guided. (Type the answer to (ii) in degrees, to 3 significant figures.)

Uses: Fibre Optics and Endoscopy

Show hint

(i) $\sin\theta_c = n_2/n_1$. (ii) $\sin\alpha_{\max} = \sqrt{n_1^2 - n_2^2}$.

Show solution
  1. (i) $\sin\theta_c = \dfrac{1.52}{1.62} \approx 0.9383 \implies \theta_c \approx 69.8^\circ$.
  2. (ii) $\sqrt{1.62^2 - 1.52^2} = \sqrt{2.6244 - 2.3104} = \sqrt{0.3140} \approx 0.560$, so $\alpha_{\max} \approx 34.1^\circ$.

(i) $\theta_c \approx 69.8^\circ$   (ii) $\alpha_{\max} \approx 34.1^\circ$
