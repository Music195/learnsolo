Electromagnetic Induction, Generation and Distribution

Topic review: a mix of the whole chapter, best attempted after completing the concept sets. Six exam-style problems on AC, induction, generators, transformers and power transmission. Try each one first. Open the hint if you are stuck, then compare with the full step-by-step solution.

Problem 1

A transformer at a power station steps $11\ \mathrm{kV}$ up to $132\ \mathrm{kV}$. What is the turns ratio $N_s : N_p$? (Type $N_s/N_p$.)

Uses: Applications: Induction Coil, AC Generator, and Transformers, Power Transmission and the Grid System

Show hint

$\dfrac{N_s}{N_p} = \dfrac{V_s}{V_p}$.

Show solution
  1. $\dfrac{N_s}{N_p} = \dfrac{132}{11} = 12$.

$12 : 1$

Problem 2

A $50$-turn coil of area $4.0\ \mathrm{cm^2}$ lies perpendicular to a magnetic field that rises steadily from $0.10\ \mathrm{T}$ to $0.50\ \mathrm{T}$ in $0.020\ \mathrm{s}$. Find the induced emf. (Type $E$ in V.)

Uses: Faraday's Law and Lenz's Law, Electromagnetic Induction and Magnetic Flux

Show hint

$\Delta\Phi = A\,\Delta B$ with $A$ in $\mathrm{m^2}$.

Show solution
  1. $\Delta\Phi = 4.0 \times 10^{-4} \times (0.50 - 0.10) = 1.6 \times 10^{-4}\ \mathrm{Wb}$.
  2. $|E| = 50 \times \dfrac{1.6 \times 10^{-4}}{0.020} = 0.40\ \mathrm{V}$.

$E = 0.40\ \mathrm{V}$

Problem 3

A generator coil of area $0.020\ \mathrm{m^2}$ turns at $50\ \mathrm{Hz}$ in a $0.30\ \mathrm{T}$ field. What is the smallest whole number of turns that gives a peak emf of at least $240\ \mathrm{V}$? (Type $N$.)

Uses: Applications: Induction Coil, AC Generator, and Transformers

Show hint

$N = \dfrac{E_0}{2\pi f BA}$, then round up.

Show solution
  1. $2\pi f BA = 2\pi \times 50 \times 0.30 \times 0.020 \approx 1.885\ \mathrm{V}$ per turn.
  2. $N = \dfrac{240}{1.885} \approx 127.3$, so at least $128$ turns.

$N = 128$ turns

Problem 4

A town takes $2.0\ \mathrm{MW}$ through lines of total resistance $10\ \Omega$ at $20\ \mathrm{kV}$. What percentage of the power is lost in the lines? (Type the loss in %.)

Uses: Power Transmission and the Grid System

Show hint

$I = P/V$, $P_{\text{loss}} = I^2R$, then divide by $P$.

Show solution
  1. $I = \dfrac{2.0 \times 10^6}{20 \times 10^3} = 100\ \mathrm{A}$.
  2. $P_{\text{loss}} = 100^2 \times 10 = 1.0 \times 10^5\ \mathrm{W}$.
  3. $\dfrac{1.0 \times 10^5}{2.0 \times 10^6} \times 100\,\% = 5.0\,\%$.

$5.0\,\%$

Problem 5

A transformer that is $95\,\%$ efficient takes power from the $220\ \mathrm{V}$ mains and supplies $1.0\ \mathrm{kW}$ at $22\ \mathrm{V}$. Find the secondary current and the primary current. (Type $I_p$ in A, to 3 significant figures.)

Uses: Applications: Induction Coil, AC Generator, and Transformers

Show hint

$I_s = P_{\text{out}}/V_s$; $P_{\text{in}} = P_{\text{out}}/\eta$; $I_p = P_{\text{in}}/V_p$.

Show solution
  1. $I_s = \dfrac{1000}{22} \approx 45.5\ \mathrm{A}$.
  2. $P_{\text{in}} = \dfrac{1000}{0.95} \approx 1053\ \mathrm{W}$, so $I_p = \dfrac{1053}{220} \approx 4.78\ \mathrm{A}$.

$I_s \approx 45.5\ \mathrm{A}$, $I_p \approx 4.78\ \mathrm{A}$

Problem 6

The supply $E = 311\sin(100\pi t)$ volts is connected across a $50\ \Omega$ resistor. Find the peak current, and the first time after $t = 0$ at which the voltage reaches half its peak value. (Type that time in ms, to 3 significant figures.)

Uses: Alternating Current and AC Waveforms

Show hint

$I_0 = E_0/R$. Half the peak means $\sin(100\pi t) = \tfrac12$, first at $100\pi t = \pi/6$.

Show solution
  1. $I_0 = \dfrac{311}{50} \approx 6.22\ \mathrm{A}$.
  2. $100\pi t = \dfrac{\pi}{6} \implies t = \dfrac{1}{600}\ \mathrm{s} \approx 1.67\ \mathrm{ms}$.

$I_0 \approx 6.22\ \mathrm{A}$; $t = 1/600\ \mathrm{s} \approx 1.67\ \mathrm{ms}$
