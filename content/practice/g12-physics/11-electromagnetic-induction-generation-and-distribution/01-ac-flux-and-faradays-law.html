AC Waveforms, Magnetic Flux and Faraday's Law

Six problems on $E = E_0\sin\omega t$, magnetic flux $\Phi = BA\cos\theta$, Faraday's law $E = -N\dfrac{\Delta\Phi}{\Delta t}$ and Lenz's law. Try each one first. Open the hint if you are stuck, then compare with the full step-by-step solution.

Problem 1

The mains supply has frequency $50\ \mathrm{Hz}$ and peak voltage $311\ \mathrm{V}$. Find its angular frequency and write the equation for the voltage. (Type $\omega$ in $\mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}$; you may type 100π.)

Uses: Alternating Current and AC Waveforms

Show hint

$\omega = 2\pi f$.

Show solution
  1. $\omega = 2\pi \times 50 = 100\pi \approx 314\ \mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}$.
  2. $E(t) = 311\sin(100\pi t)$ volts. The period is $T = 1/50 = 0.020\ \mathrm{s}$.

$\omega = 100\pi\ \mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}$; $E = 311\sin(100\pi t)$

Problem 2

A coil of area $0.050\ \mathrm{m^2}$ is in a uniform field of $0.40\ \mathrm{T}$. The normal to the coil makes $60^\circ$ with the field. Find the flux through it. (Type $\Phi$ in mWb.)

Uses: Electromagnetic Induction and Magnetic Flux

Show hint

$\Phi = BA\cos\theta$, with $\theta$ between $B$ and the normal.

Show solution
  1. $\Phi = 0.40 \times 0.050 \times \cos 60^\circ = 0.020 \times 0.50 = 0.010\ \mathrm{Wb} = 10\ \mathrm{mWb}$.

$\Phi = 10\ \mathrm{mWb}$

Problem 3

The flux through a $500$-turn coil falls steadily from $2.0\ \mathrm{mWb}$ to zero in $0.10\ \mathrm{s}$. Find the size of the induced emf. (Type $E$ in V.)

Uses: Faraday's Law and Lenz's Law

Show hint

$|E| = N\dfrac{|\Delta\Phi|}{\Delta t}$.

Show solution
  1. $|E| = 500 \times \dfrac{2.0 \times 10^{-3}}{0.10} = 500 \times 0.020 = 10\ \mathrm{V}$.

$E = 10\ \mathrm{V}$

Problem 4

An AC voltage is $E = 150\sin(377t)$ volts. Find its frequency, its period and its value at $t = 2.0\ \mathrm{ms}$. (Type $E$ at $2.0$ ms in V, to the nearest volt.)

Uses: Alternating Current and AC Waveforms

Show hint

$\omega = 2\pi f$. Work out $377t$ in radians before taking the sine.

Show solution
  1. $f = \dfrac{377}{2\pi} \approx 60\ \mathrm{Hz}$ and $T = \dfrac{1}{60} \approx 16.7\ \mathrm{ms}$.
  2. $377 \times 2.0 \times 10^{-3} = 0.754\ \mathrm{rad}$, so $E = 150\sin(0.754) = 150 \times 0.685 \approx 103\ \mathrm{V}$.

$f \approx 60\ \mathrm{Hz}$, $T \approx 16.7\ \mathrm{ms}$, $E \approx 103\ \mathrm{V}$

Problem 5

The north pole of a magnet is pushed into a $100$-turn coil connected to a resistance of $5.0\ \Omega$ (total). The flux through the coil rises by $0.020\ \mathrm{Wb}$. Find the charge that flows, and state which pole appears at the end of the coil facing the magnet. (Type $q$ in C.)

Uses: Faraday's Law and Lenz's Law

Show hint

$q = \dfrac{N|\Delta\Phi|}{R}$; it does not depend on how fast the magnet moves. Lenz: the induced current opposes the change.

Show solution
  1. $q = \dfrac{100 \times 0.020}{5.0} = 0.40\ \mathrm{C}$.
  2. The current opposes the approaching north pole, so that end of the coil becomes a north pole and repels the magnet. Work must be done to push the magnet in; that work becomes electrical energy.

$q = 0.40\ \mathrm{C}$; a north pole faces the magnet

Problem 6

A $200$-turn coil of area $0.010\ \mathrm{m^2}$ is at first perpendicular to a $0.50\ \mathrm{T}$ field. It is turned through $90^\circ$ in $0.050\ \mathrm{s}$ so that its plane lies along the field. Find the average emf and the average current if the coil circuit has resistance $10\ \Omega$. (Type $E$ in V.)

Uses: Faraday's Law and Lenz's Law, Electromagnetic Induction and Magnetic Flux

Show hint

At first $\Phi = BA$ (plane perpendicular to $B$, normal along $B$); at the end $\Phi = 0$.

Show solution
  1. $\Delta\Phi = 0 - 0.50 \times 0.010 = -5.0 \times 10^{-3}\ \mathrm{Wb}$.
  2. $|E| = 200 \times \dfrac{5.0 \times 10^{-3}}{0.050} = 20\ \mathrm{V}$.
  3. $I = \dfrac{20}{10} = 2.0\ \mathrm{A}$ on average.

$E = 20\ \mathrm{V}$, $I = 2.0\ \mathrm{A}$
