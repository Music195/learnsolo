Effects and Characteristics of Sound

Topic review: a mix of the whole chapter, best attempted after completing the concept sets. Six exam-style problems on the speed of sound, echoes, ultrasound, the Doppler effect and loudness. Try each one first. Open the hint if you are stuck, then compare with the full step-by-step solution.

Problem 1

The ear keeps a sound for about $0.1\ \mathrm{s}$, so an echo is heard separately only if it returns at least $0.1\ \mathrm{s}$ after the sound. What is the least distance to a reflecting wall for a clear echo? ($v = 340\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$; type $d$ in m.)

Uses: Echo, Reverberation, and Echolocation

Show hint

$d = \dfrac{vt}{2}$ with $t = 0.1\ \mathrm{s}$.

Show solution
  1. $d = \dfrac{340 \times 0.1}{2} = 17\ \mathrm{m}$. Nearer walls give reverberation instead: the reflections blend with the sound.

$d = 17\ \mathrm{m}$

Problem 2

An ultrasound pulse sent into the body returns from the edge of an organ $60\ \mu\mathrm{s}$ after it was sent. Sound travels at $1540\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ in tissue. How deep is the edge? (Type the depth in cm, to 3 significant figures.)

Uses: Range of Audibility, Infrasound, and Ultrasound, Echo, Reverberation, and Echolocation

Show hint

This is an echo: $d = vt/2$, with $1\ \mu\mathrm{s} = 10^{-6}\ \mathrm{s}$.

Show solution
  1. $d = \dfrac{1540 \times 60 \times 10^{-6}}{2} = 0.0462\ \mathrm{m} = 4.62\ \mathrm{cm}$.

$d \approx 4.62\ \mathrm{cm}$

Problem 3

A lightning flash is seen and the thunder is heard $4.0\ \mathrm{s}$ later on a day when the air is at $35^\circ\mathrm{C}$. How far away was the lightning? (Speed of sound $331\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ at $0^\circ\mathrm{C}$; type the distance in km, to 3 significant figures.)

Uses: Propagation and Speed of Sound

Show hint

Light arrives almost at once. Correct the speed of sound with $v \propto \sqrt{T}$.

Show solution
  1. $v = 331\sqrt{\dfrac{308}{273}} \approx 351.6\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$.
  2. $d = vt \approx 351.6 \times 4.0 \approx 1406\ \mathrm{m} \approx 1.41\ \mathrm{km}$.

$d \approx 1.41\ \mathrm{km}$

Problem 4

One machine in a workshop gives a sound intensity level of $80\ \mathrm{dB}$ at a worker's position. What is the level when ten identical machines run together? (Type $\beta$ in dB.)

Uses: Characteristics of Musical Sound

Show hint

Intensities add, not decibels. Ten machines give ten times the intensity.

Show solution
  1. $\Delta\beta = 10\log_{10} 10 = 10\ \mathrm{dB}$.
  2. $\beta = 80 + 10 = 90\ \mathrm{dB}$.

$\beta = 90\ \mathrm{dB}$

Problem 5

A police car's siren has frequency $1000\ \mathrm{Hz}$. A person standing still hears $1100\ \mathrm{Hz}$ as the car approaches. How fast is the car moving? ($v = 340\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$; type $v_s$ in $\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$, to 3 significant figures.)

Uses: The Doppler Effect

Show hint

Solve $f_o = f_s\dfrac{v}{v - v_s}$ for $v_s$.

Show solution
  1. $1100 = 1000 \times \dfrac{340}{340 - v_s} \implies 340 - v_s = \dfrac{340 \times 1000}{1100} \approx 309.09$.
  2. $v_s \approx 340 - 309.09 \approx 30.9\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ (about $111\ \mathrm{km\,h^{-1}}$).

$v_s \approx 30.9\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$

Problem 6

A $500\ \mathrm{Hz}$ sound wave in air ($\rho = 1.29\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$, $v = 343\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$) has a displacement amplitude of $1.0 \times 10^{-8}\ \mathrm{m}$. Find its intensity and intensity level. ($I_0 = 10^{-12}\ \mathrm{W\,m^{-2}}$; type $\beta$ in dB, to 3 significant figures.)

Uses: Characteristics of Musical Sound

Show hint

$I = 2\pi^2\rho v f^2 A^2$, then $\beta = 10\log_{10}(I/I_0)$.

Show solution
  1. $I = 2\pi^2 \times 1.29 \times 343 \times 500^2 \times (1.0 \times 10^{-8})^2 \approx 2.18 \times 10^{-7}\ \mathrm{W\,m^{-2}}$.
  2. $\beta = 10\log_{10}\dfrac{2.18 \times 10^{-7}}{10^{-12}} \approx 53.4\ \mathrm{dB}$, about the level of ordinary conversation.

$I \approx 2.18 \times 10^{-7}\ \mathrm{W\,m^{-2}}$, $\beta \approx 53.4\ \mathrm{dB}$
