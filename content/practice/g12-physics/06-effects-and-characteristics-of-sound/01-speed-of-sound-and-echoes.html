Speed of Sound and Echoes

Six problems on the speed of sound in solids, liquids and gases, its change with temperature, echoes, sonar and echolocation. Try each one first. Open the hint if you are stuck, then compare with the full step-by-step solution.

Problem 1

Find the speed of sound in aluminium ($Y = 7.0 \times 10^{10}\ \mathrm{N\,m^{-2}}$, $\rho = 2700\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$). (Type $v$ in $\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$, to 3 significant figures.)

Uses: Propagation and Speed of Sound

Show hint

In a solid $v = \sqrt{Y/\rho}$.

Show solution
  1. $v = \sqrt{\dfrac{7.0 \times 10^{10}}{2700}} = \sqrt{2.593 \times 10^{7}} \approx 5092\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}} \approx 5.09\ \mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$, about 15 times faster than in air.

$v \approx 5.09\ \mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$

Problem 2

A girl shouts towards a cliff and hears the echo $1.5\ \mathrm{s}$ later. The speed of sound is $340\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. How far away is the cliff? (Type $d$ in m.)

Uses: Echo, Reverberation, and Echolocation

Show hint

The sound goes to the cliff and back: $2d = vt$.

Show solution
  1. $d = \dfrac{vt}{2} = \dfrac{340 \times 1.5}{2} = 255\ \mathrm{m}$.

$d = 255\ \mathrm{m}$

Problem 3

The speed of sound in air is $331\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ at $0^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Find it at $27^\circ\mathrm{C}$. (Type $v$ in $\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$, to the nearest whole number.)

Uses: Propagation and Speed of Sound

Show hint

$v = \sqrt{\gamma RT/M}$, so $v \propto \sqrt{T}$ with $T$ in kelvin.

Show solution
  1. $\dfrac{v_2}{v_1} = \sqrt{\dfrac{T_2}{T_1}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{300}{273}} = 1.048$.
  2. $v_2 = 331 \times 1.048 \approx 347\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$.

$v \approx 347\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$

Problem 4

Find the speed of sound in helium at a pressure of $1.01 \times 10^5\ \mathrm{Pa}$, where its density is $0.179\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$ and $\gamma = 1.67$. Why does a person who breathes helium speak in a high voice? (Type $v$ in $\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$, to the nearest whole number.)

Uses: Propagation and Speed of Sound

Show hint

$v = \sqrt{\dfrac{\gamma p}{\rho}}$.

Show solution
  1. $v = \sqrt{\dfrac{1.67 \times 1.01 \times 10^5}{0.179}} = \sqrt{9.423 \times 10^{5}} \approx 971\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$.
  2. The throat sets the wavelengths of the voice; with nearly three times the speed, $f = v/\lambda$ is nearly three times higher for those resonances.

$v \approx 971\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$

Problem 5

A ship's sonar sends a pulse to the sea bed and receives the echo $0.80\ \mathrm{s}$ later. Sound travels at $1500\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ in sea water. How deep is the sea? (Type the depth in m.)

Uses: Echo, Reverberation, and Echolocation

Show hint

Echo: $d = \dfrac{vt}{2}$.

Show solution
  1. $d = \dfrac{1500 \times 0.80}{2} = 600\ \mathrm{m}$.

$d = 600\ \mathrm{m}$

Problem 6

A bat flying at $10\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ straight towards a wall gives a click and hears its echo $0.20\ \mathrm{s}$ later. How far from the wall was it when it clicked? (Speed of sound $340\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$; type $d_0$ in m.)

Uses: Echo, Reverberation, and Echolocation

Show hint

The sound and the bat together cover twice the starting distance: $v_s t + v_b t = 2d_0$.

Show solution
  1. Sound path: $340 \times 0.20 = 68\ \mathrm{m}$; bat moves $10 \times 0.20 = 2.0\ \mathrm{m}$.
  2. $2d_0 = 68 + 2.0 = 70 \implies d_0 = 35\ \mathrm{m}$.
  3. Check: sound goes $35$ m to the wall and $33$ m back to the bat, now at $35 - 2 = 33$ m. ✓

$d_0 = 35\ \mathrm{m}$
