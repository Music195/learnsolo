Problem 1

What is the output of an XOR gate with inputs $A = 1$, $B = 1$? And with $A = 1$, $B = 0$? (Type the output for $A = B = 1$.)

Uses: Digital Electronics and Logic Gates

Show hint

XOR gives $1$ only when the inputs are different.

Show solution
  1. $A = B = 1$: the inputs are the same, so $Y = 0$.
  2. $A = 1$, $B = 0$: different, so $Y = 1$.

$0$ (and $1$ for $A = 1$, $B = 0$)

Problem 2

A $5.1\ \mathrm{V}$ Zener diode with a series resistor must supply a load taking $30\ \mathrm{mA}$ from a $9.0\ \mathrm{V}$ input, with $10\ \mathrm{mA}$ through the Zener. What series resistance is needed? (Type $R_S$ in $\Omega$, to 3 significant figures.)

Uses: Electronic Systems and Sensor Components

Show hint

$R_S = \dfrac{V_{\text{in}} - V_Z}{I_Z + I_L}$.

Show solution
  1. $R_S = \dfrac{9.0 - 5.1}{0.010 + 0.030} = \dfrac{3.9}{0.040} = 97.5\ \Omega$ (a $100\ \Omega$ resistor would be used).

$R_S = 97.5\ \Omega$

Problem 3

An LDR ($R_1$) and a $5.0\ \mathrm{k\Omega}$ resistor ($R_2$) form a divider across $6.0\ \mathrm{V}$, with $V_{\text{out}}$ across $R_2$. In bright light $V_{\text{out}} = 5.0\ \mathrm{V}$. What is the LDR resistance then? (Type $R_1$ in $\mathrm{k\Omega}$.)

Uses: Electronic Systems and Sensor Components

Show hint

Rearrange $V_{\text{out}} = V_{\text{in}}\dfrac{R_2}{R_1 + R_2}$: $R_1 + R_2 = R_2\dfrac{V_{\text{in}}}{V_{\text{out}}}$.

Show solution
  1. $R_1 + 5.0 = 5.0 \times \dfrac{6.0}{5.0} = 6.0\ \mathrm{k\Omega}$.
  2. $R_1 = 1.0\ \mathrm{k\Omega}$.

$R_1 = 1.0\ \mathrm{k\Omega}$

Problem 4

Explain the difference between amplitude modulation (AM) and frequency modulation (FM). Why is a high-frequency carrier wave needed at all?

Uses: Communication Systems and Modulation

Show hint

Think about what changes in the carrier, and about aerial size and how many stations can share the air.

Show solution
  1. AM: the amplitude of the carrier changes with the audio signal; the frequency stays fixed. FM: the frequency of the carrier changes with the signal; the amplitude stays fixed, so FM is less affected by electrical noise.
  2. Audio waves ($20$ Hz–$20$ kHz) would need aerials kilometres long and could not be separated from each other. A high-frequency carrier needs a short aerial, travels well, and lets each station use its own frequency.

AM varies amplitude, FM varies frequency; the carrier makes aerials practical and separates stations

Problem 5

An FM receiver uses a $0.10\ \mu\mathrm{H}$ coil. (i) What capacitance tunes it to $100\ \mathrm{MHz}$? (ii) To what frequency is it tuned with $25\ \mathrm{pF}$? (Type the answer to (ii) in MHz, to the nearest MHz.)

Uses: Communication Systems and Modulation

Show hint

$C = \dfrac{1}{4\pi^2 f^2 L}$ for (i); $f_0 = \dfrac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{LC}}$ for (ii).

Show solution
  1. (i) $C = \dfrac{1}{4\pi^2 (10^8)^2 (0.10 \times 10^{-6})} \approx 2.53 \times 10^{-11}\ \mathrm{F} \approx 25.3\ \mathrm{pF}$.
  2. (ii) $f_0 = \dfrac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{0.10 \times 10^{-6} \times 25 \times 10^{-12}}} \approx 101\ \mathrm{MHz}$.

(i) $\approx 25.3\ \mathrm{pF}$   (ii) $\approx 101\ \mathrm{MHz}$

Problem 6

A greenhouse fan must run ($Y = 1$) when it is hot ($H = 1$) and it is not night ($N = 0$), or whenever the manual switch is on ($M = 1$). Write $Y$, and find how many of the $8$ input combinations turn the fan on. (Type the number.)

Uses: Digital Electronics and Logic Gates

Show hint

"Not night" is $\overline{N}$. Join the two conditions with OR.

Show solution
  1. $Y = H \cdot \overline{N} + M$.
  2. $M = 1$: $Y = 1$ for all four combinations of $H$ and $N$.
  3. $M = 0$: $Y = H \cdot \overline{N}$, which is $1$ only for $H = 1$, $N = 0$.
  4. Total: $4 + 1 = 5$ combinations.

$Y = H\overline{N} + M$; on for $5$ of the $8$ combinations
