Wavefronts and Young's Double-Slit Experiment

Six problems on path and phase difference $\Delta\phi = \dfrac{2\pi}{\lambda}\Delta x$, bright and dark fringes and the fringe spacing $\Delta y = \dfrac{\lambda D}{d}$. Try each one first. Open the hint if you are stuck, then compare with the full step-by-step solution.

Problem 1

Two coherent waves reach a point with a path difference of $2.5\lambda$. Find the phase difference. Is the point bright or dark? (Type $\Delta\phi$ in rad; you may type 5π.)

Uses: Wavefronts and Huygens' Principle

Show hint

$\Delta\phi = \dfrac{2\pi}{\lambda}\Delta x$.

Show solution
  1. $\Delta\phi = \dfrac{2\pi}{\lambda} \times 2.5\lambda = 5\pi\ \mathrm{rad}$.
  2. $5\pi$ is an odd multiple of $\pi$: the waves arrive out of phase, so the point is dark.

$\Delta\phi = 5\pi$ rad: dark (destructive)

Problem 2

In a Young's experiment $\lambda = 600\ \mathrm{nm}$, the slits are $0.30\ \mathrm{mm}$ apart and the screen is $1.5\ \mathrm{m}$ away. Find the fringe spacing. (Type $\Delta y$ in mm.)

Uses: Interference of Light and Young's Double-Slit Experiment

Show hint

$\Delta y = \dfrac{\lambda D}{d}$, all in metres.

Show solution
  1. $\Delta y = \dfrac{600 \times 10^{-9} \times 1.5}{0.30 \times 10^{-3}} = 3.0 \times 10^{-3}\ \mathrm{m} = 3.0\ \mathrm{mm}$.

$\Delta y = 3.0\ \mathrm{mm}$

Problem 3

Bright fringes $2.2\ \mathrm{mm}$ apart are seen on a screen $1.0\ \mathrm{m}$ from two slits $0.25\ \mathrm{mm}$ apart. Find the wavelength of the light. (Type $\lambda$ in nm.)

Uses: Interference of Light and Young's Double-Slit Experiment

Show hint

Rearrange $\Delta y = \dfrac{\lambda D}{d}$.

Show solution
  1. $\lambda = \dfrac{\Delta y\,d}{D} = \dfrac{2.2 \times 10^{-3} \times 0.25 \times 10^{-3}}{1.0} = 5.5 \times 10^{-7}\ \mathrm{m} = 550\ \mathrm{nm}$ (green).

$\lambda = 550\ \mathrm{nm}$

Problem 4

With $\lambda = 600\ \mathrm{nm}$, $d = 0.30\ \mathrm{mm}$ and $D = 1.5\ \mathrm{m}$, how far from the central bright fringe is the third dark fringe? (Type $y$ in mm.)

Uses: Interference of Light and Young's Double-Slit Experiment

Show hint

Dark fringes: $y = \left(m + \tfrac12\right)\dfrac{\lambda D}{d}$, and the third dark fringe has $m = 2$.

Show solution
  1. $\dfrac{\lambda D}{d} = 3.0\ \mathrm{mm}$ (fringe spacing).
  2. $y = \left(2 + \tfrac12\right) \times 3.0 = 7.5\ \mathrm{mm}$.

$y = 7.5\ \mathrm{mm}$

Problem 5

At a point on the screen of a double-slit experiment the path difference from the two slits is $1.8\ \mu\mathrm{m}$ and the light has $\lambda = 600\ \mathrm{nm}$. Is the point bright or dark? What is the order $m$ of the fringe? (Type $m$.)

Uses: Interference of Light and Young's Double-Slit Experiment, Wavefronts and Huygens' Principle

Show hint

Divide the path difference by $\lambda$: a whole number means bright, a half-number means dark.

Show solution
  1. $\dfrac{\Delta r}{\lambda} = \dfrac{1.8 \times 10^{-6}}{600 \times 10^{-9}} = 3.0$.
  2. A whole number of wavelengths: the waves arrive in phase, a bright fringe of order $m = 3$.

Bright, the third-order fringe ($m = 3$)

Problem 6

The Young's apparatus of Problem 2 (spacing $3.0\ \mathrm{mm}$ in air) is put under water, refractive index $1.33$. What is the new fringe spacing? (Type $\Delta y$ in mm, to 3 significant figures.)

Uses: Interference of Light and Young's Double-Slit Experiment, Wavefronts and Huygens' Principle

Show hint

In a medium of index $n$ the wavelength becomes $\lambda/n$, and $\Delta y \propto \lambda$.

Show solution
  1. $\lambda_{\text{water}} = \dfrac{600}{1.33} \approx 451\ \mathrm{nm}$.
  2. $\Delta y = \dfrac{3.0}{1.33} \approx 2.26\ \mathrm{mm}$: the fringes move closer together.

$\Delta y \approx 2.26\ \mathrm{mm}$
