Flow, Continuity and Bernoulli's Equation

Six problems on the Reynolds number, the continuity equation $A_1v_1 = A_2v_2$, flow rate and Bernoulli's equation $p + \tfrac12\rho v^2 + \rho g h = \text{constant}$. Try each one first. Open the hint if you are stuck, then compare with the full step-by-step solution.

Problem 1

Water ($\rho = 1000\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$, $\eta = 1.0 \times 10^{-3}\ \mathrm{Pa\,s}$) flows at $0.050\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ through a pipe of diameter $2.0\ \mathrm{cm}$. Find the Reynolds number. Is the flow laminar or turbulent?

Uses: Laminar and Turbulent Flow

Show hint

$Re = \dfrac{\rho v d}{\eta}$. Below about 2000 the flow is laminar; above about 4000 it is turbulent.

Show solution
  1. $Re = \dfrac{1000 \times 0.050 \times 0.020}{1.0 \times 10^{-3}} = \dfrac{1.0}{1.0 \times 10^{-3}} = 1000$.
  2. $1000 < 2000$, so the flow is laminar: the water moves in smooth layers.

$Re = 1000$: laminar

Problem 2

Water leaves a hose of diameter $2.0\ \mathrm{cm}$ at $1.5\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ and passes through a nozzle of diameter $0.50\ \mathrm{cm}$. How fast does it leave the nozzle? (Type $v$ in $\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$.)

Uses: Equation of Continuity

Show hint

$A_1v_1 = A_2v_2$, and the area of a circle goes as the diameter squared.

Show solution
  1. $v_2 = v_1 \dfrac{A_1}{A_2} = v_1 \left(\dfrac{d_1}{d_2}\right)^2 = 1.5 \times \left(\dfrac{2.0}{0.50}\right)^2 = 1.5 \times 16 = 24\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$.

$v_2 = 24\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$

Problem 3

Water flows at $2.0\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ through a pipe of radius $1.0\ \mathrm{cm}$. Find the flow rate and the time needed to fill a $100$-litre tank ($0.10\ \mathrm{m^3}$). (Type the time in s, to the nearest second.)

Uses: Equation of Continuity

Show hint

Flow rate $\dfrac{V}{t} = Av$.

Show solution
  1. $A = \pi r^2 = \pi (0.010)^2 = 3.14 \times 10^{-4}\ \mathrm{m^2}$.
  2. $\dfrac{V}{t} = Av = 3.14 \times 10^{-4} \times 2.0 = 6.28 \times 10^{-4}\ \mathrm{m^3\,s^{-1}}$.
  3. $t = \dfrac{0.10}{6.28 \times 10^{-4}} \approx 159\ \mathrm{s}$ (about $2.7$ minutes).

Flow rate $6.28 \times 10^{-4}\ \mathrm{m^3\,s^{-1}}$; $t \approx 159\ \mathrm{s}$

Problem 4

What is the greatest speed at which water ($\rho = 1000\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$, $\eta = 1.0 \times 10^{-3}\ \mathrm{Pa\,s}$) can flow through a pipe of diameter $1.0\ \mathrm{cm}$ and still be laminar? Take the limit as $Re = 2000$. (Type $v$ in $\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$.)

Uses: Laminar and Turbulent Flow

Show hint

Rearrange $Re = \dfrac{\rho v d}{\eta}$ for $v$.

Show solution
  1. $v = \dfrac{Re\,\eta}{\rho d} = \dfrac{2000 \times 1.0 \times 10^{-3}}{1000 \times 0.010} = 0.20\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$.
  2. Faster than this the flow starts to become turbulent; a wider pipe would reach that point at an even lower speed.

$v_{\max} = 0.20\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$

Problem 5

A large open tank has a small hole in its side $5.0\ \mathrm{m}$ below the water surface. How fast does water come out of the hole? (Type $v$ in $\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$, to 3 significant figures.)

Uses: Bernoulli's Equation

Show hint

Apply Bernoulli between the surface and the hole. Both are at atmospheric pressure, and the surface of a large tank hardly moves.

Show solution
  1. $p_0 + 0 + \rho g h = p_0 + \tfrac12\rho v^2 + 0$.
  2. $v = \sqrt{2gh} = \sqrt{2 \times 9.8 \times 5.0} = \sqrt{98} \approx 9.90\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ (Torricelli\'s law), the same speed as a stone falling $5.0$ m.

$v \approx 9.90\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$

Problem 6

Water flows at $1.0\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ with pressure $2.0 \times 10^5\ \mathrm{Pa}$ in a pipe at ground level. The pipe rises $5.0\ \mathrm{m}$ to an upper floor and its cross-section halves. Find the speed and the pressure upstairs. (Type the pressure in kPa, to 4 significant figures.)

Uses: Equation of Continuity, Bernoulli's Equation

Show hint

Continuity gives the new speed; then Bernoulli with $h_1 = 0$, $h_2 = 5.0\ \mathrm{m}$.

Show solution
  1. $A_2 = \tfrac12 A_1 \implies v_2 = 2v_1 = 2.0\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$.
  2. $p_2 = p_1 + \tfrac12\rho(v_1^2 - v_2^2) - \rho g h_2 = 2.0 \times 10^5 + \tfrac12(1000)(1 - 4) - 1000 \times 9.8 \times 5.0$.
  3. $p_2 = 200\,000 - 1500 - 49\,000 = 149\,500\ \mathrm{Pa} = 149.5\ \mathrm{kPa}$.

$v_2 = 2.0\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$, $p_2 = 149.5\ \mathrm{kPa}$
