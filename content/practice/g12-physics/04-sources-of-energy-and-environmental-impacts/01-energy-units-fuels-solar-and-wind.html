Energy Units, Fuels, Solar and Wind Power

Six problems on energy units (kWh, Btu), the energy from fission and $E = \Delta m\,c^2$, solar panels and the wind power formula $P = \tfrac12\rho A v^3$. Try each one first. Open the hint if you are stuck, then compare with the full step-by-step solution.

Problem 1

A household uses $250\ \mathrm{kWh}$ of electrical energy in a month. Express this in joules. (Type the energy in units of $10^8\ \mathrm{J}$.)

Uses: Energy Demand and Crisis

Show hint

$1\ \mathrm{kWh} = 1000\ \mathrm{W} \times 3600\ \mathrm{s} = 3.6 \times 10^6\ \mathrm{J}$.

Show solution
  1. $E = 250 \times 3.6 \times 10^6 = 9.0 \times 10^8\ \mathrm{J}$.

$E = 9.0 \times 10^8\ \mathrm{J}$

Problem 2

Each fission of a uranium-235 nucleus releases about $200\ \mathrm{MeV}$. Express this in joules. ($1\ \mathrm{eV} = 1.6 \times 10^{-19}\ \mathrm{J}$; type the energy in units of $10^{-11}\ \mathrm{J}$.)

Uses: Non-Renewable Sources of Energy

Show hint

$1\ \mathrm{MeV} = 10^6\ \mathrm{eV}$.

Show solution
  1. $E = 200 \times 10^6 \times 1.6 \times 10^{-19} = 3.2 \times 10^{-11}\ \mathrm{J}$.
  2. Tiny for one nucleus, but $1$ g of U-235 holds about $2.6 \times 10^{21}$ nuclei.

$E = 3.2 \times 10^{-11}\ \mathrm{J}$

Problem 3

If $1.0\ \mathrm{g}$ of mass were changed completely into energy, how much energy would be released? Express it in kWh. ($c = 3.0 \times 10^8\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$; type the answer in units of $10^7\ \mathrm{kWh}$.)

Uses: Non-Renewable Sources of Energy, Energy Demand and Crisis

Show hint

$E = \Delta m\,c^2$ with $\Delta m$ in kg, then divide by $3.6 \times 10^6\ \mathrm{J}$ per kWh.

Show solution
  1. $E = (1.0 \times 10^{-3})(3.0 \times 10^8)^2 = 9.0 \times 10^{13}\ \mathrm{J}$.
  2. $E = \dfrac{9.0 \times 10^{13}}{3.6 \times 10^6} = 2.5 \times 10^7\ \mathrm{kWh}$: enough for about $8000$ such households for a year.

$E = 9.0 \times 10^{13}\ \mathrm{J} = 2.5 \times 10^7\ \mathrm{kWh}$

Problem 4

A solar panel of area $2.0\ \mathrm{m^2}$ receives sunlight of intensity $1000\ \mathrm{W\,m^{-2}}$ and has an efficiency of $15\,\%$. Find its electrical power output and the energy it gives in $5.0$ hours of sunshine. (Type the energy in kWh.)

Uses: Renewable Energy Sources — Solar and Wind

Show hint

Input power $= IA$; output $=$ efficiency $\times$ input.

Show solution
  1. Input: $P_{\text{in}} = IA = 1000 \times 2.0 = 2000\ \mathrm{W}$.
  2. Output: $P = 0.15 \times 2000 = 300\ \mathrm{W}$.
  3. Energy: $E = Pt = 0.300\ \mathrm{kW} \times 5.0\ \mathrm{h} = 1.5\ \mathrm{kWh}$.

$P = 300\ \mathrm{W}$; $E = 1.5\ \mathrm{kWh}$

Problem 5

The blades of a wind turbine are $20\ \mathrm{m}$ long. Find the power carried by a $10\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ wind through the circle they sweep ($\rho_{\text{air}} = 1.3\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$). If the turbine converts $40\,\%$ of it, what is its output? (Type the wind power in kW, to the nearest kW.)

Uses: Renewable Energy Sources — Solar and Wind

Show hint

$P = \tfrac12\rho A v^3$ with $A = \pi r^2$ and $r$ = blade length.

Show solution
  1. $A = \pi (20)^2 = 1257\ \mathrm{m^2}$.
  2. $P = \tfrac12 \times 1.3 \times 1257 \times 10^3 = 8.168 \times 10^{5}\ \mathrm{W} \approx 817\ \mathrm{kW}$.
  3. Output: $0.40 \times 817 \approx 327\ \mathrm{kW}$.

Wind power $\approx 817\ \mathrm{kW}$; output $\approx 327\ \mathrm{kW}$

Problem 6

For the turbine in the last problem ($20\ \mathrm{m}$ blades, $\rho = 1.3\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$): (i) by what factor does the wind power change when the wind speed doubles? (ii) What wind speed carries $1.0\ \mathrm{MW}$ through the blade circle? (Type the speed in $\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$, to 3 significant figures.)

Uses: Renewable Energy Sources — Solar and Wind

Show hint

$P \propto v^3$. For (ii) solve $v^3 = \dfrac{2P}{\rho A}$.

Show solution
  1. (i) $P \propto v^3$, so doubling $v$ multiplies $P$ by $2^3 = 8$.
  2. (ii) $v^3 = \dfrac{2 \times 1.0 \times 10^6}{1.3 \times 1257} = 1224$.
  3. $v = \sqrt[3]{1224} \approx 10.7\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$: only about $7\,\%$ more wind than $10\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ gives $22\,\%$ more power.

(i) $\times 8$   (ii) $v \approx 10.7\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$
