Counting with Sets

Five problems on counting elements of unions, complements and subsets. Try each one first. Open the hint if you are stuck, then compare with the full solution.

Problem 1

How many integers from $1$ to $100$ are multiples of $3$ or $5$?

Uses: Intersection and Union

Show hint

$n(A \cup B) = n(A) + n(B) - n(A \cap B)$. Multiples of both $3$ and $5$ are multiples of $15$.

Show solution
  1. Multiples of $3$: $33$. Multiples of $5$: $20$. Multiples of $15$: $6$.
  2. $33 + 20 - 6 = 47$.

$47$

Problem 2

How many integers from $1$ to $100$ are multiples of neither $3$ nor $5$?

Uses: De Morgan, Complement

Show hint

“Neither” is $\overline{A} \cap \overline{B} = \overline{A \cup B}$. Use Problem 1.

Show solution
  1. $n(\overline{A} \cap \overline{B}) = n(\overline{A \cup B}) = 100 - n(A \cup B)$.
  2. $100 - 47 = 53$.

$53$

Problem 3

How many subsets of $\{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$ contain $1$ but not $2$?

Uses: Subset and Empty Set

Show hint

For each element, a subset either contains it or not. Here $1$ and $2$ are already decided.

Show solution
  1. $1$ is in, $2$ is out: no choice for those.
  2. $3$, $4$, $5$ are each in or out: $2^3 = 8$ subsets.

$8$ subsets

Problem 4

In a class of $50$, $30$ students like maths, $25$ like English, and $8$ like neither. How many like both?

Uses: Intersection and Union, Complement

Show hint

First find $n(A \cup B)$ from the students who like neither.

Show solution
  1. $n(A \cup B) = 50 - 8 = 42$.
  2. $n(A \cap B) = n(A) + n(B) - n(A \cup B) = 30 + 25 - 42 = 13$.

$13$ students

Problem 5

How many integers from $1$ to $100$ are multiples of $2$, $3$ or $5$?

Uses: Intersection and Union

Show hint

For three sets: $n(A \cup B \cup C) = n(A) + n(B) + n(C) - n(A \cap B) - n(B \cap C) - n(C \cap A) + n(A \cap B \cap C)$.

Show solution
  1. Multiples of $2, 3, 5$: $50, 33, 20$.
  2. Of $6, 15, 10$ (two at a time): $16, 6, 10$. Of $30$ (all three): $3$.
  3. $50 + 33 + 20 - 16 - 6 - 10 + 3 = 74$.

$74$

A Venn diagram shows why $+3$ is needed: the multiples of $30$ are added three times and subtracted three times, so they must be added back once.
