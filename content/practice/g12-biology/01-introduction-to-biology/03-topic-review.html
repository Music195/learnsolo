Introduction to Biology: Topic Review

Topic review: a mix of the whole chapter, best attempted after completing the concept sets. Six problems mixing the themes of biology with its uses in agriculture, medicine and industry. Try each one first. Open the hint if you are stuck, then compare with the full step-by-step solution.

Problem 1

Name the level of organization: (a) all the tilapia in one pond, (b) all the living things in the pond, (c) the pond with its living things, water and mud.

Uses: Themes of Biology

Show hint

Population, community and ecosystem each add something to the level before.

Show solution
  1. (a) One species in one place: a population.
  2. (b) All the populations living together: a community.
  3. (c) The community plus its physical surroundings: an ecosystem.

(a) population, (b) community, (c) ecosystem

Problem 2

Myanmar leads ASEAN in one group of crops. Which group? Name the three main types of crop in Myanmar and three examples of the leading group.

Uses: Importance of Biology in Daily Life

Show hint

About 18 types are grown, led by blackgram.

Show solution
  1. The main crops are cereals, pulses and oilseeds.
  2. Myanmar is the leading pulse and bean producer in ASEAN.
  3. Examples: blackgram, greengram, pigeonpea (also chickpea).

Pulses and beans; cereals, pulses, oilseeds; blackgram, greengram, pigeonpea.

Problem 3

Continue the beetle problem from Set 1: $60$ green and $40$ brown beetles, and each generation only $50\,\%$ of green and $90\,\%$ of brown beetles survive. What percentage are brown after two generations? (Type the percentage, to 3 significant figures.)

Uses: Themes of Biology

Show hint

Apply the survival rates twice.

Show solution
  1. After one generation: $30$ green, $36$ brown.
  2. After two: green $30 \times 0.5 = 15$, brown $36 \times 0.9 = 32.4$.
  3. Brown share: $\dfrac{32.4}{15 + 32.4} \times 100\,\% \approx 68.4\,\%$: the adapted form keeps spreading.

About $68.4\,\%$ brown

Problem 4

What is zootherapy? Give three examples of medicines or tests that come from animals.

Uses: Importance of Biology in Daily Life

Show hint

It is the animal-based partner of pharmacognosy.

Show solution
  1. Zootherapy is treating illness with medicines from animals.
  2. Examples: leeches (to remove blood after surgery), viper venom (led to the blood-pressure drug captopril), maggots (clean wounds), salmon calcitonin, and horseshoe crab blood (the LAL test for bacterial toxins).

Medicines from animals: e.g. viper venom → captopril, horseshoe crab blood → LAL test, leeches.

Problem 5

Why is evolution called the core theme of biology? Use the words unity and diversity.

Uses: Themes of Biology

Show hint

Think about why all organisms share a genetic code, yet look so different.

Show solution
  1. Unity: all organisms come from common ancestors, so they share features such as DNA, the genetic code and cells.
  2. Diversity: populations in different environments were shaped by natural selection, so they changed in different ways.
  3. Evolution therefore explains both what all life has in common and why it is so varied.

Common descent explains the unity of life; natural selection in different environments explains its diversity.

Problem 6

Describe the structure of a plant fibre. Name two plant fibres grown in Myanmar with one use each, and Myanmar's two most valuable timbers.

Uses: Importance of Biology in Daily Life

Show hint

Plant fibres are a kind of supporting cell.

Show solution
  1. Plant fibres are long sclerenchyma cells whose walls are thickened with cellulose and lignin.
  2. Cotton (Gossypium, central Myanmar): cloth. Jute (Corchorus, a bast fibre): gunny bags.
  3. Timbers: teak (Tectona grandis) and pyinkado (Xylia dolabriformis).

Long sclerenchyma cells with cellulose and lignin; cotton (cloth), jute (gunny bags); teak and pyinkado.
