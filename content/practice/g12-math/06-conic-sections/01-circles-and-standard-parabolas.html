Conic Sections — Circles and Standard Parabolas

Practise equations of circles, center and radius, standard parabolas with vertex at the origin, focal length, focus coordinates, directrix lines, and focal properties.

Find the radius of the circle with equation $x^2 + y^2 = 49$.

Uses: Circles and Degenerate Cases

Hint

The standard equation of a circle centered at the origin is $x^2 + y^2 = r^2$.

Solution

Comparing with $x^2 + y^2 = r^2$:

\[ r^2 = 49 \implies r = \sqrt{49} = 7. \]

A parabola has equation $y^2 = 12x$. Find the $x$-coordinate of its focus.

Uses: Parabolas with Vertex at the Origin

Hint

Compare with $y^2 = 4px$. The focus is located at $(p, 0)$.

Solution

Equating coefficients with $y^2 = 4px$:

\[ 4p = 12 \implies p = 3. \]

The focus is at $(p, 0) = (3, 0)$, so its $x$-coordinate is $3$.

Find the radius of the circle given by the general equation $x^2 + y^2 - 6x + 8y = 0$.

Uses: Circles and Degenerate Cases

Hint

Complete the square for $x$ and $y$: $(x - 3)^2 + (y + 4)^2 = 3^2 + 4^2$.

Solution

Complete the squares in $x$ and $y$:

\[ (x^2 - 6x + 9) + (y^2 + 8y + 16) = 9 + 16 \] \[ (x - 3)^2 + (y + 4)^2 = 25. \]

The center is $(3, -4)$ and the radius is $r = \sqrt{25} = 5$.

The parabola $x^2 = -16y$ opens downwards with vertex at the origin. Find the value of $k$ if its directrix has equation $y = k$.

Uses: Parabolas with Vertex at the Origin

Hint

Match with $x^2 = 4py$. Here $4p = -16 \implies p = -4$. The directrix is the horizontal line $y = -p$.

Solution

From $x^2 = 4py$, we have:

\[ 4p = -16 \implies p = -4. \]

The focus is at $(0, p) = (0, -4)$, and the directrix is given by:

\[ y = -p = -(-4) = 4. \]

Find the length of the latus rectum of the parabola $y^2 = 20x$.

Uses: Parabolas with Vertex at the Origin

Hint

The length of the latus rectum of a parabola $y^2 = 4px$ is $|4p|$.

Solution

The standard form is $y^2 = 4px$.

The length of the focal chord perpendicular to the axis of symmetry (the latus rectum) is:

\[ \text{Length} = |4p| = 20. \]

A point $P(x_0, y_0)$ lies on the parabola $y^2 = 8x$. If the distance from $P$ to the focus of the parabola is $6$, find the $x$-coordinate $x_0$.

Uses: Parabolas with Vertex at the Origin

Hint

By the geometric definition of a parabola, the distance from any point on the parabola to the focus equals its perpendicular distance to the directrix: $d = x_0 + p$.

Solution

For $y^2 = 8x$, we have $4p = 8 \implies p = 2$. The directrix is $x = -2$.

By definition, the distance from $P(x_0, y_0)$ to the focus equals the distance from $P$ to the directrix:

\[ d = x_0 - (-p) = x_0 + p = x_0 + 2. \]

Given that the focal distance is $6$:

\[ x_0 + 2 = 6 \implies x_0 = 4. \]
