Conic Sections

A comprehensive review of Conic Sections, covering circle geometry, parabola properties at origin and translated vertices, rotation of coordinate axes, and discriminant conic classification.

The equation $(x - 4)^2 + (y + 1)^2 = r^2$ represents a single point (a degenerate point circle) rather than a curve. What must be the value of $r^2$?

Uses: Circles and Degenerate Cases

Hint

A circle equation with radius $r = 0$ reduces to the single point $(h, k)$, meaning $r^2 = 0$.

Solution

If $r^2 > 0$, the graph is a real circle. If $r^2 < 0$, no real points satisfy the equation.

When $r^2 = 0$, only the point $(4, -1)$ satisfies the equation, forming a degenerate point circle:

\[ r^2 = 0. \]

A parabola has its vertex at the origin $(0, 0)$ and its focus at $(4, 0)$. What is the focal parameter $p$?

Uses: Parabolas with Vertex at the Origin

Hint

For a standard horizontal parabola with vertex at $(0, 0)$, the focus is $(p, 0)$.

Solution

Since the focus is $(4, 0)$ and the vertex is $(0, 0)$:

\[ p = 4 - 0 = 4. \]

(The equation of this parabola is $y^2 = 4(4)x = 16x$.)

Find the diameter of the circle whose equation is $x^2 + y^2 - 6x - 8y = 0$.

Uses: Circles and Degenerate Cases

Hint

Find the radius $r$ by completing the square, then compute the diameter $D = 2r$.

Solution

Completing the square in $x$ and $y$:

\[ (x - 3)^2 + (y - 4)^2 = 3^2 + 4^2 = 9 + 16 = 25. \]

The radius is $r = \sqrt{25} = 5$.

The diameter is:

\[ D = 2r = 2(5) = 10. \]

Find the $x$-coordinate of the vertex of the parabola $y^2 - 4y - 8x + 20 = 0$.

Uses: Translated Parabolas with Vertex (h, k)

Hint

Complete the square in $y$ to put the equation in standard form $(y - k)^2 = 4p(x - h)$.

Solution

Isolate the $y$-terms and complete the square:

\[ y^2 - 4y = 8x - 20 \] \[ y^2 - 4y + 4 = 8x - 20 + 4 \] \[ (y - 2)^2 = 8x - 16 = 8(x - 2). \]

Comparing with $(y - k)^2 = 4p(x - h)$, the vertex is $(h, k) = (2, 2)$.

The $x$-coordinate is $2$.

Evaluate the discriminant $B^2 - 4AC$ for the conic $2x^2 + 2xy + 2y^2 - 5 = 0$.

Uses: Rotation of Axes and General Conics

Hint

Here $A = 2$, $B = 2$, and $C = 2$. Calculate $B^2 - 4AC$.

Solution

From the given conic equation:

\[ A = 2, \quad B = 2, \quad C = 2. \]

The discriminant is:

\[ B^2 - 4AC = 2^2 - 4(2)(2) = 4 - 16 = -12. \]

(Since $B^2 - 4AC < 0$, the conic is an ellipse).

For any point $P(x_0, y_0)$ on $y^2 = 12x$ having distance $7$ to the directrix, find its distance to the focus plus $3$.

Uses: Parabolas with Vertex at the Origin

Hint

Recall the focal definition of a parabola: the distance from any point on the curve to the focus is identical to its distance to the directrix.

Solution

By definition of a parabola, for any point $P$ on the curve:

\[ \text{Distance to focus} = \text{Distance to directrix} = 7. \]

Therefore, its distance to the focus plus $3$ is:

\[ 7 + 3 = 10. \]
