Vector Algebra — Cross Product, Lines and Planes

Practise the vector (cross) product, areas of parallelograms and triangles, vector lines in space, and vector equations of planes.

Find the $z$-component (the coefficient of $\mathbf{k}$) of the cross product $\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v}$, where $\mathbf{u} = 2\mathbf{i} + 3\mathbf{j}$ and $\mathbf{v} = 4\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j}$.

Uses: Vector Product and Area of Parallelogram

Hint

For vectors in the $xy$-plane with $u_z = v_z = 0$, the cross product is along $\mathbf{k}$ with value $(u_x v_y - u_y v_x)\mathbf{k}$.

Solution

Using the determinant definition with $u_z = 0$ and $v_z = 0$:

\[ \mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v} = (u_x v_y - u_y v_x)\mathbf{k} = ((2)(-1) - (3)(4))\mathbf{k} = (-2 - 12)\mathbf{k} = -14\mathbf{k}. \]

The $z$-component is $-14$.

Find the area of the parallelogram spanned by the two adjacent vectors $\mathbf{a} = 2\mathbf{i}$ and $\mathbf{b} = \mathbf{i} + 5\mathbf{j}$.

Uses: Vector Product and Area of Parallelogram

Hint

The area of the parallelogram is given by the magnitude of the cross product: $\text{Area} = \|\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}\|$.

Solution

Calculate the cross product:

\[ \mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} = (2\mathbf{i}) \times (\mathbf{i} + 5\mathbf{j}) = 2(\mathbf{i} \times \mathbf{i}) + 10(\mathbf{i} \times \mathbf{j}) = \mathbf{0} + 10\mathbf{k} = 10\mathbf{k}. \]

The area is the magnitude:

\[ \text{Area} = \|10\mathbf{k}\| = 10. \]

Find the area of the triangle in space with vertices $A(1, 0, 0)$, $B(1, 3, 0)$, and $C(1, 0, 4)$.

Uses: Vector Product and Area of Parallelogram

Hint

Find the edge vectors $\mathbf{AB}$ and $\mathbf{AC}$, then use $\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}\|\mathbf{AB} \times \mathbf{AC}\|$.

Solution

Determine the vectors forming two sides of the triangle from vertex $A$:

\[ \mathbf{AB} = \langle 0, 3, 0 \rangle, \quad \mathbf{AC} = \langle 0, 0, 4 \rangle. \]

Compute their cross product:

\[ \mathbf{AB} \times \mathbf{AC} = (3\mathbf{j}) \times (4\mathbf{k}) = 12(\mathbf{j} \times \mathbf{k}) = 12\mathbf{i}. \]

The area of the triangle is:

\[ \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \|\mathbf{AB} \times \mathbf{AC}\| = \frac{1}{2}(12) = 6. \]

A line has vector equation $\mathbf{r}(t) = (2\mathbf{i} + 3\mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k}) + t(\mathbf{i} - 2\mathbf{j} + 4\mathbf{k})$. Find the $z$-coordinate of the point on the line where $x = 5$.

Uses: Vector Equations of Lines and Planes

Hint

Express the components: $x(t) = 2 + t$ and $z(t) = -1 + 4t$. Find $t$ when $x = 5$.

Solution

From the vector equation:

\[ x = 2 + t, \quad y = 3 - 2t, \quad z = -1 + 4t. \]

Given $x = 5$:

\[ 2 + t = 5 \implies t = 3. \]

Substitute $t = 3$ into the equation for $z$:

\[ z = -1 + 4(3) = -1 + 12 = 11. \]

A plane is parallel to the vectors $\mathbf{u} = \langle 1, 1, 0 \rangle$ and $\mathbf{v} = \langle 0, 1, 1 \rangle$, and it passes through the point $(2, 1, 3)$. Its Cartesian equation is $x - y + z = D$. Find the constant $D$.

Uses: Vector Equations of Lines and Planes

Hint

Substitute the point $(2, 1, 3)$ into the given plane equation $x - y + z = D$.

Solution

The normal vector is $\mathbf{n} = \mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v} = \langle 1, -1, 1 \rangle$.

Since the point $(2, 1, 3)$ lies on the plane $x - y + z = D$:

\[ D = 2 - 1 + 3 = 4. \]

Find the volume of the parallelepiped defined by the three coterminous vectors $\mathbf{u} = \langle 2, 0, 0 \rangle$, $\mathbf{v} = \langle 1, 3, 0 \rangle$, and $\mathbf{w} = \langle 4, 2, 5 \rangle$.

Uses: Vector Product and Area of Parallelogram

Hint

The volume is given by the absolute value of the scalar triple product: $V = |\mathbf{u} \cdot (\mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{w})|$, which equals the determinant of the $3 \times 3$ matrix formed by the vectors.

Solution

Calculate the determinant of the matrix whose rows are the three vectors:

\[ V = \left| \det \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 3 & 0 \\ 4 & 2 & 5 \end{pmatrix} \right| \]

Expanding along the first row:

\[ V = \left| 2 \cdot ((3)(5) - (0)(2)) \right| = |2 \cdot 15| = 30. \]
