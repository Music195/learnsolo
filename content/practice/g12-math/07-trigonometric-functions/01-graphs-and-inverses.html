Trigonometric Functions — Graphs and Inverses

Practise amplitudes, periods, and phase shifts of sinusoidal waves, asymptotes and periods of tangent/cotangent graphs, and principal values of inverse trigonometric functions.

Find the amplitude of the trigonometric function $y = -5\sin(2x - \pi) + 3$.

Uses: Graphs of Sine and Cosine Functions

Hint

For a function in the form $y = A\sin(Bx - C) + D$, the amplitude is $|A|$.

Solution

The amplitude is the vertical distance from the midline to a peak or trough:

\[ \text{Amplitude} = |A| = |-5| = 5. \]

Find the fundamental period $T$ (in degrees) of the function $f(x) = \sin(4x)$.

Uses: Graphs of Sine and Cosine Functions

Hint

The period of $\sin(Bx)$ is $\frac{360^\circ}{B}$.

Solution

Since the coefficient of the argument is $B = 4$:

\[ T = \frac{360^\circ}{B} = \frac{360^\circ}{4} = 90^\circ. \]

Find the principal value of $\arcsin\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)$ in degrees.

Uses: Inverse Trigonometric Functions

Hint

The principal range of $\arcsin\theta$ is $\left[-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$, or $[-90^\circ, 90^\circ]$. Determine the angle whose sine is $1/2$.

Solution

We seek $\theta \in [-90^\circ, 90^\circ]$ such that $\sin\theta = \frac{1}{2}$:

\[ \theta = 30^\circ. \]

Find the principal value of $\arccos\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)$ in degrees.

Uses: Inverse Trigonometric Functions

Hint

The principal range of $\arccos x$ is $[0, \pi]$, or $[0^\circ, 180^\circ]$. Use $\arccos(-x) = 180^\circ - \arccos(x)$.

Solution

Since $\cos(60^\circ) = \frac{1}{2}$, for negative argument in quadrant II:

\[ \arccos\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right) = 180^\circ - 60^\circ = 120^\circ. \]

Evaluate the exact numerical value of $\tan(\arctan(7))$.

Uses: Inverse Trigonometric Functions

Hint

For any real number $x \in (-\infty, \infty)$, the inverse identity $\tan(\arctan(x)) = x$ holds.

Solution

Since the domain of $\arctan(x)$ is all real numbers $\mathbb{R}$:

\[ \tan(\arctan(7)) = 7. \]

Evaluate $\sin\left(2\arcsin\left(\frac{3}{5}\right)\right)$ as a decimal.

Uses: Inverse Trigonometric Functions

Hint

Let $\theta = \arcsin(3/5)$, so $\sin\theta = 3/5$ and $\cos\theta = \sqrt{1 - (3/5)^2} = 4/5$. Then apply $\sin(2\theta) = 2\sin\theta\cos\theta$.

Solution

Let $\theta = \arcsin\left(\frac{3}{5}\right)$. Then $\sin\theta = \frac{3}{5}$ and $\cos\theta = \sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{3}{5}\right)^2} = \frac{4}{5}$.

By the double-angle identity:

\[ \sin(2\theta) = 2\sin\theta\cos\theta = 2\left(\frac{3}{5}\right)\left(\frac{4}{5}\right) = \frac{24}{25} = 0.96. \]
