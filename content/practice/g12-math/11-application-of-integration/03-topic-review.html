Application of Integration

A comprehensive review of Application of Integration, covering definite integral properties, area between curves, volumes of solids of revolution (disc and washer methods), and volume by slicing.

If $\int_1^5 f(x)\,dx = 8$, evaluate $\int_5^1 f(x)\,dx$.

Uses: Definite Integrals and Properties

Hint

Reversing the upper and lower limits of integration negates the value of the integral: $\int_b^a f(x)\,dx = -\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$.

Solution

By the reversal property of definite integrals:

\[ \int_5^1 f(x)\,dx = -\int_1^5 f(x)\,dx = -8. \]

Find the area of the region bounded by $y = 2x$, the $x$-axis, and the vertical line $x = 4$.

Uses: Area Between a Curve and the x-axis

Hint

Evaluate the definite integral $\int_0^4 2x\,dx$, or calculate the area of the right triangle $\frac{1}{2}\times\text{base}\times\text{height}$.

Solution

Using integration:

\[ \text{Area} = \int_0^4 2x\,dx = [x^2]_0^4 = 4^2 - 0 = 16. \]

Alternatively, the right triangle has base $4$ and height $2(4) = 8$:

\[ \text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}(4)(8) = 16. \]

Find the area enclosed between the parabola $y = 9 - x^2$ and the $x$-axis.

Uses: Area Between a Curve and the x-axis

Hint

The $x$-intercepts are $x = \pm 3$. Integrate $9 - x^2$ from $-3$ to $3$, or double the integral from $0$ to $3$.

Solution

The parabola crosses the $x$-axis at $x = \pm 3$. Using symmetry:

\[ \text{Area} = 2\int_0^3 (9 - x^2)\,dx = 2\left[9x - \frac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^3 = 2(27 - 9) = 2(18) = 36. \]

The horizontal line segment $y = 2$ from $x = 0$ to $x = 3$ is revolved about the $x$-axis to generate a circular cylinder of volume $V$. Evaluate $\frac{V}{\pi}$.

Uses: Volumes of Solids: Slicing, Disc, and Washer Methods

Hint

Use the disc method $V = \pi \int_0^3 2^2\,dx = 4\pi(3)$, or cylinder volume formula $V = \pi r^2 h$.

Solution

Using the disc method:

\[ V = \pi \int_0^3 2^2\,dx = 4\pi [x]_0^3 = 12\pi. \]

Dividing by $\pi$ gives $\frac{V}{\pi} = 12$.

Find the total area enclosed between the straight line $y = 6x$ and the parabola $y = 3x^2$.

Uses: Area Between Two Curves

Hint

Solve $3x^2 = 6x$ to find the bounds, then evaluate $\int_0^2 (6x - 3x^2)\,dx$.

Solution

Find the intersection points:

\[ 3x^2 = 6x \implies 3x(x - 2) = 0 \implies x = 0 \text{ and } x = 2. \]

On $[0, 2]$, the line lies above the parabola ($6x \ge 3x^2$):

\[ \text{Area} = \int_0^2 (6x - 3x^2)\,dx = \left[3x^2 - x^3\right]_0^2 = (3(2^2) - 2^3) - 0 = 12 - 8 = 4. \]

The semicircle $y = \sqrt{9 - x^2}$ for $x \in [-3, 3]$ is revolved around the $x$-axis to generate a solid sphere of radius $R = 3$. If the volume is $V$, evaluate $\frac{V}{\pi}$.

Uses: Volumes of Solids: Slicing, Disc, and Washer Methods

Hint

Use the disc method $V = \pi \int_{-3}^3 (9 - x^2)\,dx$, or the sphere volume formula $V = \frac{4}{3}\pi R^3$.

Solution

By the sphere formula with $R = 3$:

\[ V = \frac{4}{3}\pi (3^3) = \frac{4}{3}\pi (27) = 36\pi. \]

Equivalently by integration:

\[ V = \pi \int_{-3}^3 (9 - x^2)\,dx = 2\pi \left[9x - \frac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^3 = 2\pi(27 - 9) = 36\pi. \]

Thus $\frac{V}{\pi} = 36$.
