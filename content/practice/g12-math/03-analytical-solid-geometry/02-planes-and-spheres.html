Analytical Solid Geometry — Planes and Spheres

Practise equations of planes in space, perpendicular distances from points to planes, angle between two planes, and equations of spheres in standard and general form.

Find the perpendicular distance from the point $P(1, 2, 3)$ to the plane $2x + 2y - z - 9 = 0$.

Uses: Planes in Space

Hint

Use the point-to-plane distance formula: $d = \frac{|Ax_0 + By_0 + Cz_0 + D|}{\sqrt{A^2 + B^2 + C^2}}$.

Solution

Substitute $(x_0, y_0, z_0) = (1, 2, 3)$ into $2x + 2y - z - 9 = 0$:

\[ d = \frac{|2(1) + 2(2) - (3) - 9|}{\sqrt{2^2 + 2^2 + (-1)^2}} = \frac{|2 + 4 - 3 - 9|}{\sqrt{4 + 4 + 1}} = \frac{|-6|}{\sqrt{9}} = \frac{6}{3} = 2. \]

Find the radius of the sphere whose general equation is $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 - 4x + 6y - 2z - 11 = 0$.

Uses: Equations of Spheres

Hint

Complete the squares in $x$, $y$, and $z$: $(x-h)^2 + (y-k)^2 + (z-l)^2 = R^2$, or use $R = \sqrt{u^2 + v^2 + w^2 - d}$.

Solution

Complete the square for each variable:

\[ (x^2 - 4x + 4) + (y^2 + 6y + 9) + (z^2 - 2z + 1) = 11 + 4 + 9 + 1 \] \[ (x - 2)^2 + (y + 3)^2 + (z - 1)^2 = 25. \]

The center is $(2, -3, 1)$ and the radius is $R = \sqrt{25} = 5$.

Find the acute angle $\theta$ (in degrees) between the two planes $x + y = 1$ and $y + z = 1$.

Uses: Planes in Space

Hint

The normal vectors are $\mathbf{n}_1 = \langle 1, 1, 0 \rangle$ and $\mathbf{n}_2 = \langle 0, 1, 1 \rangle$. Use $\cos\theta = \frac{|\mathbf{n}_1 \cdot \mathbf{n}_2|}{\|\mathbf{n}_1\| \|\mathbf{n}_2\|}$.

Solution

The normal vectors to the planes are:

\[ \mathbf{n}_1 = \langle 1, 1, 0 \rangle \quad \text{and} \quad \mathbf{n}_2 = \langle 0, 1, 1 \rangle. \]

Compute the magnitudes and dot product:

\[ \|\mathbf{n}_1\| = \sqrt{1^2 + 1^2 + 0^2} = \sqrt{2}, \quad \|\mathbf{n}_2\| = \sqrt{0^2 + 1^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{2} \] \[ \mathbf{n}_1 \cdot \mathbf{n}_2 = (1)(0) + (1)(1) + (0)(1) = 1. \]

Hence:

\[ \cos\theta = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}\sqrt{2}} = \frac{1}{2} \implies \theta = 60^\circ. \]

A sphere has its center at $C(1, 1, 1)$ and is tangent to the plane $2x - y + 2z + 6 = 0$. Find the radius of the sphere.

Uses: Equations of Spheres

Hint

Since the sphere is tangent to the plane, the radius is equal to the perpendicular distance from the center $C$ to the plane.

Solution

The radius $R$ is the distance from $C(1, 1, 1)$ to the plane $2x - y + 2z + 6 = 0$:

\[ R = \frac{|2(1) - (1) + 2(1) + 6|}{\sqrt{2^2 + (-1)^2 + 2^2}} = \frac{|2 - 1 + 2 + 6|}{\sqrt{4 + 1 + 4}} = \frac{|9|}{\sqrt{9}} = \frac{9}{3} = 3. \]

A line has parametric equations $x = 2t + 1$, $y = t$, and $z = 3t + 2$. If this line intersects the plane $x + 2y + 3z = 20$ at a point $Q(x_0, y_0, z_0)$, find the value of $z_0$.

Uses: Planes in Space

Hint

Substitute the expressions for $x$, $y$, and $z$ into the plane equation to find $t$, then calculate $z_0 = 3t + 2$.

Solution

Substitute the parametric coordinates into the plane equation:

\[ (2t + 1) + 2(t) + 3(3t + 2) = 20 \] \[ 2t + 1 + 2t + 9t + 6 = 20 \implies 13t + 7 = 20 \implies 13t = 13 \implies t = 1. \]

Now substitute $t = 1$ to find $z_0$:

\[ z_0 = 3(1) + 2 = 5. \]

A sphere is given by the equation $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 25$. It is intersected by the plane $z = 3$. Find the radius $r$ of the resulting circle of intersection.

Uses: Equations of Spheres

Hint

Substitute $z = 3$ directly into the sphere equation, or use the right-triangle relation $r = \sqrt{R^2 - d^2}$ where $d$ is the distance from the sphere's center to the plane.

Solution

Substitute $z = 3$ into the sphere equation $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 25$:

\[ x^2 + y^2 + 3^2 = 25 \implies x^2 + y^2 + 9 = 25 \implies x^2 + y^2 = 16. \]

This is a circle in the plane $z = 3$ with radius:

\[ r = \sqrt{16} = 4. \]
