Position Vectors, Division Points and Collinearity

Six problems on division points, the centroid, and the condition for three points to lie on a line. Try each one first. Open the hint if you are stuck, then compare with the full solution.

Problem 1

A$(-2, 5)$ and B$(4, -1)$. Find the points dividing AB internally in the ratio $1 : 2$ and externally in the ratio $1 : 2$. (Type the $x$-coordinate of the external point in the box.)

Uses: Division Points

Show hint

Internal $m : n$: $\dfrac{n\vec{a} + m\vec{b}}{m + n}$. External: $\dfrac{-n\vec{a} + m\vec{b}}{m - n}$.

Show solution
  1. Internal: $\dfrac{2(-2, 5) + (4, -1)}{3} = \dfrac{(0, 9)}{3} = (0, 3)$.
  2. External: $\dfrac{-2(-2, 5) + (4, -1)}{1 - 2} = \dfrac{(8, -11)}{-1} = (-8, 11)$.

Internal $(0, 3)$; external $(-8, 11)$

Problem 2

Triangle ABC has A$(-2, 1)$, B$(4, 2)$ and centroid G$(1, 3)$. Find C. (Type its $y$-coordinate in the box.)

Uses: Centroid

Show hint

$\vec{g} = \dfrac{\vec{a} + \vec{b} + \vec{c}}{3}$, so $\vec{c} = 3\vec{g} - \vec{a} - \vec{b}$.

Show solution
  1. $\vec{c} = (3, 9) - (-2, 1) - (4, 2) = (1, 6)$.

C$(1, 6)$

Problem 3

A$(1, 2)$, B$(4, -1)$ and C$(x, -5)$ lie on one line. Find $x$.

Uses: Points on a Line

Show hint

$\overrightarrow{AC} = k\overrightarrow{AB}$ with one $k$ for both components.

Show solution
  1. $\overrightarrow{AB} = (3, -3)$, $\overrightarrow{AC} = (x - 1, -7)$.
  2. $-7 = -3k$ gives $k = \dfrac{7}{3}$, so $x - 1 = 7$ and $x = 8$.

$x = 8$

Problem 4

In triangle OAB, $\vec{a} = \overrightarrow{OA}$ and $\vec{b} = \overrightarrow{OB}$. P divides AB internally in the ratio $2 : 1$, and Q is the midpoint of OA. Write $\overrightarrow{OP}$ and $\overrightarrow{QP}$ in terms of $\vec{a}$ and $\vec{b}$.

Uses: Division Points

Show hint

$\overrightarrow{QP} = \overrightarrow{OP} - \overrightarrow{OQ}$.

Show solution
  1. $\overrightarrow{OP} = \dfrac{1 \cdot \vec{a} + 2\vec{b}}{3} = \dfrac{1}{3}\vec{a} + \dfrac{2}{3}\vec{b}$.
  2. $\overrightarrow{QP} = \dfrac{1}{3}\vec{a} + \dfrac{2}{3}\vec{b} - \dfrac{1}{2}\vec{a} = -\dfrac{1}{6}\vec{a} + \dfrac{2}{3}\vec{b}$.

$\overrightarrow{OP} = \dfrac{1}{3}\vec{a} + \dfrac{2}{3}\vec{b}$, $\overrightarrow{QP} = -\dfrac{1}{6}\vec{a} + \dfrac{2}{3}\vec{b}$

Problem 5

In triangle OAB, $\vec{a} = \overrightarrow{OA}$ and $\vec{b} = \overrightarrow{OB}$. C divides OA in the ratio $1 : 2$, and D divides OB in the ratio $3 : 1$. AD and BC meet at P. Write $\overrightarrow{OP}$ in terms of $\vec{a}$ and $\vec{b}$. (Type the coefficient of $\vec{a}$ in the box.)

Uses: Points on a Line, Division Points

Show hint

Write P on AD with one parameter and on BC with another, then compare coefficients ($\vec{a}$ and $\vec{b}$ are not parallel).

Show solution
  1. $\overrightarrow{OC} = \dfrac{1}{3}\vec{a}$, $\overrightarrow{OD} = \dfrac{3}{4}\vec{b}$.
  2. On AD: $\overrightarrow{OP} = (1 - s)\vec{a} + \dfrac{3s}{4}\vec{b}$. On BC: $\overrightarrow{OP} = \dfrac{t}{3}\vec{a} + (1 - t)\vec{b}$.
  3. $1 - s = \dfrac{t}{3}$ and $\dfrac{3s}{4} = 1 - t$: then $t = 3 - 3s$, $\dfrac{3s}{4} = 3s - 2$, so $s = \dfrac{8}{9}$, $t = \dfrac{1}{3}$.
  4. $\overrightarrow{OP} = \dfrac{1}{9}\vec{a} + \dfrac{2}{3}\vec{b}$.

$\overrightarrow{OP} = \dfrac{1}{9}\vec{a} + \dfrac{2}{3}\vec{b}$

Problem 6

Point P inside triangle ABC satisfies $2\overrightarrow{PA} + 3\overrightarrow{PB} + 4\overrightarrow{PC} = \vec{0}$. Use A as the origin, with $\vec{b} = \overrightarrow{AB}$ and $\vec{c} = \overrightarrow{AC}$.

  1. (1) Write $\overrightarrow{AP}$ in terms of $\vec{b}$ and $\vec{c}$.
  2. (2) Line AP meets BC at D. Find $BD : DC$ and $AP : PD$.
  3. (3) Find $\dfrac{\text{area of PBC}}{\text{area of ABC}}$. (Type it in the box.)
  4. (4) Where is P if instead $\overrightarrow{PA} + \overrightarrow{PB} + \overrightarrow{PC} = \vec{0}$?

Uses: Division Points, Points on a Line, Centroid

Show hint

(1) $\overrightarrow{PA} = -\vec{p}$, $\overrightarrow{PB} = \vec{b} - \vec{p}$, $\overrightarrow{PC} = \vec{c} - \vec{p}$. (2) Rewrite $\vec{p}$ as (number) $\times$ (a division point of BC).

Show solution
  1. (1) $-2\vec{p} + 3(\vec{b} - \vec{p}) + 4(\vec{c} - \vec{p}) = \vec{0}$, so $\overrightarrow{AP} = \dfrac{3\vec{b} + 4\vec{c}}{9}$.
  2. (2) $\overrightarrow{AP} = \dfrac{7}{9} \cdot \dfrac{3\vec{b} + 4\vec{c}}{7}$. The point $\dfrac{3\vec{b} + 4\vec{c}}{7}$ is D, on BC with $BD : DC = 4 : 3$, and $AP : PD = 7 : 2$.
  3. (3) Triangles PBC and ABC share base BC, and their heights are in the ratio $PD : AD = 2 : 9$. So the ratio is $\dfrac{2}{9}$.
  4. (4) Then $\overrightarrow{AP} = \dfrac{\vec{b} + \vec{c}}{3}$: P is the centroid of ABC.

(1) $\dfrac{3\vec{b} + 4\vec{c}}{9}$ (2) $4 : 3$, $7 : 2$ (3) $\dfrac{2}{9}$ (4) the centroid
