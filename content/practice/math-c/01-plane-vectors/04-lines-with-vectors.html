Vector Equations of Lines

Seven problems on the vector equation of a line, the line through two points, and lines with a given normal vector. Try each one first. Open the hint if you are stuck, then compare with the full solution.

Problem 1

Write the line through A$(2, -1)$ with direction $\vec{d} = (1, 3)$ in parametric form and as an equation in $x$ and $y$. Is $(5, 8)$ on it? If so, for which $t$? (Type $t$ in the box.)

Uses: Vector Equation of a Line

Show hint

$(x, y) = (2, -1) + t(1, 3)$. Eliminate $t$.

Show solution
  1. $x = 2 + t$, $y = -1 + 3t$. Eliminating $t$: $y = 3x - 7$.
  2. $(5, 8)$: $x = 5$ gives $t = 3$, and then $y = 8$ ✓.

$x = 2 + t$, $y = -1 + 3t$; $y = 3x - 7$; yes, $t = 3$

Problem 2

Find the equation of the line through $(-1, 2)$ perpendicular to $\vec{n} = (3, -2)$, and its $y$-intercept. (Type the $y$-intercept in the box.)

Uses: Line Perpendicular to a Vector

Show hint

$\vec{n} \cdot (\vec{p} - \vec{a}) = 0$: $a(x - x_1) + b(y - y_1) = 0$.

Show solution
  1. $3(x + 1) - 2(y - 2) = 0 \iff 3x - 2y + 7 = 0$.
  2. $x = 0$: $y = \dfrac{7}{2}$.

$3x - 2y + 7 = 0$; $y$-intercept $\dfrac{7}{2}$

Problem 3

A$(-1, 3)$ and B$(5, 0)$. Write line AB as $\vec{p} = (1 - t)\vec{a} + t\vec{b}$ in components. Find the point dividing AB internally in the ratio $2 : 1$, and the point where the line meets the $y$-axis. (Type its $y$-coordinate in the box.)

Uses: Line Through Two Points

Show hint

The point dividing AB in $m : n$ has $t = \dfrac{m}{m + n}$.

Show solution
  1. $\vec{p} = (-1 + 6t, 3 - 3t)$.
  2. $2 : 1$: $t = \dfrac{2}{3}$, the point $(3, 1)$.
  3. $y$-axis: $-1 + 6t = 0$, $t = \dfrac{1}{6}$, $y = 3 - \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{5}{2}$.

$(-1 + 6t, 3 - 3t)$; $(3, 1)$; $\left(0, \dfrac{5}{2}\right)$

Problem 4

Find the intersection of the lines $\vec{p} = (2, 1) + s(1, 2)$ and $\vec{p} = (5, 0) + t(-1, 1)$. (Type its $x$-coordinate in the box.)

Uses: Vector Equation of a Line

Show hint

Use different parameters $s$ and $t$, and match components.

Show solution
  1. $2 + s = 5 - t$ and $1 + 2s = t$.
  2. $2 + s = 4 - 2s$, so $s = \dfrac{2}{3}$, $t = \dfrac{7}{3}$.
  3. Point $\left(\dfrac{8}{3}, \dfrac{7}{3}\right)$.

$\left(\dfrac{8}{3}, \dfrac{7}{3}\right)$

Problem 5

Find the acute angle between the lines $2x - y + 3 = 0$ and $x - 3y - 1 = 0$.

Uses: Line Perpendicular to a Vector

Show hint

The normals are $(2, -1)$ and $(1, -3)$. The angle between the lines equals the angle between the normals (or its supplement).

Show solution
  1. $\cos\theta = \dfrac{2 + 3}{\sqrt{5}\sqrt{10}} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$.
  2. $\theta = 45^\circ$.

$45^\circ$

Problem 6

Use vectors to find the foot H of the perpendicular from P$(4, 5)$ to the line $x + 2y - 4 = 0$, and the distance PH.

Uses: Line Perpendicular to a Vector, Vector Equation of a Line

Show hint

$\vec{n} = (1, 2)$ is normal to the line, so H $= $ P $+ k\vec{n}$ for some $k$.

Show solution
  1. H $= (4 + k, 5 + 2k)$ lies on the line: $(4 + k) + 2(5 + 2k) - 4 = 0$, so $k = -2$.
  2. H $= (2, 1)$ and $PH = |k||\vec{n}| = 2\sqrt{5}$.

H$(2, 1)$; $PH = 2\sqrt{5}$

Problem 7

O is the origin, A$(4, 0)$ and B$(1, 3)$.

  1. (1) Write line AB in parametric form and as an equation in $x$ and $y$.
  2. (2) Describe the points P with $\overrightarrow{OP} = s\overrightarrow{OA} + t\overrightarrow{OB}$ when $s + t = 1$, $s, t \ge 0$; and when $s + t = 2$.
  3. (3) H is the foot of the perpendicular from O to line AB. Find H and OH. (Type OH in the box.)
  4. (4) Find the area of triangle OAB using (3).

Uses: Line Through Two Points, Vector Equation of a Line, Line Perpendicular to a Vector

Show hint

(3) Write $\overrightarrow{OH} = (1 - t)\overrightarrow{OA} + t\overrightarrow{OB}$ and use $\overrightarrow{OH} \cdot \overrightarrow{AB} = 0$.

Show solution
  1. (1) $\overrightarrow{AB} = (-3, 3)$: $x = 4 - 3t$, $y = 3t$, i.e. $x + y = 4$.
  2. (2) $s + t = 1$, $s, t \ge 0$: the segment AB. $s + t = 2$: write $\overrightarrow{OP} = \dfrac{s}{2}(2\overrightarrow{OA}) + \dfrac{t}{2}(2\overrightarrow{OB})$; P runs along the line through $(8, 0)$ and $(2, 6)$, i.e. $x + y = 8$.
  3. (3) $\overrightarrow{OH} = (4 - 3t, 3t)$; $(4 - 3t)(-3) + 3t \cdot 3 = 0$ gives $t = \dfrac{2}{3}$. H $= (2, 2)$, $OH = 2\sqrt{2}$.
  4. (4) $AB = 3\sqrt{2}$: area $= \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 3\sqrt{2} \cdot 2\sqrt{2} = 6$.

(1) $x + y = 4$ (2) segment AB; the line $x + y = 8$ (3) H$(2, 2)$, $OH = 2\sqrt{2}$ (4) $6$
