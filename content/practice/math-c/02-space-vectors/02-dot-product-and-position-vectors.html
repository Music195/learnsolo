Dot Product and Position Vectors in Space

Seven problems on the dot product in space, perpendicular vectors, and position vectors. Try each one first. Open the hint if you are stuck, then compare with the full solution.

Problem 1

Find the angle between $\vec{a} = (2, 1, 1)$ and $\vec{b} = (1, -1, 2)$.

Uses: Dot Product

Show hint

$\cos\theta = \dfrac{\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}}{|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|}$.

Show solution
  1. $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = 2 - 1 + 2 = 3$; $|\vec{a}| = |\vec{b}| = \sqrt{6}$.
  2. $\cos\theta = \dfrac{3}{6} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, so $\theta = 60^\circ$.

$60^\circ$

Problem 2

$\vec{a} = (3, x, -2)$ and $\vec{b} = (x, 1, 4)$ are perpendicular. Find $x$.

Uses: Perpendicular Vectors

Show hint

$\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = 0$.

Show solution
  1. $3x + x - 8 = 0$, so $x = 2$.

$x = 2$

Problem 3

A$(2, -1, 3)$, B$(0, 5, 1)$ and C$(4, 2, -1)$. Find the midpoint M of AB, the centroid G of triangle ABC, and the length MG. (Type MG in the box.)

Uses: Position Vectors

Show hint

$\dfrac{\vec{a} + \vec{b}}{2}$ and $\dfrac{\vec{a} + \vec{b} + \vec{c}}{3}$.

Show solution
  1. M $= (1, 2, 2)$, G $= (2, 2, 1)$.
  2. $\overrightarrow{MG} = (1, 0, -1)$, $MG = \sqrt{2}$. (Check: $MC = 3\sqrt{2} = 3MG$, as G divides the median MC in the ratio $1 : 2$ from M.)

M$(1, 2, 2)$, G$(2, 2, 1)$, $MG = \sqrt{2}$

Problem 4

Find the area of the triangle with vertices A$(1, 1, 0)$, B$(2, 3, 1)$ and C$(0, 2, 2)$.

Uses: Dot Product

Show hint

$S = \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{|\overrightarrow{AB}|^2|\overrightarrow{AC}|^2 - (\overrightarrow{AB} \cdot \overrightarrow{AC})^2}$.

Show solution
  1. $\overrightarrow{AB} = (1, 2, 1)$, $\overrightarrow{AC} = (-1, 1, 2)$: lengths squared $6$ and $6$, dot product $3$.
  2. $S = \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{36 - 9} = \dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}$.

$\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}$

Problem 5

Find the unit vectors perpendicular to both $\vec{a} = (1, 0, 1)$ and $\vec{b} = (2, 1, 0)$. (Type the $x$-component of the one with positive $x$-component in the box.)

Uses: Perpendicular Vectors

Show hint

Let $\vec{n} = (x, y, z)$ with $\vec{n} \cdot \vec{a} = \vec{n} \cdot \vec{b} = 0$, and $|\vec{n}| = 1$.

Show solution
  1. $x + z = 0$ and $2x + y = 0$: $\vec{n} = x(1, -2, -1)$.
  2. $|\vec{n}| = |x|\sqrt{6} = 1$: $\vec{n} = \pm\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{6}}(1, -2, -1)$.
  3. Positive $x$-component: $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{6}} = \dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{6}$.

$\pm\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{6}}(1, -2, -1)$

Problem 6

In tetrahedron OABC, $\vec{a} = \overrightarrow{OA}$, $\vec{b} = \overrightarrow{OB}$, $\vec{c} = \overrightarrow{OC}$. P is the point with $\overrightarrow{OP} = 2\vec{a} + 3\vec{b} + \vec{c}$, and line OP meets plane ABC at Q. Find $\overrightarrow{OQ}$ and $OQ : QP$. (Type $\dfrac{OQ}{QP}$ in the box.)

Uses: Position Vectors

Show hint

$\overrightarrow{OQ} = k\overrightarrow{OP}$, and Q is on plane ABC exactly when the coefficients add up to $1$.

Show solution
  1. $\overrightarrow{OQ} = 2k\vec{a} + 3k\vec{b} + k\vec{c}$ with $6k = 1$: $\overrightarrow{OQ} = \dfrac{2\vec{a} + 3\vec{b} + \vec{c}}{6}$.
  2. $OQ = \dfrac{1}{6}OP$, so $OQ : QP = 1 : 5$.

$\overrightarrow{OQ} = \dfrac{2\vec{a} + 3\vec{b} + \vec{c}}{6}$; $OQ : QP = 1 : 5$

Problem 7

O is the origin, A$(2, 0, 0)$, B$(0, 2, 0)$ and C$(0, 0, 2)$.

  1. (1) Find $\angle BAC$ using the dot product. What kind of triangle is ABC?
  2. (2) G is the centroid of triangle ABC. Show that $\overrightarrow{OG}$ is perpendicular to both $\overrightarrow{AB}$ and $\overrightarrow{AC}$.
  3. (3) Find OG, the distance from O to plane ABC. (Type it in the box.)
  4. (4) Use (3) to find the volume of tetrahedron OABC, and check it another way.

Uses: Dot Product, Perpendicular Vectors, Position Vectors

Show hint

(2) A vector perpendicular to two non-parallel vectors in a plane is perpendicular to the whole plane.

Show solution
  1. (1) $\overrightarrow{AB} = (-2, 2, 0)$, $\overrightarrow{AC} = (-2, 0, 2)$: dot product $4$, lengths $2\sqrt{2}$. $\cos\angle BAC = \dfrac{4}{8} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, so $60^\circ$; with $AB = AC$ the triangle is equilateral.
  2. (2) G $= \left(\dfrac{2}{3}, \dfrac{2}{3}, \dfrac{2}{3}\right)$. $\overrightarrow{OG} \cdot \overrightarrow{AB} = \dfrac{2}{3}(-2 + 2) = 0$ and $\overrightarrow{OG} \cdot \overrightarrow{AC} = \dfrac{2}{3}(-2 + 2) = 0$.
  3. (3) $OG = \dfrac{2}{3}\sqrt{3} = \dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}$.
  4. (4) Area of ABC $= \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}(2\sqrt{2})^2 = 2\sqrt{3}$; $V = \dfrac{1}{3} \cdot 2\sqrt{3} \cdot \dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{3} = \dfrac{4}{3}$. Check: $V = \dfrac{1}{3} \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 = \dfrac{4}{3}$ ✓.

(1) $60^\circ$, equilateral (3) $\dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}$ (4) $\dfrac{4}{3}$
