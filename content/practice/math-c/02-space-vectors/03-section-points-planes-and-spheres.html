Section Points, Planes and Spheres

Seven problems on distances and section points in space, planes parallel to the coordinate planes, and spheres. Try each one first. Open the hint if you are stuck, then compare with the full solution.

Problem 1

A$(-1, 2, 4)$ and B$(5, -1, 1)$. Find AB and the point dividing AB internally in the ratio $2 : 1$. (Type AB in the box.)

Uses: Distance and Section Points

Show hint

Internal $m : n$: $\dfrac{n\vec{a} + m\vec{b}}{m + n}$.

Show solution
  1. $AB = \sqrt{36 + 9 + 9} = \sqrt{54} = 3\sqrt{6}$.
  2. $\dfrac{\vec{a} + 2\vec{b}}{3} = \dfrac{(9, 0, 6)}{3} = (3, 0, 2)$.

$3\sqrt{6}$; $(3, 0, 2)$

Problem 2

Find the plane through $(2, -3, 5)$ parallel to the $zx$-plane, and the plane through it perpendicular to the $z$-axis. Then find the distance from $(1, 4, -2)$ to the first plane. (Type the distance in the box.)

Uses: Planes Parallel to Coordinate Planes

Show hint

Parallel to the $zx$-plane means $y$ is constant.

Show solution
  1. Parallel to the $zx$-plane: $y = -3$. Perpendicular to the $z$-axis: $z = 5$.
  2. Distance from $(1, 4, -2)$ to $y = -3$: $|4 - (-3)| = 7$.

$y = -3$; $z = 5$; distance $7$

Problem 3

Find the centre and radius of the sphere $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 - 4x + 6y - 8z + 4 = 0$.

Uses: Equation of a Sphere

Show hint

Complete the square in each variable.

Show solution
  1. $(x - 2)^2 + (y + 3)^2 + (z - 4)^2 = 4 + 9 + 16 - 4 = 25$.
  2. Centre $(2, -3, 4)$, radius $5$.

Centre $(2, -3, 4)$, radius $5$

Problem 4

The sphere $(x - 1)^2 + (y - 2)^2 + (z + 3)^2 = 25$ meets the plane $z = 1$ in a circle. Find its centre and radius.

Uses: Equation of a Sphere, Planes Parallel to Coordinate Planes

Show hint

Put $z = 1$. The distance from the centre of the sphere to the plane is $|1 - (-3)|$.

Show solution
  1. $z = 1$: $(x - 1)^2 + (y - 2)^2 = 25 - 16 = 9$.
  2. Centre $(1, 2, 1)$, radius $3$.

Centre $(1, 2, 1)$, radius $3$

Problem 5

A$(1, 3, -2)$ and B$(4, 0, 1)$. Find the point dividing AB externally in the ratio $2 : 1$, and the point where line AB meets the $xy$-plane. (Type the $x$-coordinate of the second point in the box.)

Uses: Distance and Section Points, Planes Parallel to Coordinate Planes

Show hint

External $m : n$: $\dfrac{-n\vec{a} + m\vec{b}}{m - n}$. A point on line AB is $\vec{a} + t(\vec{b} - \vec{a})$; the $xy$-plane is $z = 0$.

Show solution
  1. External $2 : 1$: $-\vec{a} + 2\vec{b} = (7, -3, 4)$.
  2. $\vec{a} + t(3, -3, 3) = (1 + 3t, 3 - 3t, -2 + 3t)$; $z = 0$ gives $t = \dfrac{2}{3}$: the point $(3, 1, 0)$.

$(7, -3, 4)$; $(3, 1, 0)$

Problem 6

Find the sphere through O, A$(2, 0, 0)$, B$(0, 4, 0)$ and C$(0, 0, 6)$, and its radius.

Uses: Equation of a Sphere

Show hint

Write it as $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 + ax + by + cz + d = 0$ and substitute the four points.

Show solution
  1. O: $d = 0$. A: $4 + 2a = 0$, $a = -2$. B: $16 + 4b = 0$, $b = -4$. C: $36 + 6c = 0$, $c = -6$.
  2. $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 - 2x - 4y - 6z = 0 \iff (x - 1)^2 + (y - 2)^2 + (z - 3)^2 = 14$.
  3. Centre $(1, 2, 3)$, radius $\sqrt{14}$.

$(x - 1)^2 + (y - 2)^2 + (z - 3)^2 = 14$; radius $\sqrt{14}$

Problem 7

A$(1, -1, 3)$ and B$(5, 3, 1)$. S is the sphere with diameter AB.

  1. (1) Find the equation of S.
  2. (2) S meets the $xy$-plane in a circle. Find its centre and radius. (Type the radius in the box.)
  3. (3) For which $k$ does the plane $x = k$ touch S?
  4. (4) P divides AB externally in the ratio $3 : 1$. Find P and its distance from the centre of S. Is P inside S?

Uses: Equation of a Sphere, Planes Parallel to Coordinate Planes, Distance and Section Points

Show hint

The centre is the midpoint of AB and the radius is $\dfrac{AB}{2}$. A plane touches the sphere when its distance from the centre equals the radius.

Show solution
  1. (1) Centre $(3, 1, 2)$, $AB = \sqrt{16 + 16 + 4} = 6$, radius $3$: $(x - 3)^2 + (y - 1)^2 + (z - 2)^2 = 9$.
  2. (2) $z = 0$: $(x - 3)^2 + (y - 1)^2 = 5$. Centre $(3, 1, 0)$, radius $\sqrt{5}$.
  3. (3) $|k - 3| = 3$: $k = 0$ or $6$.
  4. (4) P $= \dfrac{-\vec{a} + 3\vec{b}}{2} = \dfrac{(14, 10, 0)}{2} = (7, 5, 0)$. Distance from $(3, 1, 2)$: $\sqrt{16 + 16 + 4} = 6 > 3$, so P is outside S.

(1) $(x - 3)^2 + (y - 1)^2 + (z - 2)^2 = 9$ (2) centre $(3, 1, 0)$, radius $\sqrt{5}$ (3) $k = 0, 6$ (4) P$(7, 5, 0)$, distance $6$, outside
