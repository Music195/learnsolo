Parabolas, Ellipses and Hyperbolas

Six problems on the focus and directrix of a parabola, and the foci, axes and asymptotes of ellipses and hyperbolas. Try each one first. Open the hint if you are stuck, then compare with the full solution.

Problem 1

Find the focus and directrix of $y^2 = -6x$. P is the point on it with $x = -4$. Find the distance from P to the focus.

Uses: Parabolas

Show hint

Compare with $y^2 = 4px$. The distance to the focus equals the distance to the directrix.

Show solution
  1. $4p = -6$, $p = -\dfrac{3}{2}$: focus $\left(-\dfrac{3}{2}, 0\right)$, directrix $x = \dfrac{3}{2}$.
  2. $PF = $ distance to the directrix $= \dfrac{3}{2} - (-4) = \dfrac{11}{2}$.

Focus $\left(-\dfrac{3}{2}, 0\right)$, directrix $x = \dfrac{3}{2}$; $PF = \dfrac{11}{2}$

Problem 2

For the ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{16} + \dfrac{y^2}{25} = 1$, find the foci and the lengths of the major and minor axes. (Type the distance between the foci in the box.)

Uses: Ellipses

Show hint

The larger denominator is under $y^2$, so the major axis is vertical and the foci are on the $y$-axis.

Show solution
  1. $\sqrt{25 - 16} = 3$: foci $(0, \pm 3)$, $3 - (-3) = 6$ apart.
  2. Major axis $2 \cdot 5 = 10$, minor axis $2 \cdot 4 = 8$.

Foci $(0, \pm 3)$; axes $10$ and $8$

Problem 3

For the hyperbola $\dfrac{x^2}{4} - \dfrac{y^2}{12} = 1$, find the foci and asymptotes. P is a point on the right branch with $PF = 7$, where F is the focus with positive $x$. Find $PF'$.

Uses: Hyperbolas

Show hint

$c = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$ and $|PF - PF'| = 2a$. On the right branch P is nearer to F.

Show solution
  1. $c = \sqrt{4 + 12} = 4$: foci $(\pm 4, 0)$. Asymptotes $y = \pm\sqrt{3}x$.
  2. On the right branch $PF' - PF = 2a = 4$, so $PF' = 11$.

Foci $(\pm 4, 0)$, asymptotes $y = \pm\sqrt{3}x$; $PF' = 11$

Problem 4

Find the parabola with focus $(2, 1)$ and directrix $x = -4$, and its vertex. (Type the $x$-coordinate of the vertex in the box.)

Uses: Parabolas

Show hint

Distance to the focus $=$ distance to the directrix. The vertex is halfway between the focus and the directrix.

Show solution
  1. $(x - 2)^2 + (y - 1)^2 = (x + 4)^2 \iff (y - 1)^2 = 12x + 12 = 12(x + 1)$.
  2. Vertex $(-1, 1)$.

$(y - 1)^2 = 12(x + 1)$; vertex $(-1, 1)$

Problem 5

An ellipse has foci $(\pm 4, 0)$ and passes through P$\left(4, \dfrac{9}{5}\right)$. Use $PF + PF' = 2a$ to find its equation. (Type $b^2$ in the box.)

Uses: Ellipses

Show hint

$PF$ is vertical; find $PF'$ with the distance formula. Then $b^2 = a^2 - c^2$.

Show solution
  1. $PF = \dfrac{9}{5}$; $PF' = \sqrt{8^2 + \left(\dfrac{9}{5}\right)^2} = \sqrt{\dfrac{1681}{25}} = \dfrac{41}{5}$.
  2. $2a = \dfrac{50}{5} = 10$, so $a = 5$ and $b^2 = 25 - 16 = 9$.
  3. $\dfrac{x^2}{25} + \dfrac{y^2}{9} = 1$.

$\dfrac{x^2}{25} + \dfrac{y^2}{9} = 1$

Problem 6

E is the ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{25} + \dfrac{y^2}{9} = 1$, with foci F (positive $x$) and F'.

  1. (1) Find F and F'.
  2. (2) H is the hyperbola with the same foci and asymptotes $y = \pm\dfrac{\sqrt{7}}{3}x$. Find its equation.
  3. (3) E and H meet at P in the first quadrant. Find PF and PF'.
  4. (4) Find $\cos\angle FPF'$. (Type it in the box.)

Uses: Ellipses, Hyperbolas

Show hint

(3) P is on both curves: $PF + PF' = 10$ and $PF' - PF = 2a_H$. (4) Use the law of cosines with $FF' = 8$.

Show solution
  1. (1) $c = \sqrt{25 - 9} = 4$: F$(4, 0)$, F'$(-4, 0)$.
  2. (2) $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2} - \dfrac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$ with $\dfrac{b}{a} = \dfrac{\sqrt{7}}{3}$ and $a^2 + b^2 = 16$: $a^2 \cdot \dfrac{16}{9} = 16$, $a^2 = 9$, $b^2 = 7$. $\dfrac{x^2}{9} - \dfrac{y^2}{7} = 1$.
  3. (3) $PF + PF' = 10$ and $PF' - PF = 6$ (P on the right branch): $PF = 2$, $PF' = 8$.
  4. (4) $\cos\angle FPF' = \dfrac{2^2 + 8^2 - 8^2}{2 \cdot 2 \cdot 8} = \dfrac{1}{8}$.

(1) $(\pm 4, 0)$ (2) $\dfrac{x^2}{9} - \dfrac{y^2}{7} = 1$ (3) $PF = 2$, $PF' = 8$ (4) $\dfrac{1}{8}$
