Points, Lines and Circles

Five problems on distances, dividing points, perpendicular lines and circles. Try each one first. Open the hint if you are stuck, then compare with the full solution.

Problem 1

Find the distance between A$(1, 2)$ and B$(4, 6)$.

Uses: Distance Formula

Show hint

$AB = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$.

Show solution
  1. $AB = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{25} = 5$.

$AB = 5$

Problem 2

Find the point P dividing A$(-1, 3)$ and B$(5, -3)$ internally in the ratio $2 : 1$.

Uses: Section Formula (2D)

Show hint

Dividing internally in $m : n$: $\left(\dfrac{nx_1 + mx_2}{m + n}, \dfrac{ny_1 + my_2}{m + n}\right)$.

Show solution
  1. $x = \dfrac{1 \cdot (-1) + 2 \cdot 5}{3} = 3$.
  2. $y = \dfrac{1 \cdot 3 + 2 \cdot (-3)}{3} = -1$.

P$(3, -1)$

Problem 3

Find the line through $(1, 2)$ perpendicular to $2x - y + 3 = 0$.

Uses: Parallel and Perpendicular Lines

Show hint

The given line has slope $2$. Perpendicular slopes multiply to $-1$.

Show solution
  1. $y = 2x + 3$ has slope $2$, so the perpendicular slope is $-\dfrac{1}{2}$.
  2. $y - 2 = -\dfrac{1}{2}(x - 1)$; multiply by $2$: $2y - 4 = -x + 1$.

$x + 2y - 5 = 0$

Problem 4

Find the distance from the point $(2, 1)$ to the line $3x + 4y - 5 = 0$.

Uses: Point–Line Distance

Show hint

$d = \dfrac{|ax_1 + by_1 + c|}{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}}$.

Show solution
  1. $d = \dfrac{|6 + 4 - 5|}{\sqrt{9 + 16}} = \dfrac{5}{5} = 1$.

$d = 1$

Problem 5

Find the length of the chord cut from the circle $x^2 + y^2 - 4x + 2y - 4 = 0$ by the line $x - y - 1 = 0$.

Uses: Circle Equation, Circle and Line Position, Point–Line Distance

Show hint

Find the centre and radius. The distance $d$ from the centre to the line and half the chord form a right triangle with the radius.

Show solution
  1. $(x - 2)^2 + (y + 1)^2 = 9$: centre $(2, -1)$, radius $3$.
  2. $d = \dfrac{|2 + 1 - 1|}{\sqrt{2}} = \sqrt{2}$.
  3. Half the chord is $\sqrt{3^2 - (\sqrt{2})^2} = \sqrt{7}$, so the chord is $2\sqrt{7}$.

Chord length $= 2\sqrt{7}$
