Equations of Lines

Six problems on the equation of a line, parallel and perpendicular lines, and the distance from a point to a line. Try each one first. Open the hint if you are stuck, then compare with the full solution.

Problem 1

Find the line through $(-2, 5)$ with slope $-\dfrac{3}{2}$, in the form $y = mx + n$ and in the form $ax + by + c = 0$. (Type the $y$-intercept in the box.)

Uses: Line: Point-Slope Form

Show hint

$y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$.

Show solution
  1. $y - 5 = -\dfrac{3}{2}(x + 2) = -\dfrac{3}{2}x - 3$, so $y = -\dfrac{3}{2}x + 2$.
  2. Multiply by $2$: $3x + 2y - 4 = 0$.

$y = -\dfrac{3}{2}x + 2$, i.e. $3x + 2y - 4 = 0$

Problem 2

Find the line through $(1, 1)$ and $(4, 7)$, and the line through $(2, -3)$ and $(2, 5)$. (Type the $y$-intercept of the first line in the box.)

Uses: Line: Two-Point Form

Show hint

Slope $= \dfrac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$. If the two $x$-coordinates are equal, the line is vertical.

Show solution
  1. Slope $\dfrac{7 - 1}{4 - 1} = 2$: $y - 1 = 2(x - 1)$, so $y = 2x - 1$.
  2. Both points have $x = 2$: the line is $x = 2$.

$y = 2x - 1$; $x = 2$

Problem 3

The lines $ax + 3y + 1 = 0$ and $2x + (a + 1)y - 4 = 0$ are given. Find $a$ so that they are (i) perpendicular (ii) parallel. (Type the answer to (i) in the box.)

Uses: Parallel and Perpendicular Lines

Show hint

For $a_1x + b_1y + c_1 = 0$ and $a_2x + b_2y + c_2 = 0$: perpendicular $\iff a_1a_2 + b_1b_2 = 0$, parallel $\iff a_1b_2 - a_2b_1 = 0$.

Show solution
  1. (i) $2a + 3(a + 1) = 0$, so $a = -\dfrac{3}{5}$.
  2. (ii) $a(a + 1) - 2 \cdot 3 = 0 \iff a^2 + a - 6 = 0 \iff a = 2$ or $a = -3$.
  3. Check they are not the same line: $a = 2$ gives $2x + 3y + 1 = 0$ and $2x + 3y - 4 = 0$; $a = -3$ gives $x - y - \dfrac{1}{3} = 0$ and $x - y - 2 = 0$. Both pairs are distinct.

(i) $a = -\dfrac{3}{5}$ (ii) $a = 2$ or $a = -3$

Problem 4

Find the distance from the point $(1, -2)$ to the line $5x - 12y + 4 = 0$.

Uses: Distance from a Point to a Line

Show hint

$d = \dfrac{|ax_1 + by_1 + c|}{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}}$.

Show solution
  1. $d = \dfrac{|5 \cdot 1 - 12 \cdot (-2) + 4|}{\sqrt{25 + 144}} = \dfrac{33}{13}$.

$\dfrac{33}{13}$

Problem 5

Find the mirror image $B$ of the point $A(4, 1)$ in the line $\ell$: $2x - y + 3 = 0$. (Type the $x$-coordinate of $B$ in the box.)

Uses: Parallel and Perpendicular Lines, Distance from a Point to a Line

Show hint

Let $B(p, q)$. Two conditions: $AB \perp \ell$, and the midpoint of $AB$ lies on $\ell$.

Show solution
  1. $\ell$ has slope $2$, so $AB$ has slope $-\dfrac{1}{2}$: $\dfrac{q - 1}{p - 4} = -\dfrac{1}{2}$, i.e. $p + 2q = 6$.
  2. Midpoint $\left(\dfrac{p + 4}{2}, \dfrac{q + 1}{2}\right)$ on $\ell$: $(p + 4) - \dfrac{q + 1}{2} + 3 = 0$, i.e. $2p - q = -13$.
  3. Solving: $p = -4$, $q = 5$. So $B(-4, 5)$.
  4. Check: $AB = \sqrt{64 + 16} = 4\sqrt{5}$, twice the distance $\dfrac{|8 - 1 + 3|}{\sqrt{5}} = 2\sqrt{5}$ from $A$ to $\ell$ ✓.

$B(-4, 5)$

Problem 6

$O(0, 0)$, $A(6, 2)$ and $B(0, 5)$.

  1. (1) Find the equation of the line AB.
  2. (2) Find the foot H of the perpendicular from O to AB.
  3. (3) Find the distance from O to AB.
  4. (4) Find the area of triangle OAB. (Type it in the box.)

Uses: Line: Two-Point Form, Line: Point-Slope Form, Parallel and Perpendicular Lines, Distance from a Point to a Line

Show hint

For (2), the line OH is perpendicular to AB and passes through O. For (4), use base AB and height OH.

Show solution
  1. (1) Slope $\dfrac{5 - 2}{0 - 6} = -\dfrac{1}{2}$: $y - 5 = -\dfrac{1}{2}x$, i.e. $x + 2y - 10 = 0$.
  2. (2) OH has slope $2$: $y = 2x$. With $x + 2y = 10$: $5x = 10$, so $H(2, 4)$.
  3. (3) $\dfrac{|0 + 0 - 10|}{\sqrt{1 + 4}} = \dfrac{10}{\sqrt{5}} = 2\sqrt{5}$ (also $OH = \sqrt{4 + 16}$ ✓).
  4. (4) $AB = \sqrt{36 + 9} = 3\sqrt{5}$, area $= \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 3\sqrt{5} \cdot 2\sqrt{5} = 15$.

(1) $x + 2y - 10 = 0$ (2) $H(2, 4)$ (3) $2\sqrt{5}$ (4) $15$

Check with the formula for a triangle with a vertex at the origin: $\dfrac{1}{2}|6 \cdot 5 - 2 \cdot 0| = 15$ ✓.
