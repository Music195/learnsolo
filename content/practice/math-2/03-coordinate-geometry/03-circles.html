Circles and Lines

Six problems on the equation of a circle, where a circle and a line meet, tangents and chord lengths. Try each one first. Open the hint if you are stuck, then compare with the full solution.

Problem 1

Find the centre and the radius of the circle $x^2 + y^2 + 6x - 2y - 6 = 0$.

Uses: Equation of a Circle

Show hint

Complete the square in $x$ and in $y$.

Show solution
  1. $(x + 3)^2 - 9 + (y - 1)^2 - 1 - 6 = 0$, so $(x + 3)^2 + (y - 1)^2 = 16$.
  2. Centre $(-3, 1)$, radius $4$.

Centre $(-3, 1)$, radius $4$

Problem 2

Find the equation of the circle with diameter AB, where $A(-1, 3)$ and $B(5, -5)$. (Type the radius in the box.)

Uses: Equation of a Circle

Show hint

The centre is the midpoint of AB, and the radius is half of AB.

Show solution
  1. Centre: $\left(\dfrac{-1 + 5}{2}, \dfrac{3 - 5}{2}\right) = (2, -1)$.
  2. Radius: $\dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = 5$.
  3. $(x - 2)^2 + (y + 1)^2 = 25$.

$(x - 2)^2 + (y + 1)^2 = 25$

Problem 3

Find the common points of the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 13$ and the line $y = x + 1$, and the length of the chord between them.

Uses: Circle and Line: Discriminant Method

Show hint

Substitute $y = x + 1$ into the circle and solve the quadratic in $x$.

Show solution
  1. $x^2 + (x + 1)^2 = 13 \iff 2x^2 + 2x - 12 = 0 \iff (x + 3)(x - 2) = 0$.
  2. $x = 2$: $(2, 3)$. $\;x = -3$: $(-3, -2)$.
  3. Chord: $\sqrt{5^2 + 5^2} = 5\sqrt{2}$.

$(2, 3)$ and $(-3, -2)$; chord $5\sqrt{2}$

Problem 4

Find the tangent to the circle $(x - 1)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = 10$ at the point $(4, -1)$. (Type its $y$-intercept in the box.)

Uses: Tangent to a Circle

Show hint

For $(x - a)^2 + (y - b)^2 = r^2$, the tangent at $(x_1, y_1)$ is $(x_1 - a)(x - a) + (y_1 - b)(y - b) = r^2$.

Show solution
  1. Check the point is on the circle: $3^2 + 1^2 = 10$ ✓.
  2. $(4 - 1)(x - 1) + (-1 + 2)(y + 2) = 10 \iff 3x - 3 + y + 2 = 10 \iff 3x + y - 11 = 0$.
  3. $y = -3x + 11$: $y$-intercept $11$.

$3x + y - 11 = 0$

Problem 5

Find $k$ so that the line $y = kx + 4$ touches the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 8$. Solve it with the distance method, then check with the discriminant. (Type the positive value in the box.)

Uses: Circle and Line: Distance Method, Circle and Line: Discriminant Method

Show hint

Touching means the distance from the centre $(0, 0)$ to the line equals the radius $2\sqrt{2}$.

Show solution
  1. Line: $kx - y + 4 = 0$. Distance $\dfrac{4}{\sqrt{k^2 + 1}} = 2\sqrt{2}$, so $k^2 + 1 = 2$, $k = \pm 1$.
  2. Check: $x^2 + (kx + 4)^2 = 8 \iff (1 + k^2)x^2 + 8kx + 8 = 0$, and $\dfrac{D}{4} = 16k^2 - 8(1 + k^2) = 8k^2 - 8 = 0$ gives $k = \pm 1$ ✓.

$k = \pm 1$

Problem 6

The circle $C$: $x^2 + y^2 - 4x - 2y - 4 = 0$ and the line $\ell$: $3x + 4y + k = 0$ are given.

  1. (1) Find the centre and the radius of $C$.
  2. (2) Find the range of $k$ for which $C$ and $\ell$ meet at two points.
  3. (3) When $k = -5$, find the length of the chord cut from $\ell$ by $C$. (Type it in the box.)
  4. (4) Find the tangent to $C$ at the point $(5, 1)$.

Uses: Equation of a Circle, Circle and Line: Distance Method, Tangent to a Circle

Show hint

Two points $\iff$ distance from the centre to $\ell$ $<$ radius. Chord $= 2\sqrt{r^2 - d^2}$.

Show solution
  1. (1) $(x - 2)^2 + (y - 1)^2 = 9$: centre $(2, 1)$, radius $3$.
  2. (2) $d = \dfrac{|6 + 4 + k|}{5} < 3 \iff |k + 10| < 15 \iff -25 < k < 5$.
  3. (3) $k = -5$: $d = \dfrac{5}{5} = 1$, chord $= 2\sqrt{9 - 1} = 4\sqrt{2}$.
  4. (4) $(5 - 2)(x - 2) + (1 - 1)(y - 1) = 9 \iff x = 5$.

(1) $(2, 1)$, $3$ (2) $-25 < k < 5$ (3) $4\sqrt{2}$ (4) $x = 5$
