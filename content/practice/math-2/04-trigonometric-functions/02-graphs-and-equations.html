Graphs, Angle Rules and Equations

Six problems on graphs of trigonometric functions, the angle transformation rules, and trigonometric equations and inequalities. Try each one first. Open the hint if you are stuck, then compare with the full solution.

Problem 1

Find the period and the range of $y = 2\sin 3\theta - 1$. (Type the period in the box.)

Uses: Graphs of Trigonometric Functions

Show hint

$y = a\sin k\theta$ has period $\dfrac{2\pi}{|k|}$ and goes from $-|a|$ to $|a|$; the $-1$ moves the graph down.

Show solution
  1. Period $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$.
  2. $-2 \le 2\sin 3\theta \le 2$, so $-3 \le y \le 1$.

Period $\dfrac{2}{3}\pi$; range $-3 \le y \le 1$

Problem 2

Find $\sin\dfrac{17}{6}\pi + \cos\left(-\dfrac{4}{3}\pi\right) + \tan\dfrac{11}{4}\pi$.

Uses: Angle Transformation Rules

Show hint

Remove whole turns ($2\pi$ for sin and cos, $\pi$ for tan), and use $\cos(-\theta) = \cos\theta$.

Show solution
  1. $\sin\dfrac{17}{6}\pi = \sin\dfrac{5}{6}\pi = \dfrac{1}{2}$.
  2. $\cos\left(-\dfrac{4}{3}\pi\right) = \cos\dfrac{4}{3}\pi = -\dfrac{1}{2}$.
  3. $\tan\dfrac{11}{4}\pi = \tan\dfrac{3}{4}\pi = -1$.
  4. Total: $\dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{1}{2} - 1 = -1$.

$-1$

Problem 3

Solve $2\cos\theta + \sqrt{3} = 0$ for $0 \le \theta < 2\pi$. (Type the smaller solution in the box.)

Uses: Trigonometric Equations and Inequalities

Show hint

$\cos\theta = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$: where does the line $x = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ meet the unit circle?

Show solution
  1. $\cos\theta = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.
  2. On the unit circle: $\theta = \dfrac{5}{6}\pi$ (second quadrant) and $\theta = \dfrac{7}{6}\pi$ (third quadrant).

$\theta = \dfrac{5}{6}\pi, \dfrac{7}{6}\pi$

Problem 4

Find the period of $y = 2\sin\left(2\theta - \dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)$, describe how its graph is obtained from $y = 2\sin 2\theta$, and find $y$ at $\theta = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$. (Type the value in the box.)

Uses: Graphs of Trigonometric Functions, Angle Transformation Rules

Show hint

Write $2\theta - \dfrac{\pi}{3} = 2\left(\theta - \dfrac{\pi}{6}\right)$: the shift is $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$, not $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$.

Show solution
  1. Period $\dfrac{2\pi}{2} = \pi$.
  2. $y = 2\sin 2\left(\theta - \dfrac{\pi}{6}\right)$: move $y = 2\sin 2\theta$ by $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ to the right.
  3. At $\theta = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$: $2\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2} - \dfrac{\pi}{3}\right) = 2\sin\dfrac{\pi}{6} = 1$.

Period $\pi$; shift $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ to the right; $y = 1$

Problem 5

Solve $2\sin^2\theta + 3\cos\theta \ge 0$ for $0 \le \theta < 2\pi$.

Uses: Trigonometric Equations and Inequalities

Show hint

Use $\sin^2\theta = 1 - \cos^2\theta$ to get an inequality in $\cos\theta$ only, and remember $-1 \le \cos\theta \le 1$.

Show solution
  1. $2(1 - \cos^2\theta) + 3\cos\theta \ge 0 \iff 2\cos^2\theta - 3\cos\theta - 2 \le 0$.
  2. $(2\cos\theta + 1)(\cos\theta - 2) \le 0$. Since $\cos\theta - 2 < 0$ always, this means $2\cos\theta + 1 \ge 0$.
  3. $\cos\theta \ge -\dfrac{1}{2}$: $0 \le \theta \le \dfrac{2}{3}\pi$ or $\dfrac{4}{3}\pi \le \theta < 2\pi$.

$0 \le \theta \le \dfrac{2}{3}\pi$, $\;\dfrac{4}{3}\pi \le \theta < 2\pi$

Problem 6

$y = 2\cos^2\theta - 2\sin(\pi + \theta)$ for $0 \le \theta < 2\pi$.

  1. (1) Let $t = \sin\theta$. Write $y$ in terms of $t$.
  2. (2) Find the range of $t$.
  3. (3) Find the largest value of $y$ and the values of $\theta$ that give it. (Type the largest value in the box.)
  4. (4) Find the smallest value of $y$ and the value of $\theta$ that gives it.

Uses: Angle Transformation Rules, Trigonometric Identities, Trigonometric Equations and Inequalities

Show hint

$\sin(\pi + \theta) = -\sin\theta$ and $\cos^2\theta = 1 - \sin^2\theta$. Then complete the square in $t$.

Show solution
  1. (1) $y = 2(1 - t^2) + 2t = -2t^2 + 2t + 2$.
  2. (2) $-1 \le t \le 1$.
  3. (3) $y = -2\left(t - \dfrac{1}{2}\right)^2 + \dfrac{5}{2}$: largest $\dfrac{5}{2}$ at $t = \dfrac{1}{2}$, i.e. $\theta = \dfrac{\pi}{6}, \dfrac{5}{6}\pi$.
  4. (4) The vertex is at $t = \dfrac{1}{2}$, so $t = -1$ is the end farthest from it: $y = -2 - 2 + 2 = -2$, at $\theta = \dfrac{3}{2}\pi$.

(1) $y = -2t^2 + 2t + 2$ (2) $-1 \le t \le 1$ (3) $\dfrac{5}{2}$ at $\theta = \dfrac{\pi}{6}, \dfrac{5}{6}\pi$ (4) $-2$ at $\theta = \dfrac{3}{2}\pi$
