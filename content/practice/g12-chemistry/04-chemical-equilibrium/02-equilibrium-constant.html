The Equilibrium Constant

Six problems on writing and calculating $K_c$, finding an unknown equilibrium concentration, ICE tables and comparing $Q$ with $K$. Try each one first. Open the hint if you are stuck, then compare with the full step-by-step solution.

Problem 1

For $\mathrm{H_2 + I_2 \rightleftharpoons 2HI}$ at equilibrium, $[\mathrm{H_2}] = [\mathrm{I_2}] = 0.10\ \mathrm{M}$ and $[\mathrm{HI}] = 0.74\ \mathrm{M}$. Calculate $K_c$. (Type $K_c$, to 3 significant figures.)

Uses: The Equilibrium Constant

Show hint

$K_c = \dfrac{[\mathrm{HI}]^2}{[\mathrm{H_2}][\mathrm{I_2}]}$.

Show solution
  1. $K_c = \dfrac{0.74^2}{0.10 \times 0.10} = \dfrac{0.5476}{0.010} \approx 54.8$.

$K_c \approx 54.8$

Problem 2

For $\mathrm{N_2 + 3H_2 \rightleftharpoons 2NH_3}$ the equilibrium concentrations are $[\mathrm{N_2}] = 0.50$, $[\mathrm{H_2}] = 0.20$ and $[\mathrm{NH_3}] = 0.10\ \mathrm{M}$. Calculate $K_c$. (Type $K_c$.)

Uses: The Equilibrium Constant

Show hint

Each concentration is raised to the power of its coefficient.

Show solution
  1. $K_c = \dfrac{[\mathrm{NH_3}]^2}{[\mathrm{N_2}][\mathrm{H_2}]^3} = \dfrac{0.10^2}{0.50 \times 0.20^3} = \dfrac{0.010}{0.50 \times 0.008} = 2.5$ (units $\mathrm{M^{-2}}$).

$K_c = 2.5$

Problem 3

For $\mathrm{H_2 + I_2 \rightleftharpoons 2HI}$, $K_c = 54.3$. At equilibrium $[\mathrm{H_2}] = [\mathrm{I_2}] = 0.020\ \mathrm{M}$. Find $[\mathrm{HI}]$. (Type it in M, to 3 significant figures.)

Uses: The Equilibrium Constant

Show hint

Rearrange: $[\mathrm{HI}]^2 = K_c[\mathrm{H_2}][\mathrm{I_2}]$.

Show solution
  1. $[\mathrm{HI}]^2 = 54.3 \times 0.020 \times 0.020 = 0.02172$.
  2. $[\mathrm{HI}] = \sqrt{0.02172} \approx 0.147\ \mathrm{M}$.

$[\mathrm{HI}] \approx 0.147\ \mathrm{M}$

Problem 4

$0.50\ \mathrm{mol}$ of $\mathrm{H_2}$ and $0.50\ \mathrm{mol}$ of $\mathrm{I_2}$ are heated in a $1.0\ \mathrm{dm^3}$ flask. At equilibrium there is $0.78\ \mathrm{mol}$ of HI. Find $K_c$. (Type $K_c$, to 3 significant figures.)

Uses: The Equilibrium Constant

Show hint

ICE table: forming $0.78$ mol HI uses $0.39$ mol of each reactant.

Show solution
  1. Change: $\mathrm{H_2}$ and $\mathrm{I_2}$ each $-0.39$; HI $+0.78$.
  2. Equilibrium: $[\mathrm{H_2}] = [\mathrm{I_2}] = 0.50 - 0.39 = 0.11\ \mathrm{M}$, $[\mathrm{HI}] = 0.78\ \mathrm{M}$.
  3. $K_c = \dfrac{0.78^2}{0.11^2} = \dfrac{0.6084}{0.0121} \approx 50.3$.

$K_c \approx 50.3$

Problem 5

For $\mathrm{H_2 + I_2 \rightleftharpoons 2HI}$ ($K_c = 54.3$) a mixture has $[\mathrm{HI}] = 0.50$ and $[\mathrm{H_2}] = [\mathrm{I_2}] = 0.10\ \mathrm{M}$. Is it at equilibrium? If not, which way will it go? (Type $1$ for a shift to the right, $-1$ for a shift to the left, $0$ for no shift.)

Uses: The Equilibrium Constant, Le Chatelier's Principle

Show hint

Work out the reaction quotient $Q$ the same way as $K_c$ and compare.

Show solution
  1. $Q = \dfrac{0.50^2}{0.10 \times 0.10} = 25$.
  2. $Q < K_c$: there is too little product, so the reaction goes forward (right) until $Q = 54.3$.

Not at equilibrium; it moves right ($1$)

Problem 6

$1.0\ \mathrm{mol}$ of ethanoic acid and $1.0\ \mathrm{mol}$ of ethanol are mixed: $\mathrm{CH_3COOH + C_2H_5OH \rightleftharpoons CH_3COOC_2H_5 + H_2O}$, $K_c = 4.0$. How many moles of ester are present at equilibrium? (Type the amount in mol.)

Uses: The Equilibrium Constant

Show hint

Let $x$ mol of ester form. The volume cancels because there are two molecules on each side.

Show solution
  1. Equilibrium: acid $= $ alcohol $= 1.0 - x$; ester $=$ water $= x$.
  2. $\dfrac{x^2}{(1.0 - x)^2} = 4.0 \implies \dfrac{x}{1.0 - x} = 2.0$.
  3. $x = 2.0 - 2.0x \implies x = \dfrac{2}{3} \approx 0.667\ \mathrm{mol}$.

$x = \dfrac23 \approx 0.667\ \mathrm{mol}$ of ester
