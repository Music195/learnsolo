Problem 1

Butan-2-ol is oxidised by acidified potassium dichromate. Name the product and find its molar mass. (Type $M$ in $\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$.)

Uses: Ethers, Aldehydes and Ketones

Show hint

A secondary alcohol gives a ketone.

Show solution
  1. $\mathrm{CH_3CH(OH)CH_2CH_3 + [O] \to CH_3COCH_2CH_3 + H_2O}$: butanone.
  2. $\mathrm{C_4H_8O}$: $48 + 8 + 16 = 72\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$. The orange dichromate turns green.

Butanone, $M = 72\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$

Problem 2

A compound $\mathrm{C_3H_6O_2}$ gives an IR spectrum with a very broad band from $2500$ to $3000\ \mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ and a strong band at $1710\ \mathrm{cm^{-1}}$. It fizzes with sodium carbonate. Identify it and find its molar mass. (Type $M$ in $\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$.)

Uses: Identifying Functional Groups: Tests and IR Spectra, Carboxylic Acids and Esters

Show hint

Broad O–H plus C=O, and an acid reaction.

Show solution
  1. Broad O–H ($2500$–$3000$) and C=O ($1710$) together mean a carboxylic acid; fizzing with $\mathrm{Na_2CO_3}$ confirms it.
  2. $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2COOH}$, propanoic acid: $M = 36 + 6 + 32 = 74\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$.

Propanoic acid, $M = 74\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$

Problem 3

$8.8\ \mathrm{g}$ of ethyl ethanoate is boiled with excess sodium hydroxide solution. Write the equation and find the mass of sodium ethanoate formed. ($M(\mathrm{CH_3COONa}) = 82$; type the mass in g.)

Uses: Carboxylic Acids and Esters

Show hint

Alkaline hydrolysis (saponification) goes to completion.

Show solution
  1. $\mathrm{CH_3COOC_2H_5 + NaOH \to CH_3COONa + C_2H_5OH}$.
  2. $n = \dfrac{8.8}{88} = 0.10\ \mathrm{mol}$, so $0.10 \times 82 = 8.2\ \mathrm{g}$ of sodium ethanoate.

$8.2\ \mathrm{g}$

Problem 4

Describe a chemical test that tells ethanal from propanone, and say what is seen with each.

Uses: Identifying Functional Groups: Tests and IR Spectra, Ethers, Aldehydes and Ketones

Show hint

Aldehydes are easily oxidised; ketones are not.

Show solution
  1. Warm each with Tollens' reagent (ammoniacal silver nitrate): ethanal gives a silver mirror, propanone shows no change.
  2. Or warm with Fehling's solution: ethanal turns the blue solution to a brick-red precipitate; propanone stays blue.

Tollens' silver mirror (or Fehling's red precipitate) with ethanal only

Problem 5

A polyester is made from ethane-1,2-diol ($M = 62$) and benzene-1,4-dicarboxylic acid ($M = 166$). Each repeat unit contains one of each monomer joined by ester links. What is the mass of one repeat unit? (Type it in $\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$.)

Uses: Polymers and Plastics

Show hint

Each repeat unit has two ester links, and each link releases one water molecule.

Show solution
  1. Mass $= 62 + 166 - 2 \times 18 = 228 - 36 = 192\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$.
  2. This polyester (PET) is used for bottles and fibres.

$192\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$

Problem 6

A nylon chain is made from $500$ molecules of a diamine and $500$ molecules of a dicarboxylic acid, joined alternately in one long line. How many water molecules are released? Explain the difference between addition and condensation polymerisation. (Type the number.)

Uses: Polymers and Plastics, Amines and Amides

Show hint

$1000$ monomers in a line are joined by one fewer link.

Show solution
  1. $1000 - 1 = 999$ amide links, each releasing one water molecule: $999$ water molecules.
  2. Addition polymers (e.g. poly(ethene)) form from C=C monomers with nothing lost; condensation polymers (nylon, polyesters) form from monomers with two reactive groups, losing a small molecule such as water at each link.

$999$ water molecules
