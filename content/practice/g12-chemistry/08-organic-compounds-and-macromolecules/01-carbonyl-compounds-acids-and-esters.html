Carbonyl Compounds, Carboxylic Acids and Esters

Six problems on oxidising alcohols to aldehydes and ketones, reactions of carboxylic acids, making esters and $M(\mathrm{C}_n\mathrm{H}_{2n}\mathrm{O}_2) = 14n + 32$. ($\mathrm{C} = 12$, $\mathrm{H} = 1$, $\mathrm{O} = 16$, $\mathrm{Mg} = 24$; one mole of gas $= 24\ \mathrm{dm^3}$.) Try each one first. Open the hint if you are stuck, then compare with the full step-by-step solution.

Problem 1

Propan-1-ol is warmed with acidified potassium dichromate and the product is distilled off at once. Name the product and find its molar mass. (Type $M$ in $\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$.)

Uses: Ethers, Aldehydes and Ketones

Show hint

A primary alcohol is oxidised first to an aldehyde; distilling it off stops further oxidation.

Show solution
  1. $\mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH_2OH + [O] \to CH_3CH_2CHO + H_2O}$: propanal.
  2. $\mathrm{C_3H_6O}$: $3(12) + 6(1) + 16 = 58\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$.

Propanal, $M = 58\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$

Problem 2

Ethanoic acid and ethanol react with a little concentrated sulphuric acid. Name the ester formed and find its molar mass. (Type $M$ in $\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$.)

Uses: Carboxylic Acids and Esters

Show hint

The ester $\mathrm{CH_3COOC_2H_5}$ is $\mathrm{C_4H_8O_2}$; use $M = 14n + 32$.

Show solution
  1. $\mathrm{CH_3COOH + C_2H_5OH \rightleftharpoons CH_3COOC_2H_5 + H_2O}$: ethyl ethanoate.
  2. $n = 4$: $M = 14(4) + 32 = 88\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$.

Ethyl ethanoate, $M = 88\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$

Problem 3

Name the ester formed from ethanoic acid and butan-1-ol, write its structural formula and find its molar mass. (Type $M$ in $\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$.)

Uses: Carboxylic Acids and Esters

Show hint

The acid gives the "-oate" part and the alcohol gives the "alkyl" part.

Show solution
  1. Butyl ethanoate, $\mathrm{CH_3COOCH_2CH_2CH_2CH_3}$.
  2. Formula $\mathrm{C_6H_{12}O_2}$, so $M = 14(6) + 32 = 116\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$. (It smells of pear drops.)

Butyl ethanoate, $M = 116\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$

Problem 4

$6.0\ \mathrm{g}$ of ethanoic acid is heated with excess ethanol. The yield of ethyl ethanoate is $60\,\%$. What mass of ester is obtained? (Type the mass in g, to 3 significant figures.)

Uses: Carboxylic Acids and Esters

Show hint

$M(\mathrm{CH_3COOH}) = 60$; one mole of acid gives at most one mole of ester. The reaction is reversible, so the yield is below $100\,\%$.

Show solution
  1. $n(\text{acid}) = \dfrac{6.0}{60} = 0.10\ \mathrm{mol}$, so at most $0.10 \times 88 = 8.8\ \mathrm{g}$ of ester.
  2. At $60\,\%$ yield: $0.60 \times 8.8 = 5.28\ \mathrm{g}$.

$5.28\ \mathrm{g}$ of ester

Problem 5

$0.24\ \mathrm{g}$ of magnesium reacts completely with excess ethanoic acid: $\mathrm{2CH_3COOH + Mg \to (CH_3COO)_2Mg + H_2}$. What volume of hydrogen is formed at room temperature and pressure? (Type the volume in $\mathrm{cm^3}$.)

Uses: Carboxylic Acids and Esters

Show hint

$1$ mol Mg gives $1$ mol $\mathrm{H_2}$; $24\ \mathrm{dm^3} = 24\,000\ \mathrm{cm^3}$.

Show solution
  1. $n(\mathrm{Mg}) = \dfrac{0.24}{24} = 0.010\ \mathrm{mol}$, so $0.010$ mol $\mathrm{H_2}$.
  2. $V = 0.010 \times 24\,000 = 240\ \mathrm{cm^3}$. The fizzing is slower than with HCl because ethanoic acid is weak.

$240\ \mathrm{cm^3}$ of hydrogen

Problem 6

An ester has molar mass $130\ \mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$ and formula $\mathrm{C}_n\mathrm{H}_{2n}\mathrm{O}_2$. Find $n$. On hydrolysis it gives ethanoic acid. What alcohol was used to make it? (Type $n$.)

Uses: Carboxylic Acids and Esters

Show hint

Solve $14n + 32 = 130$. Ethanoic acid supplies two of the carbons.

Show solution
  1. $14n = 98 \implies n = 7$: $\mathrm{C_7H_{14}O_2}$.
  2. Ethanoate ($\mathrm{CH_3COO{-}}$) has $2$ carbons, so the alkyl group has $5$: the alcohol is pentan-1-ol, and the ester is pentyl ethanoate (banana smell).

$n = 7$; pentyl ethanoate from pentan-1-ol
