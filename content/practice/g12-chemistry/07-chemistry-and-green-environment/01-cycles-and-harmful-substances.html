Biogeochemical Cycles and Harmful Substances

Six problems on the carbon and sulphur cycles, masses of $\mathrm{CO_2}$ and $\mathrm{SO_2}$ from burning fuels, acid rain, and biomagnification $c_{\text{next}} \approx F \times c$. ($\mathrm{C} = 12$, $\mathrm{O} = 16$, $\mathrm{S} = 32$, $\mathrm{Ca} = 40$, $\mathrm{H} = 1$.) Try each one first. Open the hint if you are stuck, then compare with the full step-by-step solution.

Problem 1

In photosynthesis, $\mathrm{6CO_2 + 6H_2O \to C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6O_2}$. What mass of $\mathrm{CO_2}$ is taken from the air to make $180\ \mathrm{g}$ of glucose? (Type the mass in g.)

Uses: Biogeochemical Cycles and a Green Environment

Show hint

$180\ \mathrm{g}$ of glucose is $1$ mol; $M(\mathrm{CO_2}) = 44$.

Show solution
  1. $1$ mol glucose needs $6$ mol $\mathrm{CO_2}$.
  2. Mass $= 6 \times 44 = 264\ \mathrm{g}$.

$264\ \mathrm{g}$ of $\mathrm{CO_2}$

Problem 2

DDT in a lake is $0.001\ \mathrm{ppm}$. Its concentration rises about $10$ times at each step of the food chain: plankton, small fish, large fish, fish-eating bird. What is the concentration in the bird? (Type it in ppm.)

Uses: Harmful Substances in the Environment

Show hint

Four steps from the water to the bird.

Show solution
  1. $0.001 \times 10^4 = 10\ \mathrm{ppm}$, ten thousand times the level in the water. DDT is fat-soluble and not broken down, so it builds up.

About $10\ \mathrm{ppm}$

Problem 3

What mass of $\mathrm{CO_2}$ is released when $1.0\ \mathrm{kg}$ of pure carbon (coal) burns completely? (Type the mass in kg, to 3 significant figures.)

Uses: Biogeochemical Cycles and a Green Environment

Show hint

$\mathrm{C + O_2 \to CO_2}$: $12\ \mathrm{g}$ of C gives $44\ \mathrm{g}$ of $\mathrm{CO_2}$.

Show solution
  1. Mass of $\mathrm{CO_2} = 1.0 \times \dfrac{44}{12} \approx 3.67\ \mathrm{kg}$: more than three times the mass of the fuel, because oxygen from the air is added.

About $3.67\ \mathrm{kg}$

Problem 4

A power station burns $1000\ \mathrm{kg}$ of coal containing $2.0\,\%$ sulphur by mass. What mass of $\mathrm{SO_2}$ is formed? Write the equations that turn it into acid rain. (Type the mass in kg.)

Uses: Biogeochemical Cycles and a Green Environment, Harmful Substances in the Environment

Show hint

$\mathrm{S + O_2 \to SO_2}$: $32\ \mathrm{g}$ of S gives $64\ \mathrm{g}$ of $\mathrm{SO_2}$.

Show solution
  1. Sulphur $= 0.020 \times 1000 = 20\ \mathrm{kg}$, so $\mathrm{SO_2} = 20 \times \dfrac{64}{32} = 40\ \mathrm{kg}$.
  2. $\mathrm{2SO_2 + O_2 \to 2SO_3}$, then $\mathrm{SO_3 + H_2O \to H_2SO_4}$: sulphuric acid in rain.

$40\ \mathrm{kg}$ of $\mathrm{SO_2}$

Problem 5

Methylmercury is concentrated about $8$ times at each step of a food chain. Water contains $0.0020\ \mathrm{ppm}$. What is the concentration after three steps (plankton, small fish, tuna)? (Type it in ppm, to 3 significant figures.)

Uses: Harmful Substances in the Environment

Show hint

Multiply by $8$ three times.

Show solution
  1. $0.0020 \times 8^3 = 0.0020 \times 512 \approx 1.02\ \mathrm{ppm}$, about $500$ times the water level, which is why large predatory fish carry mercury warnings.

About 1.02 ppm

Problem 6

The $40\ \mathrm{kg}$ of $\mathrm{SO_2}$ from the last problem is removed by a limestone scrubber: $\mathrm{CaCO_3 + SO_2 + \tfrac12 O_2 \to CaSO_4 + CO_2}$. What mass of limestone is needed? What is the drawback of this method? (Type the mass in kg, to 3 significant figures.)

Uses: Harmful Substances in the Environment, Biogeochemical Cycles and a Green Environment

Show hint

$1$ mol $\mathrm{CaCO_3}$ ($100$ g) removes $1$ mol $\mathrm{SO_2}$ ($64$ g).

Show solution
  1. Mass $= 40 \times \dfrac{100}{64} = 62.5\ \mathrm{kg}$.
  2. It stops acid rain, but releases $\mathrm{CO_2}$ (a greenhouse gas) and needs limestone to be quarried.

$62.5\ \mathrm{kg}$ of $\mathrm{CaCO_3}$
