Chemistry and Green Environment

Topic review: a mix of the whole chapter, best attempted after completing the concept sets. Six exam-style problems on cycles, pollutants, radioactive substances and green chemistry. Try each one first. Open the hint if you are stuck, then compare with the full step-by-step solution.

Problem 1

When plants take in $264\ \mathrm{g}$ of $\mathrm{CO_2}$ in photosynthesis, what mass of oxygen do they release? ($\mathrm{6CO_2 + 6H_2O \to C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6O_2}$; type the mass in g.)

Uses: Biogeochemical Cycles and a Green Environment

Show hint

Moles of $\mathrm{CO_2}$ = moles of $\mathrm{O_2}$.

Show solution
  1. $n(\mathrm{CO_2}) = \dfrac{264}{44} = 6.0\ \mathrm{mol}$, so $6.0$ mol $\mathrm{O_2}$.
  2. Mass $= 6.0 \times 32 = 192\ \mathrm{g}$.

$192\ \mathrm{g}$ of $\mathrm{O_2}$

Problem 2

A fish-eating bird contains $5.0\ \mathrm{ppm}$ of DDT. The concentration rises $10$ times at each of $4$ steps from the water. What was the concentration in the water? (Type it in units of $10^{-4}$ ppm.)

Uses: Harmful Substances in the Environment

Show hint

Work backwards: divide by $10^4$.

Show solution
  1. $\dfrac{5.0}{10^4} = 5.0 \times 10^{-4}\ \mathrm{ppm}$: far too little to harm the plankton, but enough to thin the bird's eggshells after biomagnification.

$5.0 \times 10^{-4}\ \mathrm{ppm}$

Problem 3

Strontium-90, $^{90}_{38}\mathrm{Sr}$, from nuclear fallout is a beta-minus emitter. What is the atomic number of its product? Why is it dangerous in milk? (Type $Z$.)

Uses: Radioactive Substances

Show hint

In $\beta^-$ decay, $Z$ rises by one and $A$ is unchanged.

Show solution
  1. $^{90}_{38}\mathrm{Sr} \to {}^{90}_{39}\mathrm{Y} + {}^{\ \ 0}_{-1}e$: $Z = 39$ (yttrium).
  2. Strontium behaves like calcium, so it passes into milk and is stored in bones, where its radiation damages the bone marrow for years (half-life about $29$ years).

$Z = 39$ (yttrium-90)

Problem 4

State three principles of green chemistry and give an example of each.

Uses: Green Chemistry in Action

Show hint

Think about waste, raw materials and energy.

Show solution
  1. Prevent waste (high atom economy), e.g. catalytic processes that make fewer by-products.
  2. Use renewable raw materials, e.g. bioethanol from sugar cane instead of fuel from crude oil.
  3. Use safer solvents and less energy, e.g. water or supercritical $\mathrm{CO_2}$ instead of chlorinated solvents, and catalysts that work at low temperature.

Less waste, renewable feedstocks, safer solvents and lower energy use

Problem 5

Iodine-131, used to treat thyroid disease, has a half-life of $8.0$ days. How long until a patient's dose falls to $12.5\,\%$ of its starting activity? (Type the time in days.)

Uses: Radioactive Substances

Show hint

$12.5\,\% = \tfrac18$.

Show solution
  1. $\tfrac18 = \left(\tfrac12\right)^3$: three half-lives.
  2. Time $= 3 \times 8.0 = 24$ days.

$24$ days

Problem 6

Bioethanol burns as $\mathrm{C_2H_5OH + 3O_2 \to 2CO_2 + 3H_2O}$. What mass of $\mathrm{CO_2}$ comes from burning $1.0\ \mathrm{kg}$ of ethanol? Explain why bioethanol is still described as nearly carbon neutral. (Type the mass in g, to the nearest gram.)

Uses: Green Chemistry in Action, Biogeochemical Cycles and a Green Environment

Show hint

$46\ \mathrm{g}$ of ethanol gives $2 \times 44 = 88\ \mathrm{g}$ of $\mathrm{CO_2}$.

Show solution
  1. $n = \dfrac{1000}{46} \approx 21.74\ \mathrm{mol}$, giving $43.48$ mol $\mathrm{CO_2}$.
  2. Mass $\approx 43.48 \times 44 \approx 1913\ \mathrm{g}$.
  3. The same carbon was taken from the air by the sugar cane as it grew, so the overall cycle adds little $\mathrm{CO_2}$; only fuel for farming and processing adds extra.

About $1913\ \mathrm{g}$ of $\mathrm{CO_2}$
