pH, pOH and the Strengths of Acids and Bases

Six problems on $\mathrm{pH} = -\log[\mathrm{H^+}]$, $\mathrm{pH} + \mathrm{pOH} = 14$, strong and weak acids and bases with $[\mathrm{H^+}] \approx \sqrt{K_a c}$, and conjugate pairs. Try each one first. Open the hint if you are stuck, then compare with the full step-by-step solution.

Problem 1

Find the pH of $0.010\ \mathrm{M}$ hydrochloric acid. (Type the pH.)

Uses: Ionic Product of Water, pH and pOH, Strengths of Acids and Bases: Ka and Kb

Show hint

HCl is a strong acid: it ionises completely, so $[\mathrm{H^+}] = 0.010\ \mathrm{M}$.

Show solution
  1. $\mathrm{pH} = -\log(0.010) = -\log(10^{-2}) = 2$.

$\mathrm{pH} = 2$

Problem 2

A solution has $[\mathrm{H^+}] = 1.0 \times 10^{-3}\ \mathrm{M}$ at $25^\circ\mathrm{C}$. Find $[\mathrm{OH^-}]$ and the pOH. (Type the pOH.)

Uses: Ionic Product of Water, pH and pOH

Show hint

$K_w = [\mathrm{H^+}][\mathrm{OH^-}] = 1.0 \times 10^{-14}$.

Show solution
  1. $[\mathrm{OH^-}] = \dfrac{1.0 \times 10^{-14}}{1.0 \times 10^{-3}} = 1.0 \times 10^{-11}\ \mathrm{M}$.
  2. $\mathrm{pOH} = 11$ (and $\mathrm{pH} = 3$; $3 + 11 = 14$ ✓).

$[\mathrm{OH^-}] = 10^{-11}\ \mathrm{M}$, $\mathrm{pOH} = 11$

Problem 3

Find the pH of $0.050\ \mathrm{M}$ sodium hydroxide at $25^\circ\mathrm{C}$. (Type the pH to 2 decimal places.)

Uses: Ionic Product of Water, pH and pOH, Strengths of Acids and Bases: Ka and Kb

Show hint

NaOH is a strong base: $[\mathrm{OH^-}] = 0.050\ \mathrm{M}$. Find pOH, then $\mathrm{pH} = 14 - \mathrm{pOH}$.

Show solution
  1. $\mathrm{pOH} = -\log(0.050) = 1.30$.
  2. $\mathrm{pH} = 14 - 1.30 = 12.70$.

$\mathrm{pH} = 12.70$

Problem 4

Find the pH of $0.10\ \mathrm{M}$ ethanoic acid ($K_a = 1.8 \times 10^{-5}$). Compare with $0.10\ \mathrm{M}$ HCl. (Type the pH to 2 decimal places.)

Uses: Strengths of Acids and Bases: Ka and Kb

Show hint

For a weak acid $[\mathrm{H^+}] \approx \sqrt{K_a c}$.

Show solution
  1. $[\mathrm{H^+}] \approx \sqrt{1.8 \times 10^{-5} \times 0.10} = \sqrt{1.8 \times 10^{-6}} \approx 1.34 \times 10^{-3}\ \mathrm{M}$.
  2. $\mathrm{pH} = -\log(1.34 \times 10^{-3}) \approx 2.87$.
  3. $0.10\ \mathrm{M}$ HCl has pH $1.00$: the weak acid has far fewer $\mathrm{H^+}$ ions (only about $1.3\,\%$ ionised).

$\mathrm{pH} \approx 2.87$ (HCl: $1.00$)

Problem 5

A $0.20\ \mathrm{M}$ solution of a weak acid HA has pH $3.00$. Find $K_a$ and the percentage of HA molecules that are ionised. (Type $K_a$ in units of $10^{-6}$.)

Uses: Strengths of Acids and Bases: Ka and Kb

Show hint

$[\mathrm{H^+}] = [\mathrm{A^-}] = 10^{-\mathrm{pH}}$; for a weak acid $[\mathrm{HA}] \approx c$.

Show solution
  1. $[\mathrm{H^+}] = [\mathrm{A^-}] = 1.0 \times 10^{-3}\ \mathrm{M}$.
  2. $K_a = \dfrac{(1.0 \times 10^{-3})^2}{0.20} = 5.0 \times 10^{-6}$.
  3. Ionised: $\dfrac{1.0 \times 10^{-3}}{0.20} \times 100\,\% = 0.50\,\%$.

$K_a = 5.0 \times 10^{-6}$; $0.50\,\%$ ionised

Problem 6

For $\mathrm{NH_3 + H_2O \rightleftharpoons NH_4^+ + OH^-}$, name the two conjugate acid–base pairs. Then find the pH of $0.10\ \mathrm{M}$ ammonia ($K_b = 1.8 \times 10^{-5}$) at $25^\circ\mathrm{C}$. (Type the pH to 2 decimal places.)

Uses: Theories of Acids and Bases, Strengths of Acids and Bases: Ka and Kb

Show hint

An acid gives $\mathrm{H^+}$; its conjugate base is what is left. For a weak base $[\mathrm{OH^-}] \approx \sqrt{K_b c}$.

Show solution
  1. Pairs: $\mathrm{NH_4^+}/\mathrm{NH_3}$ and $\mathrm{H_2O}/\mathrm{OH^-}$ (water acts as an acid here).
  2. $[\mathrm{OH^-}] \approx \sqrt{1.8 \times 10^{-5} \times 0.10} \approx 1.34 \times 10^{-3}\ \mathrm{M}$, so $\mathrm{pOH} \approx 2.87$.
  3. $\mathrm{pH} = 14 - 2.87 = 11.13$.

$\mathrm{pH} \approx 11.13$
