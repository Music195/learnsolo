Exponential Function Graph

Repeated multiplication as a curve

Now that $a^x$ makes sense for every $x$, $y = a^x$ ($a > 0$, $a \ne 1$) is a function: the exponential function. Each step of 1 to the right multiplies $y$ by $a$.

Every such graph passes through $(0, 1)$ and stays above the $x$-axis, getting closer and closer to it without touching (the $x$-axis is an asymptote).

$y = a^x$

$$\text{domain: all } x, \qquad \text{range: } y > 0, \qquad \text{passes } (0, 1) \text{ and } (1, a), \qquad \text{asymptote } y = 0$$

$a > 1$: rises (increasing). $0 < a < 1$: falls (decreasing). $y = \left(\dfrac{1}{a}\right)^x$ is the mirror image of $y = a^x$ in the $y$-axis.

Change the base $a$

Example 1

How are the graphs of $y = 2^x$ and $y = \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^x$ related?

Show solution
  1. $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^x = 2^{-x}$: replacing $x$ by $-x$ reflects in the $y$-axis.

Mirror images in the $y$-axis

Example 2

Describe the graph of $y = 2^{x - 1} + 3$: how is it moved from $y = 2^x$, and what is its asymptote?

Show solution
  1. $x \to x - 1$: 1 to the right. $+3$: 3 up.
  2. The asymptote $y = 0$ moves up to $y = 3$.

Moved 1 right, 3 up; asymptote $y = 3$; passes $(1, 4)$

Example 3

A culture of bacteria doubles every hour, starting from 300. Write the number after $t$ hours, and find it after 5 hours.

Show solution
  1. $N = 300 \cdot 2^t$.
  2. $N(5) = 300 \cdot 32$.

$N = 300 \cdot 2^t$; $\;9600$

$a^x$ is never 0 or negative. The graph gets close to the $x$-axis but never reaches it.
To sketch quickly, plot $(0, 1)$ and $(1, a)$, remember the asymptote, then decide rising or falling from whether $a > 1$.
  • $y = a^x$: through $(0, 1)$, always $y > 0$, asymptote $y = 0$.
  • $a > 1$ increasing; $0 < a < 1$ decreasing.
  • $y = a^{x - p} + q$: moved $p$ right, $q$ up; asymptote $y = q$.
