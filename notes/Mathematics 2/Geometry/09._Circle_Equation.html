Equation of a Circle

All points at distance $r$ from the centre

A circle is the set of points at a fixed distance $r$ from a centre $(a, b)$. Writing that distance with the distance formula and squaring gives its equation.

Expanded, it looks like $x^2 + y^2 + lx + my + n = 0$. Completing the square brings it back to centre-radius form, if it is a circle at all.

Centre $(a, b)$, radius $r$

$$(x - a)^2 + (y - b)^2 = r^2$$

Centre at the origin: $x^2 + y^2 = r^2$.

General form

$$x^2 + y^2 + lx + my + n = 0 \iff \left(x + \frac{l}{2}\right)^2 + \left(y + \frac{m}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{l^2 + m^2 - 4n}{4}$$

A circle only when $l^2 + m^2 - 4n > 0$; equal to $0$: a single point; negative: no points at all.

  1. $P(x, y)$ is on the circle $\iff$ $CP = r$, where $C(a, b)$.
  2. $\iff \sqrt{(x - a)^2 + (y - b)^2} = r \iff (x - a)^2 + (y - b)^2 = r^2$ (both sides positive, so squaring is safe).

$x^2 + y^2 + lx + my + n = 0$

Example 1

Find the centre and radius of $x^2 + y^2 - 4x + 6y - 3 = 0$.

Show solution
  1. $(x - 2)^2 - 4 + (y + 3)^2 - 9 - 3 = 0 \Rightarrow (x - 2)^2 + (y + 3)^2 = 16$.

Centre $(2, -3)$, radius $4$

Example 2

Find the circle with diameter $AB$, where $A(1, 2)$ and $B(5, -4)$.

Show solution
  1. Centre = midpoint $(3, -1)$; $r^2 = (3 - 1)^2 + (-1 - 2)^2 = 13$.

$(x - 3)^2 + (y + 1)^2 = 13$

Example 3

Find the circle through $O(0, 0)$, $A(4, 0)$ and $B(1, 3)$.

Show solution
  1. Use $x^2 + y^2 + lx + my + n = 0$. $O$: $n = 0$. $A$: $16 + 4l = 0$, $l = -4$.
  2. $B$: $1 + 9 - 4 + 3m = 0$, $m = -2$. So $x^2 + y^2 - 4x - 2y = 0$.

$(x - 2)^2 + (y - 1)^2 = 5$

$x^2 + y^2 + 2x - 4y + 6 = 0$ becomes $(x + 1)^2 + (y - 2)^2 = -1$: no point satisfies it, so it is not a circle. Always check the right side is positive.
For a circle through three given points, use the general form: three points give three equations in $l$, $m$, $n$.
  • $(x - a)^2 + (y - b)^2 = r^2$: centre $(a, b)$, radius $r$.
  • General form → complete the square.
  • Circle only if the right side comes out positive.
