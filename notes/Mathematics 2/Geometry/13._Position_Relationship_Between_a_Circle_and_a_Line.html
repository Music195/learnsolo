Circle and Line: Distance Method

Compare the distance $d$ with the radius $r$

Let $d$ be the distance from the centre of the circle to the line (note 08). If the line is nearer than $r$, it cuts the circle; at exactly $r$ it just touches; further away it misses.

Centre $C$, radius $r$, $d$ = distance from $C$ to the line

$$d < r: \text{ 2 points}, \qquad d = r: \text{ tangent}, \qquad d > r: \text{ none}$$
$$\text{chord length } = 2\sqrt{r^2 - d^2} \quad (d < r)$$
  1. Let the line cut the circle at $A$, $B$, and let $H$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $C$. Then $CH = d$, $CA = r$.
  2. $CA = CB$, so $H$ is the midpoint of $AB$; in right triangle $CHA$, $AH = \sqrt{r^2 - d^2}$.
  3. $AB = 2AH = 2\sqrt{r^2 - d^2}$.

Drag the centre $C$; line $y = mx + n$

Example 1

How many common points do $(x - 2)^2 + (y + 1)^2 = 16$ and $x - y + 3 = 0$ have?

Show solution
  1. Centre $(2, -1)$, $r = 4$. $d = \dfrac{|2 + 1 + 3|}{\sqrt{2}} = 3\sqrt{2} \approx 4.24$.
  2. $d > r$ (compare squares: $18 > 16$).

None

Example 2

Find $k$ so that $y = kx + 2$ touches $x^2 + y^2 = 1$.

Show solution
  1. Line: $kx - y + 2 = 0$. $d = \dfrac{2}{\sqrt{k^2 + 1}} = 1$.
  2. $k^2 + 1 = 4$.

$k = \pm\sqrt{3}$

Example 3

Find the length of the chord cut from $x^2 + y^2 = 10$ by $x - y + 2 = 0$.

Show solution
  1. $d = \dfrac{|2|}{\sqrt{2}} = \sqrt{2}$.
  2. $2\sqrt{10 - 2} = 2\sqrt{8}$.

$4\sqrt{2}$

For $d$ the line must be in the form $ax + by + c = 0$: $y = kx + 2$ becomes $kx - y + 2 = 0$.
To compare $d$ and $r$, compare $d^2$ and $r^2$; the square roots disappear.
  • $d < r$: 2 points; $d = r$: tangent; $d > r$: none.
  • Chord $= 2\sqrt{r^2 - d^2}$.
  • Tangent condition: $d = r$.
