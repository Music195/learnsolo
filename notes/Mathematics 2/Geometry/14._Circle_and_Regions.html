Circle and Regions

Inside or outside the circle

$(x - a)^2 + (y - b)^2$ is the squared distance from $(x, y)$ to the centre. So "$< r^2$" means closer than $r$: inside the circle; "$> r^2$" means outside.

To find the largest value of an expression like $2x + y$ on a region, set $2x + y = k$. That is a line; slide it until it last touches the region.

Regions bounded by a circle

$$(x - a)^2 + (y - b)^2 < r^2: \text{ inside}, \qquad (x - a)^2 + (y - b)^2 > r^2: \text{ outside}$$

$\le$ and $\ge$ include the circle itself.

  1. $2x + y$ takes the value $k$ at some point of the region $\iff$ the line $2x + y = k$ meets the region.
  2. As $k$ grows, the line moves parallel to itself. The largest $k$ is where it last touches the region: a corner of a polygon, or a tangent point of a circle.

Region $x^2 + y^2 \le 5$; line $2x + y = k$

Example 2

Is $(0, 0)$ inside $(x + 1)^2 + (y - 1)^2 > 9$?

Show solution
  1. $(0 + 1)^2 + (0 - 1)^2 = 2$, and $2 > 9$ is false.
  2. So $(0, 0)$ is not in the region: it is inside the circle, and the region is the outside.

Example 3

On the region $x \ge 0$, $y \ge 0$, $x + 2y \le 8$, $3x + y \le 9$, find the largest value of $x + y$.

Show solution
  1. Corners: $(0, 0)$, $(3, 0)$, $(0, 4)$, and $x + 2y = 8$ with $3x + y = 9$: $(2, 3)$.
  2. The line $x + y = k$ (slope $-1$) last touches the region at a corner: values $0, 3, 4, 5$.

Largest value $5$, at $(2, 3)$

Example 4

Find the largest and smallest values of $x + y$ on $x^2 + y^2 \le 2$.

Show solution
  1. The extreme lines $x + y = k$ are tangent: distance from $O$ = radius, $\dfrac{|k|}{\sqrt{2}} = \sqrt{2}$.
  2. $k = \pm 2$, at the tangent points $(1, 1)$ and $(-1, -1)$.

Largest $2$, smallest $-2$

A corner only counts if it satisfies every inequality. Intersection points of boundary lines that fall outside the region must be ignored.
For a polygon region, compare the slope of $k$-line with the slopes of the edges; the best corner is where the edge slopes "change over".
  • $< r^2$ inside, $> r^2$ outside.
  • Max/min of $ax + by$: set $= k$ and slide the line.
  • Extremes at corners (polygons) or tangent points (circles).
