Dot Product of Vectors

A number that measures how much two vectors agree

The dot product (内積) $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = |\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\cos\theta$ is positive when the angle between the vectors is acute, $0$ at a right angle, and negative when the angle is obtuse.

In components it is simply "multiply matching components and add". Putting the two formulas together gives the angle between any two space vectors.

$\vec{a} = (a_1, a_2, a_3)$, $\vec{b} = (b_1, b_2, b_3)$, angle $\theta$ ($0 \le \theta \le \pi$)

$$\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = |\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\cos\theta = a_1b_1 + a_2b_2 + a_3b_3$$
$$\cos\theta = \frac{a_1b_1 + a_2b_2 + a_3b_3}{\sqrt{a_1^2 + a_2^2 + a_3^2}\,\sqrt{b_1^2 + b_2^2 + b_3^2}}$$

Rules: $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{a} = |\vec{a}|^2$, $\;\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = \vec{b} \cdot \vec{a}$, $\;\vec{a} \cdot (\vec{b} + \vec{c}) = \vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} + \vec{a} \cdot \vec{c}$, $\;(k\vec{a}) \cdot \vec{b} = k(\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b})$.

  1. In triangle OAB with $\overrightarrow{OA} = \vec{a}$, $\overrightarrow{OB} = \vec{b}$, the cosine rule gives $|\vec{b} - \vec{a}|^2 = |\vec{a}|^2 + |\vec{b}|^2 - 2|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\cos\theta$.
  2. In components the left side is $\sum (b_k - a_k)^2 = \sum a_k^2 + \sum b_k^2 - 2\sum a_kb_k$.
  3. Comparing: $|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\cos\theta = a_1b_1 + a_2b_2 + a_3b_3$.

$\vec{a} = (2, 1, 2)$ (blue) and $\vec{b}$ (orange): the shadow of $\vec{b}$ on $\vec{a}$

Example 1

Find the angle between $\vec{a} = (1, 0, 1)$ and $\vec{b} = (1, 1, 0)$.

Show solution
  1. $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = 1$, $|\vec{a}| = |\vec{b}| = \sqrt{2}$, so $\cos\theta = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

$\theta = 60°$

Example 2

Find the angle between $\vec{a} = (1, -1, 0)$ and $\vec{b} = (0, 1, -1)$.

Show solution
  1. $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = 0 - 1 + 0 = -1$, $|\vec{a}| = |\vec{b}| = \sqrt{2}$, so $\cos\theta = -\dfrac{1}{2}$.

$\theta = 120°$

Example 3

$|\vec{a}| = 2$, $|\vec{b}| = 3$ and the angle between them is $60°$. Find $|\vec{a} + \vec{b}|$.

Show solution
  1. $|\vec{a} + \vec{b}|^2 = |\vec{a}|^2 + 2\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} + |\vec{b}|^2 = 4 + 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} + 9 = 19$.

$\sqrt{19}$

Example 4

Find the area of the triangle with vertices A$(1, 0, 0)$, B$(0, 2, 0)$, C$(0, 0, 2)$.

Show solution
  1. $\overrightarrow{AB} = (-1, 2, 0)$, $\overrightarrow{AC} = (-1, 0, 2)$: $|\overrightarrow{AB}|^2 = |\overrightarrow{AC}|^2 = 5$, $\overrightarrow{AB} \cdot \overrightarrow{AC} = 1$.
  2. Area $= \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{|\overrightarrow{AB}|^2|\overrightarrow{AC}|^2 - (\overrightarrow{AB} \cdot \overrightarrow{AC})^2} = \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{25 - 1}$.

$\sqrt{6}$

$\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}$ is a number, not a vector. So $(\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b})\vec{c}$ makes sense, but "$\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} \cdot \vec{c}$" doesn't. And $|\vec{a} + \vec{b}|^2$ needs the middle term $2\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}$; it is not $|\vec{a}|^2 + |\vec{b}|^2$.
To find a length like $|\vec{a} + \vec{b}|$ from given lengths and angles, square it, expand using the dot-product rules, then take the root.
  • $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = |\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\cos\theta = a_1b_1 + a_2b_2 + a_3b_3$.
  • $\cos\theta = \dfrac{\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}}{|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|}$.
  • Triangle area $= \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{|\vec{a}|^2|\vec{b}|^2 - (\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b})^2}$.
