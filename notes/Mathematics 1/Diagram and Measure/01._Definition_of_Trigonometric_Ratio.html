Definition of Trigonometric Ratios

Same angle, same ratios

All right triangles with the same acute angle $\theta$ are similar, so the ratios of their sides are the same however big the triangle is. These ratios get names: sine, cosine and tangent.

Name the sides from $\theta$: the hypotenuse faces the right angle, the opposite side faces $\theta$, and the adjacent side touches $\theta$.

Trigonometric ratios

$$\sin\theta = \frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}}, \qquad \cos\theta = \frac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{hypotenuse}}, \qquad \tan\theta = \frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}}$$

Since the hypotenuse is the longest side, $0 < \sin\theta < 1$ and $0 < \cos\theta < 1$ for acute $\theta$. $\tan\theta$ can be any positive number.

Values to know by heart

$\theta$$30°$$45°$$60°$
$\sin\theta$$\dfrac{1}{2}$$\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$$\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
$\cos\theta$$\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$$\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$$\dfrac{1}{2}$
$\tan\theta$$\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$$1$$\sqrt{3}$

They come from the triangles with sides $1 : \sqrt{3} : 2$ (30°–60°–90°) and $1 : 1 : \sqrt{2}$ (45°–45°–90°).

Drag $B$ to change the triangle

Make the triangle bigger without changing the angle: the ratios stay the same.

Example 1

A right triangle has opposite side 5 and hypotenuse 13 for the angle $\theta$. Find $\sin\theta$, $\cos\theta$, $\tan\theta$.

Show solution
  1. Adjacent $= \sqrt{13^2 - 5^2} = 12$

$\sin\theta = \dfrac{5}{13}$, $\cos\theta = \dfrac{12}{13}$, $\tan\theta = \dfrac{5}{12}$

Example 2

From a point 20 m from the foot of a tree, the top of the tree is seen at an angle of $30°$ above the horizontal. How tall is the tree?

Show solution
  1. Height is opposite the $30°$ angle, the 20 m is adjacent: $\tan 30° = \dfrac{h}{20}$
  2. $h = 20 \times \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \dfrac{20\sqrt{3}}{3}$

$\dfrac{20\sqrt{3}}{3} \approx 11.5$ m

Example 3

In right triangle $ABC$ with $\angle C = 90°$, $AB = 10$ and $\angle A = 60°$. Find $BC$ and $AC$.

Show solution
  1. $BC$ is opposite $A$: $BC = 10 \sin 60° = 5\sqrt{3}$
  2. $AC$ is adjacent to $A$: $AC = 10 \cos 60° = 5$

$BC = 5\sqrt{3}$, $AC = 5$

Rearranged: opposite $= \text{hypotenuse} \times \sin\theta$, adjacent $= \text{hypotenuse} \times \cos\theta$, opposite $= \text{adjacent} \times \tan\theta$.
"Opposite" and "adjacent" depend on which angle you use. The side opposite $A$ is adjacent to $B$.
  • $\sin = \dfrac{\text{opp}}{\text{hyp}}$, $\cos = \dfrac{\text{adj}}{\text{hyp}}$, $\tan = \dfrac{\text{opp}}{\text{adj}}$.
  • The ratios depend only on the angle, not the size of the triangle.
  • Learn the $30°$, $45°$, $60°$ values from the two special triangles.
