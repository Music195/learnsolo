Trigonometric Ratios of 180°−θ

Mirror image in the $y$-axis

On the unit semicircle, the point for $180° - \theta$ is the mirror image of the point for $\theta$ in the $y$-axis: $(x, y)$ becomes $(-x, y)$. The height stays the same and the $x$-coordinate changes sign.

So every obtuse angle can be turned into an acute one.

Ratios of $180° - \theta$

$$\sin(180° - \theta) = \sin\theta, \qquad \cos(180° - \theta) = -\cos\theta, \qquad \tan(180° - \theta) = -\tan\theta$$

The relations $\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$ etc. (note 02) still hold for obtuse angles.

Drag $P$ (angle $\theta$); $Q$ is at $180° - \theta$

Example 1

Find $\sin 150°$, $\cos 135°$ and $\tan 120°$.

Show solution
  1. $\sin 150° = \sin 30° = \dfrac{1}{2}$
  2. $\cos 135° = -\cos 45° = -\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$
  3. $\tan 120° = -\tan 60° = -\sqrt{3}$

$\dfrac{1}{2}$, $\;-\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$, $\;-\sqrt{3}$

Example 2

$\theta$ is obtuse and $\sin\theta = \dfrac{2}{3}$. Find $\cos\theta$ and $\tan\theta$.

Show solution
  1. $\cos^2\theta = 1 - \dfrac{4}{9} = \dfrac{5}{9}$. Obtuse, so $\cos\theta < 0$: $\cos\theta = -\dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{3}$.
  2. $\tan\theta = \dfrac{2/3}{-\sqrt{5}/3} = -\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{5}}$

$\cos\theta = -\dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{3}$, $\tan\theta = -\dfrac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}$

Example 3

Find $\cos 10° + \cos 50° + \cos 130° + \cos 170°$.

Show solution
  1. $\cos 170° = -\cos 10°$ and $\cos 130° = -\cos 50°$.
  2. The terms cancel in pairs.

$0$

Only sine keeps its sign. Cosine and tangent flip. A common slip is writing $\sin 150° = -\dfrac{1}{2}$.
  • $\sin(180° - \theta) = \sin\theta$.
  • $\cos(180° - \theta) = -\cos\theta$, $\tan(180° - \theta) = -\tan\theta$.
  • Reason: reflecting in the $y$-axis keeps $y$ and flips $x$.
